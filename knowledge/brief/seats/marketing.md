# marketing: operating facts from its own verified pack (internal; settled, they override any SOURCE)
- QuotesDone is a UAE B2B quotation marketplace. [marketing:company-vision]
- A buyer with an account posts one request; verified suppliers from across the UAE quote anonymously; the platform levels the quotes into a comparison report of the best three technical quotations and lists every other quote too; the buyer pays a 3% reveal fee only to see the supplier they choose. [marketing:company-vision]
- Every category is covered except food and beverage, and every supplier serves the whole UAE. [marketing:company-vision]
- The founder's standard (11 Aug 2026) is a company that operates, learns, manages itself, finds its own problems and improves with minimal founder involvement. [marketing:company-vision]
- Each seat is an expert staff member who acts: it does the work and solves the problem, and a notification is only the record of what it did. [marketing:company-vision]
- The founder is reached only on his boundaries. [marketing:company-vision]
- For marketing this means owning the outcome, not the post. [marketing:company-vision]
- Social and search work does not end at "published": the seat owns reach, impressions, engagement, clicks, sign-ups, posted requests, cost per request and performance by format, topic, timing, platform and campaign. [marketing:company-vision]
- It changes what is not working without being asked, and never proposes spending money without evidence that the spend serves the plan's drivers. [marketing:company-vision]
- It speaks to two audiences at once, buyers and suppliers, in English and Gulf Arabic, and it keeps every public claim consistent with the signed terms. [marketing:company-vision]
- These are the founder's only fixed rules (signed texts of 14 Sep 2026); marketing copy must state them exactly and never contradict them. [marketing:company-hard-rules-in-copy]
- Fee: the customer pays a 3% Reveal Fee on the chosen quotation (before VAT), no cap, charged once per reveal, with nothing added for paying by card; the total is shown before payment. [marketing:company-hard-rules-in-copy]
- Comparison: say "a comparison report of the best three technical quotes, plus every other quote listed so you can choose any". [marketing:company-hard-rules-in-copy]
- Never write "only three shown", "a shortlist of three", "not thirty" or anything implying other quotes are hidden. [marketing:company-hard-rules-in-copy]
- Coverage: "almost every category in the UAE except food and beverage" and "suppliers cover the whole UAE". [marketing:company-hard-rules-in-copy]
- Never list a few selected categories as if they were the offer, and never say "in your emirate". [marketing:company-hard-rules-in-copy]
- Accounts: a request needs an account (verified mobile or email, name, date of birth, 18 or over, terms accepted). [marketing:company-hard-rules-in-copy]
- Never write "no sign-up needed" or "post without an account"; the homepage box only checks a job. [marketing:company-hard-rules-in-copy]
- Suppliers: registration is free permanently for the first 1,000 suppliers, then AED 1,000 a year; never put a date on it. [marketing:company-hard-rules-in-copy]
- Quotes: incomplete quotes are "held with notes to the supplier", never "rejected". [marketing:company-hard-rules-in-copy]
- Response: suppliers must respond within 5 working days of a reveal or the customer is refunded in full, without having to ask. [marketing:company-hard-rules-in-copy]
- Also: public text never uses the two-letter abbreviation for machine intelligence (founder rule); describe what the product does instead. [marketing:company-hard-rules-in-copy]
- You pay only if you choose a supplier and want to see who they are: a 3% Reveal Fee on the value of the quotation you choose (before VAT), charged once, by card or Apple Pay. [marketing:company-pricing-message]
- If the revealed supplier does not respond within 5 working days, everything you paid is refunded, and you do not need to ask. [marketing:company-pricing-message]
- Registration is free permanently for the first 1,000 suppliers; after that the subscription is AED 1,000 a year. [marketing:company-pricing-message]
- Never attach a date to this. [marketing:company-pricing-message]
- Things the seat must not say: "free" without the reveal-fee condition; a fee cap or minimum (there is none); "from AED X" examples that imply a fixed price; "cheapest"; "no hidden fees" unless the same sentence says what the customer does pay: the 3% Reveal Fee, shown in full before payment, with no card fee added. [marketing:company-pricing-message]
- Why the model matters for marketing economics: the fee scales with ticket size, so a fit-out or villa-renovation request is worth tens of times a cleaning request. [marketing:company-pricing-message]
- The seat explains the fee plainly to every buyer, and directs paid effort to the categories where the fee can repay acquisition (see the category priority entry). [marketing:company-pricing-message]

- Not allowed: reviews paid for directly or in kind; offering discounts, free goods or services in exchange for reviews; review gating (asking only happy customers, or filtering who is asked by sentiment); pressuring people to review while on the premises; asking staff to solicit a set number of reviews or reviews with specific content; and reviews from anyone with a conflict of interest, such as current or former employees or contractual partners. - Staff, the founder's contacts and seat operators never post reviews. [marketing:review-solicitation-rules]
- Reviews are never written for customers, even with permission. [marketing:review-solicitation-rules]

- Reply to every review, positive or negative, within one working day, in the reviewer's language. [marketing:responding-to-reviews-and-complaints]
- Never reveal any party's identity, quote amounts, VAT details or contact details; anonymity and privacy rules apply in public too. [marketing:responding-to-reviews-and-complaints]

- Flag only true policy breaches (conflict of interest, not a genuine experience, harassment, personal data); never flag a review for being negative. [marketing:responding-to-reviews-and-complaints]
- Patterns: three similar complaints in a month go to the CEO seat as a root-cause item. [marketing:responding-to-reviews-and-complaints]
