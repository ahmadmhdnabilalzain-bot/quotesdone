- QuotesDone is a UAE B2B quotation marketplace: a buyer who has signed up posts one request, verified suppliers across the UAE quote anonymously, the platform levels the quotes into a comparison report, and the buyer pays a reveal fee only to see the supplier they choose. [ceo:company-vision]
- The standard the founder set (11 Aug 2026) is not "the agent exists" or "the workflow runs". [ceo:company-vision]
- The fee the customer pays: a 3% Reveal Fee on the selected quotation value before VAT, uncapped, once per reveal. [ceo:company-hard-rules]
- Current wording follows the texts signed on 14 Sep 2026, which supersede older versions. [ceo:company-hard-rules]
- Revenue has one engine: the 3% Reveal Fee paid by the customer on the chosen quotation value before VAT, uncapped. [ceo:company-pricing]
- Nothing is added for paying by card (Cabinet Resolution 66/2023 Art. 5(3)): QuotesDone absorbs Stripe's cost, which the model takes as 2.9% plus AED 1 for UAE cards, or 3.9% plus AED 1 for other cards, plus the invoicing fee and tax on Stripe fees. [ceo:company-pricing]
- The plan model (run 23 Sep 2026) is the company's evidence base. [ceo:company-plan-scenarios]

- Every live trade has at least ten verified, responsive suppliers, the high-fee trades far more (the plan sizes about 40 each for fit-out, joinery, flooring and steel and building materials), and most released requests receive three or more complete quotes. [ceo:company-what-winning-looks-like]
- Refunds stay at or below about 5% of reveals, suppliers answer inside 5 working days, and the hard rules are applied exactly, including the full refund the CEO seat decides on the record when a revealed supplier stays silent (no refund is automatic). [ceo:company-what-winning-looks-like]
