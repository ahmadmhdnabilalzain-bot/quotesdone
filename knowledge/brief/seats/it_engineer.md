# it_engineer: operating facts from its own verified pack (internal; settled, they override any SOURCE)
- QuotesDone is a UAE B2B quotation marketplace: a customer signs up, posts a request, suppliers quote anonymously, the platform compares the best three technical quotations and lists every other quote, and the customer pays a reveal fee only when choosing to see a supplier's identity. [it_engineer:company-vision]
- The founder's standard for every change is that the company can operate, learn, manage itself, identify problems, make professional decisions and improve its own performance with minimal founder involvement. "The agent exists", "the workflow runs" or "the table was created" is explicitly not the standard. [it_engineer:company-vision]
- For the it_engineer seat this is concrete: the founder's example is a customer trying to pay while Stripe hangs; the IT seat detects it, diagnoses it and resolves it (retry, fail over, roll back, fix and deploy) in the moment. [it_engineer:company-vision]
- An alert or email is a record of what the seat did, never a hand-off asking the founder to solve it. [it_engineer:company-vision]
- The founder's only hard rules are those in the Terms and Conditions; the signed texts of 14 Sep 2026 supersede older wording. [it_engineer:company-hard-rules]
- The it_engineer seat enforces them in code and treats any gap as a severity-1 defect. [it_engineer:company-hard-rules]
- - Reveal fee: 3% of the selected quotation value, paid by the customer, never the supplier, with no cap, charged once per reveal by card or Apple Pay, with nothing added for paying by card (QuotesDone bears Stripe's fee), and the total shown before payment. [it_engineer:company-hard-rules]
- - No request without an account: OTP-verified mobile or email, name, date of birth (under 18 is rejected) and accepted terms. [it_engineer:company-hard-rules]
- The reveal fee: 3% of the ex-VAT value of the quotation the customer selects, uncapped, paid by the customer once per reveal. [it_engineer:company-pricing]
- Nothing is added for paying by card (Cabinet Resolution 66/2023 Art. 5(3), since 24 Sep 2026): Stripe's fee for the card actually used (recorded basis 2.9% plus AED 1 for UAE cards and 3.9% plus AED 1 otherwise), Stripe Invoicing, international, Amex or wallet rates and tax on Stripe fees are all a cost QuotesDone bears, and the checkout refuses any non-zero card-fee line (card_surcharge_refused). [it_engineer:company-pricing]
- Supplier subscription: free permanently for the first 1,000 supplier registrations and AED 1,000 a year from the 1,001st, with 30 days' notice and re-confirmation for any fee change. [it_engineer:company-pricing]
- The non-response recovery charged to a silent supplier (the refunded amount, AED 500 admin (AED 250 for a supplier whose accepted Supplier Agreement is v12 or earlier) and 9% a year interest) is a recovery, not planned revenue. [it_engineer:company-pricing]
