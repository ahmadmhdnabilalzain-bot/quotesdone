## 1. Mandatory codes and authorities, by emirate

### Dubai (Dubai Municipality, DM)
- **Dubai Building Code (DBC), 2021 edition.** Decree No. (45) of 2021 makes it the single building code for the whole emirate, special development zones included. It applies to building designs permitted after the decree took effect. The DBC sets *minimum* requirements, and a design may exceed them.
  - Decree: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2021/Decree%20No.%20(45)%20of%202021%20Concerning%20the%20Dubai%20Building%20Code.html
  - DM page: https://www.dm.gov.ae/business/dubai-building-code/
  - Code PDF (u.ae mirror): https://u.ae/-/media/Documents-2023/Dubai-Building-Code_English_2021-Edition_compressed-(1).pdf
- **DEWA** checks DBC compliance when it reviews drawings for design approval. DEWA's electrical rules sit inside DBC Parts G and K. https://dewa.gov.ae/-/media/Files/Consultants-and-Contractors/Circulars/Dubai-Building-Code-Circular-EN.ashx
- Whether a newer DBC edition has replaced the 2021 edition: (unverified). Check dm.gov.ae.

### Dubai free zones and master developments
- **Trakhees (PCFC):** a Trakhees circular makes the DBC mandatory for all permit applications in its jurisdiction from 30 Dec 2021. https://pcfc.ae/en/Rules%20and%20Regulations/Trakhees/Civil%20Engineering%20Department/Permits%20(Facilities)/Circulars/CEDGR-135%20Dubai%20Building%20Code%20Circular%202021.pdf
- **Dubai Development Authority (DDA):** businesses in DDA areas apply for event permits and building modification permits (temporary tents, kiosks, enclosures) through the DDA portal. https://dda.gov.ae/-/media/1816C2A150044F79BCD8556F1C894A44.pdf
- In a free zone, the zone's authority issues permits, not DM. The DBC still applies.

### Abu Dhabi (Department of Municipalities and Transport, DMT)
- **Abu Dhabi International Building Code** is one of a family of Abu Dhabi codes. It is based on the 2009 ICC International Codes and implemented by DMT. https://www.iccsafe.org/international-code-adoptions/united-arab-emirates/
- The Executive Council adopted it, and it was made compulsory first for government projects. https://meconstructionnews.com/6732/implementation-of-abu-dhabi-international-building-code-to-improve-standards/amp
- Adoption year and current edition: (unverified).
- **OSHAD-SF** (Abu Dhabi OSH System Framework) is Abu Dhabi's workplace safety framework, version 3.1 issued March 2017. Abu Dhabi City Municipality has asked construction contractors, consultants and developers to register under it. https://meconstructionnews.com/30134/adm-calls-on-contractors-to-join-oshad-sf-to-improve-hse-standards/amp , https://dmt.gov.ae/en/adm/Environment-Healthy-Safty/About-EHS
- The OSHAD code-of-practice number for scaffolding and work at height: (unverified). Third-party sources disagree, so do not quote a number.

### Sharjah (Sharjah Municipality)
- Sharjah Municipality's Contractors and Consultants Classification Section classifies contractors and consultants. A Contractors Evaluation and Classification committee was formed after a 2019 Executive Council resolution. https://portal.shjmun.gov.ae/en/MediaCenter/News/Pages/Contractors-Evaluation,-Classification-Committee-holds-its-first-meeting.aspx
- Applications for RCC building permits are filed by an engineering consultancy office accredited by Sharjah Municipality, not by the contractor. Source: dubizzle guide, secondary: https://www.dubizzle.com/blog/property/apply-building-permit-sharjah/
- Sharjah Housing-supervised residential permits need the contractor's valid licence, the contractor engineer card and the contractor contract. https://malihamun.shj.ae/en/services/issuing-a-residential-building-permit-under-the-supervision-of-sharjah-housing

### Federal (all emirates)
- **Midday work break (MoHRE).** Outdoor work in direct sun is banned from 12:30 to 15:00, 15 June to 15 September. 2025 was the 21st consecutive year. The fine is AED 5,000 per worker, up to AED 50,000. Employers must provide shaded rest areas, cold water and first aid. Work that cannot technically be stopped (for example a concrete pour or asphalt) can be exempt. Reports go to 600590000. Source: news coverage of MoHRE's announcement: https://gulfnews.com/uae/uae-announces-summer-midday-break-june-15-to-september-15-1230pm-to-3pm-1.88451178 , https://en.aletihad.ae/news/uae/4489967/mohre-implements-midday-break-from-june-15-to-september-15
  - The 2026 dates were not confirmed from a MoHRE source: (unverified). The pattern has been stable every year.
- **Steel rebar conformity (MoIAT/ECAS).** Under a 2023 Cabinet Resolution, carbon-steel reinforcing bars made or imported in the UAE, free zones included, need an ECAS Conformity Certificate before they go on sale, and test reports must be kept for 10 years. Billets and coils used to make rebar are also covered. https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2346/download
  - Industry reporting gives a preparation period from 1 May 2025 to 1 Nov 2026: (unverified with MoIAT). https://www.steelradar.com/en/ecas-mandatory-for-rebar-in-abu-dhabi
  - ECAS certificate valid for 1 year, renewable. Source: SGS, secondary: https://www.sgs.com/-/media/sgscorp/documents/corporate/brochures/sgs-uae-moiat-data-sheet-en-22.cdn.en.pdf
- **Concrete blocks:** whether ECAS certification is mandatory for them was not confirmed: (unverified).

## 2. Permits and approvals

- **Three separate things:** the trade licence (the company exists), contractor classification (the company may practise the activity at a grade), and the project building permit (this specific job). A licence and a classification do not replace the building permit. Source: secondary guides summarising DM practice, https://www.dm.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/4.-Contracting-Activities-Classfication.pdf (activity list)
- **Building permits in Dubai** are issued by DM, or in free zones by the zone authority (Trakhees, DDA). Civil Defence approval forms part of the approval chain for life-safety items.
- **Demolition:** DM lists "Wrecking & Demolition Works" (activity 451001) as its own specialised contracting activity. https://www.dm.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/4.-Contracting-Activities-Classfication.pdf
  - Demolition permit documents, fees and steps: (unverified). Check DM's permit services.
- **Tents and event structures (Dubai):** an event tent needs a special permit through the ePermits portal (Dubai Economy and Tourism). Required documents include a Civil Defence approval or NOC and a DM approval letter. DET estimates AED 5,000 to 15,000 per month. https://dubaidet.gov.ae/en/our-services/for-consumers-and-students/temporary-tent-permit
  - Older DM rules required Civil Defence approval before the DM permit, transportable foundations, and no blocking of roads, parking or utilities. Fines reached up to AED 50,000. These are older news sources and may be outdated: https://gulfnews.com/uae/set-up-ramadan-tents-but-follow-rules-1.1353523 , https://thenationalnews.com/uae/municipalities-get-tough-over-ramadan-tents-1.551783
- **Residential parking shades (Dubai)** are an RTA permit, not a DM permit. Installers must be RTA-approved companies, using approved colours and materials, and the shades must be removable. Source: secondary: https://www.pitstoparabia.com/en/news/residential-parking-shade-permit
- **Sharjah maintenance permits** need a Sharjah-issued contractor licence and an owner-contractor agreement. Source: dubizzle, secondary: https://www.dubizzle.com/blog/property/property-maintenance-permit-renewal-sharjah/

## 3. Contractor and consultant licensing and classification

### Contractors, Dubai
- **Dubai Law No. 7 of 2025** puts contracting under a unified DM Contractor Register:
  - Contracting has been a registered, classified activity since 8 Jan 2026.
  - It covers free zones and the DIFC.
  - Project value ceilings are tied to the contractor's classification.
  - Subcontracting needs prior approval.
  - Contractors licensed in Dubai for the first time start in the lowest category.
  - Existing contractors must comply by 8 Jan 2027.
  - Technical staff in building, construction and demolition activities need DM professional competency certificates.
  - Source: law-firm summary, secondary: https://neolegal.ae/insights/dubai-contractor-registration-law-7-2025 , https://www.fenwickelliott.com/?p=34794
  - Exact text and dates against the official legislation portal (dlp.dubai.gov.ae): (unverified).
- **Grade scale:** secondary sources disagree. One describes "G+1 to Unlimited", another "Grade 1 to 5". Do not quote a grade scale: (unverified).
- DM's activity codes: Building Contracting 452001 (a main activity with grades) and Wrecking & Demolition 451001. https://www.dm.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/4.-Contracting-Activities-Classfication.pdf

### Engineering and architectural consultancies, Dubai
- **Dubai Law No. 14 of 2025** replaces the 1994 local order.
  - No one may provide or advertise engineering consultancy without a trade licence and DM registration. Registration lasts 1 year and is renewed annually.
  - Classification is based on experience and capability, mainly the registered engineers.
  - A branch of a UAE office needs at least 3 consecutive years of experience. A branch of a foreign office needs at least 10.
  - Offices must not work outside their licensed scope or use unregistered engineers.
  - Fines run up to AED 100,000, with suspension, downgrading or deregistration.
  - Sources: https://mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2025/october/05-10/mohammed-bin-rashid , https://www.tamimi.com/news/dubai-issues-new-law-regulating-engineering-consultancy-activities-key-takeaway-points/
  - Effective date: secondary sources say around April 2026, with 1 year for existing offices to regularise: (unverified).

## 5. Spec terms glossary (brief, general trade usage, not regulatory)

- **G+n**: ground plus n upper floors (building height description).
- **RCC**: reinforced cement concrete.
- **Rebar grade (e.g. B500B)**: yield strength class of the reinforcing steel. Ask for the mill certificate and ECAS certificate.
- **Hollow or solid block (e.g. 200 mm)**: concrete masonry unit, given by thickness. Ask for the load-bearing or non-load-bearing class.
- **MS / GI / HDG**: mild steel / galvanised iron / hot-dip galvanised.
- **Shop drawings**: the fabricator's detailed drawings, approved by the consultant before fabrication.
- **Membrane (torch-on bitumen, PVC/TPO, liquid PU)**: types of waterproofing layer. Ask for the thickness, number of layers and the flood test.
- **Flood test**: water held on a waterproofed roof or wet area (often 24 to 48 h, by trade practice) to prove it does not leak.
- **Inverted roof**: insulation laid above the waterproofing membrane.
- **PTFE / PVC tensile fabric**: membranes used in shade structures and tents. Ask for the fire-rating certificate (Civil Defence).
- **Scaffold tag (green/red)**: trade practice showing whether a scaffold has been inspected and is safe or unsafe.
- **NOC**: no-objection certificate from an authority or the master developer.
- **Completion certificate**: the authority's sign-off at the end of a permitted project.

## 6. What a buyer should check in a quote

1. **Licence and activity:** the trade licence lists the exact activity (for example demolition 451001 or building contracting 452001 in Dubai) and is valid in the emirate where the work is.
2. **Classification fits the job:** the contractor's grade covers the project size and value. In Dubai this has been registered in the DM Contractor Register since Jan 2026.
3. **Consultant:** works that need a permit need a registered or accredited engineering consultant. In Dubai the consultant is registered with DM. In Sharjah, an accredited office submits the RCC permit.
4. **Who gets the permits:** the quote says who obtains the building, demolition or tent permit and the Civil Defence or NOC approvals, and who pays the fees.
5. **Materials compliance:** for rebar, the ECAS conformity certificate and mill certificates. For other products, any certificates the consultant requires.
6. **Code:** the design and works follow the governing code: the DBC in Dubai (Trakhees and DDA included), and the Abu Dhabi codes and OSHAD-SF in Abu Dhabi.
7. **Safety and programme:** the programme allows for the summer midday break, and scaffolding and work at height carry a method statement and risk assessment.
8. **Scope exclusions:** the quote lists the exclusions: authority fees, temporary works, debris removal, testing (including the flood test), and making good.
9. **Warranty:** the waterproofing and roofing warranty period, and who gives it (applicator or manufacturer). No statutory figure was verified here.
10. **Subcontracting:** in Dubai, subcontracting needs prior approval under the 2025 law. Ask whether the work will be subcontracted.
