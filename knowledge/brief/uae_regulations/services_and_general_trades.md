# UAE regulations: services and general trades

- **Scope (category codes):** cleaning, facade-cleaning, pest, movers, landscaping, job-shop-manufacturing, general-services-other
- Compiled 2026-10-12 by Claude (it_engineer session) for the sales_engineer trade digest.
- **Agents: cite this as general guidance; the authority's current text governs.**
- Every source below was accessed 2026-10-12. "(unverified)" means the item came from a secondary source or could not be checked against an official text. Do not present it as a rule.

---

## 1. Cross-trade rules (all categories)

### 1.1 The trade licence activity must match the work
- A supplier's trade licence lists its permitted activities. The work quoted should fall within those activities. Each activity type maps to a specific trade activity code and may need separate Dubai Municipality approvals depending on scope (unverified, secondary source). Source: https://www.dubaisouthbh.com/blogs/building-cleaning-license-dubai
- Licensing authorities: Dubai = Department of Economy and Tourism (DET, formerly DED); Abu Dhabi = Abu Dhabi Department of Economic Development (ADDED); Sharjah = Sharjah Economic Development Department (SEDD). Free-zone companies are licensed by their free-zone authority. (Authority names are general knowledge. The per-activity code lists were not fetched in this session.) (unverified)
- Free-zone and consultancy guides give different activity codes for the same trade. For example, pest control appears as 749301 in one guide and 8129.92 in another. **Never quote an activity code.** Ask the supplier for its licence and read the activity text. Source: https://www.meydanfz.ae/activity-hub/apply-for-a-public-health-pests-control-services-license-in-dubai

### 1.2 Midday work ban (MoHRE)
- **Rule:** MoHRE bans work under direct sunlight and in open-air areas between **12:30 and 15:00, from 15 June to 15 September**. The 2025 edition was the 21st consecutive year. Source: https://mohre.gov.ae/en/media-center/news/3/6/2025/mohre-to-implement-midday-break-for-the-21st-consecutive-year-from-15-june-to-15-september-2025
- **2026 dates:** no official 2026 notice was found. The same window is likely but unverified.
- **Employer duties:** provide a shaded rest area, plus cooling, drinking water and first aid (secondary source). Daily hours during the ban period may not exceed 8 (secondary source).
- **Exemptions:** work that technically must continue, such as asphalt or concrete pours and emergency repairs to utilities (secondary source).
- **Fine:** AED 5,000 per worker, up to AED 50,000 where several workers are involved (secondary source). Source: https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/uae-announces-midday-break-for-outdoor-workers-from-june-15-to-sept-15-1.96142042
- **Legal basis:** reported as Ministerial Resolution No. 44 of 2022 (Occupational Health and Safety and Labour Accommodation) (unverified, secondary source).
- **Effect on quotes:** outdoor jobs between June and September (landscaping, facade work, moving, outdoor pest treatment) lose midday hours. Expect longer schedules or early and late shifts.

### 1.3 VAT on quotes (FTA)
- **Rate:** standard rate 5%, in force since 1 January 2018. Source: https://mof.gov.ae/?p=44399
- **Mandatory registration:** required when taxable supplies and imports exceed **AED 375,000**. Source: https://mof.gov.ae/?p=44399
- **Voluntary registration:** possible from AED 187,500 (secondary source; confirm on https://tax.gov.ae). Source: https://www.kayrouzandassociates.com/insights/vat-registration-uae-thresholds-fta-process-2026
- **Buyer check:**
  - A supplier that charges VAT must show its TRN (Tax Registration Number) and state the price both excluding and including VAT.
  - A supplier below the threshold may lawfully charge no VAT. "No VAT" is not by itself a red flag.
  - The TRN-on-invoice requirement is general knowledge and was not fetched this session (unverified).

### 1.4 Consumer Protection (applies to consumers, not necessarily B2B)
- **Law:** Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on Consumer Protection replaced Federal Law No. 24 of 2006. It covers goods and services across the UAE, free zones included. Its Executive Regulation is Cabinet Decision No. 66 of 2023, in effect from 14 October 2023.
  - Ministry of Economy legislation page: https://www.moet.gov.ae/en/consumer-protection-legislations
  - Law-firm summary: https://www.pinsentmasons.com/en-gb/out-law/analysis/uae-consumer-protection-regime-revamped
- **Supplier duties under the Ministry's framework:**
  - announce prices
  - honour warranties
  - repair or re-provide a defective service at no charge
  - provide contracts and invoices in Arabic (other languages may accompany them)
  - protect consumer data
- **Fine levels:** secondary sources cite fines of up to AED 100,000 for some violations (unverified). Source: https://mondaq.com/consumer-law/1495004/uae-implements-revised-penalties-and-amendments-under-consumer-protection-law
- **Scope for QuotesDone:** whether the law protects a business buyer in a B2B deal is not established here (unverified). Do not tell a business buyer it is protected by this law.

## 2. Cleaning (`cleaning`)
- **Licence:** a trade licence with a cleaning-services activity. The activity covering building or exterior cleaning is a separate activity from general cleaning (secondary source). Source: https://www.dubaisouthbh.com/blogs/building-cleaning-license-dubai
- **Midday ban:** applies to any outdoor portion of the work (see 1.2).
- **Chemicals:** a specific Dubai Municipality rule on cleaning chemicals was not found this session (unverified).
- **Buyer should check:**
  - the licence activity covers the exact service (office, residential, deep clean, kitchen exhaust and so on)
  - staff count and shifts
  - whether consumables and chemicals are included
  - VAT treatment
  - liability insurance (insurance is common practice, not a verified legal requirement)

## 3. Facade cleaning: rope access and BMU (`facade-cleaning`)
- **Dubai:** Dubai Municipality guideline DM-HSD-GU35 ("Technical Guidelines for Usage of Rope Access System", V3) says rope access should be done only by specialist, certified companies and personnel. It also says companies are checked on both technical and quality-assurance aspects. Source: https://www.dm.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/DM-HSD-GU35-URAS2_Technical-Guidelines-for-Usage-of-Rope-Access-System_V3.pdf
  - The PDF could not be machine-read in full this session. Its detailed thresholds (wind limits, anchor tests) are not reproduced here.
- **IRATA:**
  - Secondary sources say only IRATA-audited companies may run rope access in Dubai since October 2019, with technicians holding accredited certification (unverified). Source: https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/facade-cleaning-company-setup-dubai
  - Rope operatives on high-rise structures need IRATA or an equivalent qualification (unverified). Source: https://www.meydanfz.ae/activity-hub/start-an-exterior-building-cleaning-business-in-dubai
- **Sharjah:** the Sharjah Private Sector Safety Authority (SPSA) code OSHJ-CoP-04 "Working at Height Safety" (V1, Sept 2021) has a rope access section. Source: https://mc.spsa.shj.ae/Media/documents/0n5TzmzMWEWSovRZUMdXaA_4.%20Working%20at%20Height%20Safety%20Version%201%20English.pdf
- **Abu Dhabi:** an OSHAD working-at-height code was not located this session (unverified). The international reference is ISO 22846-2:2012 on rope access: https://www.iso.org/standard/51354.html
- **BMU (building maintenance unit, the gondola or cradle on the roof):** a BMU-specific authority approval process was not found (unverified). Ask for the building's BMU inspection or certification records, held by the building owner or facility manager.
- **Road occupation:** RTA road-occupation permits are reported to be needed only when a cradle base or vehicle stands on a public road (unverified, secondary source).
- **Buyer should check:**
  - IRATA (or equivalent) company and technician certificates and their levels
  - the method statement and risk assessment
  - who provides anchors or the BMU
  - third-party liability insurance
  - weather stand-down terms
  - midday-ban scheduling in summer

## 4. Pest control (`pest`)
- **Dubai NOC:**
  - A company working in Dubai needs a No Objection Certificate from the **Dubai Municipality Public Health Pest Control Section** before it can get the trade licence.
  - DM publishes a monthly list of authorised companies (Arabic). The listed companies are approved for **general pest control only**. Fumigation, termite control and bird control are excluded from that list.
  - Companies are grouped by number of qualified staff: Group A (more than 25), Group B (11 to 25), Group C (1 to 10). DM rates performance from A (best) to E.
  - Authorised list, July 2026: https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2023/06/قائمة-شركات-مكافحة-الحشرات-المصرح-لها-بالعمل-يوليو2026.pdf
- **Pesticides:**
  - Pesticides must be registered federally. The Dubai Municipality page links to the MoCCAE (Ministry of Climate Change and Environment) registered-pesticide directory. Source: https://www.dm.gov.ae/?p=7618
  - Registered companies must supply DM's Pest Control Section with the list of chemicals they use (secondary source, Gulf News). Source: https://gulfnews.com/uae/health/uae-pesticide-tragedy-clear-laws-in-place-1.1209226
- **Staffing:** a qualified supervisor and operator are required, along with secure pesticide storage. Sources disagree on the supervisor's qualification (a degree vs a DM-approved certificate), so treat it as unverified.
- **Residential fumigation:** fumigation and high-toxicity treatment have been tightly restricted after fatal incidents (Gulf News source above). Exact current rules are unverified.
- **Buyer should check:**
  - the company appears on the current DM authorised list (Dubai)
  - the licence covers the specific service (general, termite or fumigation)
  - product names are registered (MoCCAE)
  - re-entry time and safety instructions
  - the warranty or re-treatment period

## 5. Movers (`movers`)
- **Licence:** a trade licence with a moving / packing / transport activity. A mover-specific authority rule was not found this session (unverified).
- **Vehicles:** goods vehicles fall under RTA (Dubai) or emirate transport-authority rules. Specific permit names are unverified.
- **Building permissions:** many towers require the community or building management's move-in/move-out permit. This is building practice, not law (unverified).
- **Midday ban:** applies to outdoor loading in summer (see 1.2).
- **Buyer should check:**
  - whether the price is fixed or by volume or truck
  - packing materials
  - insurance cover (goods in transit) and its limits
  - dismantling and assembly
  - access permits and who obtains them
  - VAT

## 6. Landscaping and irrigation (`landscaping`)
- **Dubai TSE framework:** Dubai Municipality's legislation index lists these instruments. Their full text was not retrieved, and no clause numbers are cited.
  - Local Order No. 8 of 2002 on sewage, irrigation and drainage
  - Local Order No. 1 of 2015 on the use of treated sewage effluent (TSE)
  - Resolution No. 21 of 2012 on fees for TSE use in private farm irrigation
  - Source: https://www.dm.gov.ae/?p=359668
- **Recycled-water network:** Dubai irrigates green spaces with reclaimed water through a network of about 2,400 km. Source: https://www.dm.gov.ae/municipality-business/waste-sewerage-agency/
- **Contractor registration:** Dubai Law No. 7 of 2025 on contracting activities (reported in effect from 8 January 2026) makes landscaping a classified contracting activity registered with DM, graded by certified staff (unverified, secondary source). Source: https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/landscaping-company-setup-dubai
- **Design expectations:** DM is reported to check TSE (not potable) irrigation, drip efficiency, backflow prevention and salt-tolerant planting (unverified, same source).
- **Midday ban:** applies (see 1.2).
- **Buyer should check:**
  - the water source (TSE or potable) and who arranges the connection (DM / DEWA)
  - backflow prevention
  - plant species list
  - irrigation-controller spec
  - plant-replacement warranty
  - maintenance schedule
  - the contractor's DM registration or grade (Dubai, 2026 onward)

## 7. Job-shop manufacturing (`job-shop-manufacturing`)
- No specific authority rule was located this session (unverified).
- **Licence:** an industrial or workshop activity matching the process (fabrication, machining and so on). Industrial licensing and workshop location rules vary by emirate (unverified).
- **Buyer should check:**
  - the licence activity
  - the material specification and grade, and mill certificates
  - tolerances
  - quantities
  - lead time
  - delivery terms
  - inspection and acceptance
  - VAT

## 8. General services, other (`general-services-other`)
- Apply section 1: matching licence activity, midday ban for outdoor work, VAT.
- If the service touches a regulated trade (pest, rope access, electrical, gas), apply that trade's rules.

## 9. Glossary
- **DET / ADDED / SEDD:** the economic licensing authorities of Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah.
- **DM:** Dubai Municipality.
- **MoCCAE:** Ministry of Climate Change and Environment (federal pesticide registration).
- **MoHRE:** Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation.
- **FTA:** Federal Tax Authority (VAT).
- **TRN:** Tax Registration Number.
- **NOC:** No Objection Certificate.
- **IRATA:** Industrial Rope Access Trade Association.
- **BMU:** building maintenance unit, the permanent roof-mounted cradle system.
- **TSE:** treated sewage effluent, the recycled water used for irrigation.
- **OSHAD:** Abu Dhabi Occupational Safety and Health Centre.
- **SPSA:** Sharjah Private Sector Safety Authority.

## 10. Quick buyer checklist (any quote)
1. The supplier's licence activity matches the work, and the licence is valid in the emirate where the work happens.
2. Trade-specific approval is present: DM pest list or NOC, IRATA for rope access, DM contractor registration for landscaping in Dubai.
3. The price states whether VAT is included and shows a TRN if VAT is charged.
4. Scope, exclusions, materials and chemicals are named, along with the warranty or re-service terms.
5. Insurance (liability, goods in transit) is stated where relevant.
6. Summer outdoor work allows for the 12:30–15:00 midday ban (15 June to 15 September).
