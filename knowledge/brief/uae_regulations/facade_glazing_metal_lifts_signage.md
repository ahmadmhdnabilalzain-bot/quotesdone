## 2. Facades and cladding (aluminium, curtain wall, ACP, acrylic or composite panels on a facade)

1. **The 2018 UAE Fire and Life Safety Code has a dedicated section on facade system design.** A law-firm summary places it in Chapter 1, Section 4. That location comes from a secondary source, so quote it only as "reported". Sources: https://bsalaw.com/?p=4119 ; https://wfmmedia.com/gcc-codes-facade-fire-key-methodogies/ (accessed 2026-10-12).
2. **Practitioner "ten-point" approach to Civil Defence approval (unverified as official wording).** The reported points are:
   - Test the core material in exposed form.
   - Test the panel as a product.
   - Test the full wall assembly, or use a listed system.
   - Install cavity fire barriers at every slab.
   - Use facade contractors and fabricators approved and licensed by Civil Defence.
   - Have the facade inspected during installation and certified by a facade fire expert or a Civil Defence-approved house of expertise.

   Source: https://wfmmedia.com/gcc-codes-facade-fire-key-methodogies/ (accessed 2026-10-12).
3. **Dubai: ESL certificate of conformity.** DCD set up the Emirates Safety Laboratory. Reports say cladding materials and facade systems need an ESL certificate of conformity, and that tests done at other accredited labs must be assessed by ESL before it issues the certificate. Without the certificate, approvals for construction work are not issued. The deadline reported in 2023 was "by October", and whether it took effect as described is unverified. Sources: https://www.thenationalnews.com/uae/2023/06/06/cladding-fire-dubai-skyscraper/ ; https://gulfnews.com/uae/dubai-announces-new-fire-safety-standards-1.95253431 ; https://beale-law.com/?p=11420 (accessed 2026-10-12).
4. **Older Dubai rule: Dubai Central Laboratory conformity certificate for all facade material (unverified as current).** It may have been superseded by the ESL route. Source: https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/uae%2Fauthorities-tighten-rules-on-building-materials-1.1107359 (accessed 2026-10-12).
5. **A DM circular allowed only fire-retardant panels on residential and office tower facades (reported).** Source: as in item 3 (Gulf News 2019 to 2023 coverage), accessed 2026-10-12.
6. **Manufacturer approval claims are not a register.** For example, Sto announced DCD and ADCD "approved status" for its facade systems. Verify any claimed approval with the authority. Source: https://sto.com/en/news-detail_16320.html (accessed 2026-10-12).

## 3. Glazing performance and glass

1. **Al Sa'fat applies to all new buildings in Dubai.** Silver Sa'fa is the minimum, and Gold and Platinum are enhanced tiers. The current text is the 2nd Edition (January 2023) (unverified edition date; secondary source). Source: https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/dubai-green-building-regulations/ (accessed 2026-10-12).
2. **Numeric glazing limits in Dubai: UNVERIFIED, and the sources conflict.** Do not quote these numbers to buyers as code. Ask the consultant for the project's compliance sheet.
   - One industry source: U-value below 2.1, shading coefficient (SC) at or below 0.4, light transmittance at or above 40%, with stricter limits above 40% glazing area.
   - Another industry source: SC 0.25 and U-value 1.4 for most glazing, with SC 0.4 at 40% glazing or less and 0.25 at 60% or more.

   Sources: https://www.arabianbusiness.com/industries/construction/passing-fa-ade-381529 ; https://wfmmedia.com/building-glass-facades/ (accessed 2026-10-12).
3. **DM glass certification document exists.** DM-DCLD-RD-DP32-5103 (Rev. 11, July 2024), "Certification of Glazed Glass as per Related sections of Al Sa'fat and Dubai Building Code". Only the cover was read, so its content is unverified. Source: https://www.dm.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/DM-DCLD-RD-DP32-5103-IC.pdf (accessed 2026-10-12).
4. **The World Bank profile lists the Dubai energy code as setting a maximum SHGC as a mandatory requirement for residential buildings.** No figure is given. Source: https://www.worldbank.org/en/building-green/explorecountries/united-arab-emirates (accessed 2026-10-12).
5. **Safety glass (where toughened or laminated glass is mandatory): no source found. (unverified)** Refer buyers to the Dubai Building Code text or their consultant.

## 4. Product conformity: MoIAT ECAS / EQM (all emirates)

1. **Regulated products must hold an ECAS or EQM certificate to be sold in the UAE, whether imported or made locally.** Requirements are a valid trade licence and a test report from an accredited laboratory. Sources: https://www.moiat.gov.ae/en/services/issue-conformity-certificates-for-regulated-products ; https://www.sgs.com/en/news/2024/06/pca-2024-q2-navigating-uae-product-conformity-understanding-ecas-and-eqm-certification (accessed 2026-10-12).
2. **An ECAS certificate is valid for one year with annual renewal.** This is per SGS, a MoIAT notified body (secondary source). Source: the SGS URL above (accessed 2026-10-12).
3. **Glass products appear in MoIAT's public product-conformity register.** Whether a specific glass or aluminium product is a regulated product is unverified. Check the register or the relevant technical regulation. Source: https://moiat.gov.ae/en/open-data/product-conformity?page=1554 (accessed 2026-10-12).

## 5. Lifts and escalators (elevators)

1. **Dubai: every lift, escalator, moving walkway and conveyor needs a certificate of examination and compliance.** It is issued by a company accredited by DM's accreditation centre and renewed periodically (2017 statement by a DM official). Source: https://www.pressreader.com/uae/khaleej-times/20170914/281698319912316 (accessed 2026-10-12).
2. **DM publishes guideline DM-HSD-GU48-ECLA2, "Technical Guidelines for Examination and Certification of Cranes, Hoists, Lifts and Other Lifting Equipment" (V5).** The PDF could not be parsed for this file, so the inspection interval is unverified. Source: https://www.dm.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/DM-HSD-GU48-ECLA2_Technical-Guidelines-for-Examination-and-Certification-of-Cranes-Hoists-Lifts-and-Other-Lifting-Equipment_V5.pdf (accessed 2026-10-12).
3. **Reported Dubai practice (unverified; consultancy source):**
   - No lift may operate without a valid examination certificate.
   - Installation and maintenance are done by DM-registered companies.
   - Every lift is under a maintenance contract.
   - Periodic third-party inspection is done by an EIAC-accredited body independent of the installer.

   Source: https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/elevator-lift-company-dubai (accessed 2026-10-12).
4. **Dubai escalator circular: buildings and malls must fit protective covers and anti-climb protection at escalators.** Its basis is Local Order No. 11 of 2003. Source: https://www.pressreader.com/uae/khaleej-times/20170324/281694024605501 (accessed 2026-10-12).
5. **Abu Dhabi: AED 5,000 fine where a lift has no maintenance contract with an accredited maintenance entity.** It applies to existing buildings and buildings under construction, except independent private houses. The basis is Cabinet Resolution No. 24 of 2012 on Civil Defence services. Source: https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/abu-dhabi-5000-fine-building-owners-no-elevator-maintenance-contract (accessed 2026-10-12).
6. **Abu Dhabi periodic inspection interval and certificate: not found. (unverified)** ADS 22/2017 on lifting-equipment inspection is reported, but its coverage of passenger lifts is unverified. Source: https://leeaint.com/leea-news/news-leea-and-adqcc-continue-consultations-for-proficiency-testing-of-lifting-equipment-inspectors (accessed 2026-10-12).

## 6. Signage and outdoor advertising (signage, acrylic signs)

1. **Dubai: Decree No. 6 of 2020 governs advertising.** It repealed Local Order No. 6 of 1986 and requires DM to issue a manual. Source: https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/dubai-to-impose-curbs-on-outdoor-advertising-2 (accessed 2026-10-12).
2. **The permitting body depends on location:**
   - DM
   - RTA
   - Department of Economy (formerly DED)
   - the free-zone or private-development authority
   - Dubai Civil Aviation Authority
   - Dubai Maritime City Authority

   Source: https://campaignme.com/marking-your-outdoor-territory-by-al-tamimis-fiona-robertson/ (accessed 2026-10-12).
3. **Each sign, including a shopfront facade sign, needs its own prior permit covering location, structure and content. A trade licence alone does not cover it (unverified; consultancy source).** The same source reports a single platform, "Mada Media", issuing permits for RTA and DM (unverified). Source: https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/signage-advertising-company-setup-dubai (accessed 2026-10-12).
4. **Prohibited locations include:**
   - historic, government and residential buildings
   - traffic lights and signs
   - trees
   - places of worship
   - military and restricted areas

   Source: https://gulfnews.com/business/dubai-residential-buildings-cannot-feature-ads-any-longer-1.69943225 (accessed 2026-10-12).
5. **When the permit expires, the advertiser must remove the sign and restore the site.** Source: as in item 2 (accessed 2026-10-12).
6. **Size limits: the only figure found (10 m x 30 m hoardings) was a 2010 draft. (unverified; do not quote)**

## 7. Short glossary of spec terms

- **ACP:** aluminium composite panel. The core type (polyethylene vs fire-retardant or mineral core) is what Civil Defence approval turns on.
- **Curtain wall (stick or unitised):** a non-structural aluminium-and-glass facade hung from the slabs.
- **DGU / IGU:** double or insulated glazing unit.
- **Toughened (tempered) glass:** heat-treated, breaks into small pieces.
- **Laminated glass:** layers bonded with PVB or SGP, and stays in place when broken.
- **Heat-strengthened glass:** between annealed and toughened.
- **Low-E:** a low-emissivity coating.
- **U-value (W/m²K):** heat transfer; lower is better.
- **SHGC / SC:** solar heat gain coefficient / shading coefficient; lower means less solar heat. SC ≈ SHGC / 0.87.
- **VLT:** visible light transmittance.
- **Thermal break:** an insulating strip in an aluminium profile.
- **Powder coating / anodising:** aluminium finishes.
- **Rolling shutter (garage-doors):** slat curtain on a barrel; it can be manual or motorised. Fire-rated shutters need the fire certificates in section 2.
- **MRL lift:** machine-room-less. **Rated load (kg / persons), speed (m/s), stops:** the core lift spec.
- **Cast vs extruded acrylic (PMMA):** cast is harder and better for fabricated signs.

## 8. What a buyer should check in a quote

1. **Facade or cladding:** the quote names the exact panel or system and attaches the Civil Defence approval or ESL certificate of conformity for that product (Dubai) or the ADCD approval (Abu Dhabi), plus the system test reference. A brochure claim is not enough.
2. **Facade contractor:** the quote states the contractor's Civil Defence approval or licence, if the project needs one (reported requirement; confirm with the consultant).
3. **Glazing:** the quote states glass make-up (for example 6 mm toughened Low-E + 12 mm air + 6 mm clear), U-value, SHGC or SC, VLT, and which panes are toughened or laminated. It matches the consultant's Al Sa'fat or energy compliance sheet, and DM glass certification is included where required.
4. **Aluminium:** the quote names the profile system, alloy and temper, thickness, finish and its warranty, thermal break (yes or no), hardware brand, and sealant and gasket types.
5. **Regulated products:** the quote has an ECAS or EQM certificate number, checkable on the MoIAT register, and it is still valid, since certificates last one year.
6. **Lifts:**
   - rated load, speed, stops and drive type
   - whether the supplier is DM-registered (Dubai)
   - the first examination and certificate: who pays, and whether by an accredited body
   - the maintenance contract: term, response time and price after the warranty. In Abu Dhabi, no contract means a fine.
7. **Signage:** the quote says who obtains the permit and pays its fee, whether the design matches the permitted size, location and content, and covers material (cast or extruded acrylic, ACP, LED), illumination, structure or fixing, and removal at the end of the permit.
8. **All trades:** the quote states what is included (design, shop drawings, authority submissions, testing, installation, scaffolding), the warranty period and terms, and the validity of the offer.
