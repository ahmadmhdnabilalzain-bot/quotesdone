# UAE regulations digest: HVAC, plumbing and MEP

**Scope (category codes):** hvac, plumbing, mep, combined-mep, insulation, water-tank-cleaning, swimming-pools
**Compiled:** 2026-10-12 by Claude (it_engineer session) for the sales_engineer trade digest.
**Agents:** cite this as general guidance; the authority's current text governs.

All sources were accessed on 2026-10-12. Anything marked "(unverified)" could not be confirmed from an official or primary source. Do not present it to a buyer as fact. No clause numbers are given unless the source states them.

---

## 1. Authorities and codes by emirate

### Dubai
- **Dubai Building Code (DBC).** Created by Decree No. (45) of 2021, which defines it as minimum design requirements for public health, safety and sustainability. Dubai Municipality (DM) publishes it.
  Source: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2021/Decree%20No.%20(45)%20of%202021%20Concerning%20the%20Dubai%20Building%20Code.html
- **DEWA.** A DEWA circular (17 Jan 2022) says DBC compliance is mandatory and is checked when drawings are submitted for DEWA design approval on every project in Dubai.
  Source: https://dewa.gov.ae/-/media/Files/Consultants-and-Contractors/Circulars/Dubai-Building-Code-Circular-EN.ashx
- **Trakhees (PCFC zones).** The DBC has been mandatory for permit applications in Trakhees jurisdiction since 30 Dec 2021.
  Source: https://pcfc.ae/en/Rules%20and%20Regulations/Trakhees/Civil%20Engineering%20Department/Permits%20(Facilities)/Circulars/CEDGR-135%20Dubai%20Building%20Code%20Circular%202021.pdf
- **The DBC part letters that cover HVAC and plumbing (unverified).** Check the DBC table of contents on dm.gov.ae.
- **Al Sa'fat (Dubai Green Building System).** It replaced the Dubai Green Building Regulations from 19 Oct 2020. Every new building must meet the mandatory requirements for **Silver Sa'fa**. **Golden** and **Platinum Sa'fa** are optional. The current edition is the 2nd Edition (Jan 2023), aligned with the DBC. Administrative Resolution No. (154) of 2020 approved the Al Sa'fat bylaw. The government-stated energy savings are 19% for Silver, 32% for Gold and 35% for Platinum. Al Sa'fat directly drives HVAC efficiency, insulation and water-fixture specifications.
  Sources: https://www.dm.gov.ae/municipality-business/al-safat-dubai-green-building-system/ ; https://mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2023/December/07-12/Green-Buildings-in-Dubai
  - Certificate validity periods (unverified; from a property blog only).
  - "Silver Sa'fa is required to get a building permit" (unverified as worded; an official source confirms only that Silver Sa'fa is mandatory for new buildings).

### Abu Dhabi
- **Estidama Pearl Rating System (DMT).** All new projects must reach at least **1 Pearl** to get planning and permitting approval. Government-funded buildings, as defined in Information Bulletin #1, need at least **2 Pearls**. 1 Pearl means every mandatory credit is met. 2 to 5 Pearls also need credit points.
  Source: https://pages.dmt.gov.ae/en/Urban-Planning/The-Pearl-Rating-System-for-Estidama
  - "Villas must reach 2 Pearls" (unverified; found in one secondary source only).

### Federal (all emirates)
- **AC energy-efficiency labelling (MoIAT, formerly ESMA).** Cabinet Resolution No. (157) of 2025 sets the technical regulation for AC energy efficiency. It lists **UAE.S 5010-1:2025** for low-capacity (household) units and **UAE.S 5010-5:2025** for large-capacity units. Labels use a **1 to 5 star** scale, where 5 is the most efficient, and products are certified under the **ECAS** scheme or the voluntary **EQM** scheme.
  Sources: https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/3862/download ; https://www.tuv.com/market-access-services/en/certification-filter/uae-moiat-energy-efficiency-standardization-labeling-(eesl).html
  - The 2025 star bands and minimum efficiency (MEPS) values are unverified. Older 2014 bands (split units: 5 stars from EER 9.01) are superseded. Do not quote them as current.
    Source for the 2014 bands: https://www.iea.org/policies/6901-uaes-5010-12014-household-air-conditioners
- **Refrigerants.** Decree No. 138 of 2023 regulates **HFCs**, with a progressive phase-down that reportedly aims at a full ban by 2040 (law-firm summary).
  Source: https://hadefpartners.com/news-insights/insights/road-to-cop28-another-important-step-towards-phasing-out-the-use-of-hydrofluorocarbons-hfcs-in-the-uae/
  - A UAE ban on importing R-22 (HCFC) equipment, its date, and any technician F-gas licensing scheme are all unverified. The UAE is a Montreal Protocol party that is phasing out HCFCs.

### Other emirates (Sharjah, Ajman, RAK, UAQ, Fujairah)
- Each municipality issues its own building permits. Their HVAC and plumbing code adoption, and any green-building rules, are unverified in this digest. Tell the buyer to confirm with their municipality.

## 3. Category notes

### water-tank-cleaning
- In Dubai, tank cleaning must be done by **companies approved by Dubai Municipality**. DM vets them on staff training, equipment and cleaning procedure. A buyer can ask for proof of approval, and DM publishes a list of approved cleaning and disinfection companies.
  Sources: https://gulfnews.com/uae/environment/new-rules-to-ensure-water-tanks-are-kept-clean-1.1004260 ; https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/housing/dubai-how-do-i-know-that-my-homes-water-tank-is-clean-1.1655883123621
- Frequency: tanks should be cleaned and disinfected **at least twice a year**, per the Dubai Food Code 2020 as reported in the press (unverified against the code text).
- A lab test of the water after cleaning, and before-and-after photos, are good practice (unverified as a legal requirement).

### swimming-pools
- **Dubai, public pools.** Local Order No. 11 of 2003 makes the owner or manager responsible for health and safety conditions, including safety equipment and a qualified lifeguard. Local Order No. 2 of 2012 amended this, and the full-time lifeguard duty does not apply to pools in residential buildings or residential compounds.
  Source: https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2012/Local%20Order%20%282%29%20of%202012_Final_amended.html
- From 2017, lifeguard certificates had to come from training providers accredited by EIAC (press report).
  Source: https://gulfnews.com/business/tourism/certified-lifeguards-must-for-pools-in-dubai-by-july-1.1968489
- Rules on children's use of hotel pools changed over time and the current rule is unverified. Rules on pool water-quality parameters are also unverified.

### hvac
- Equipment must carry the MoIAT energy label. Building design must meet the DBC and Al Sa'fat (Dubai) or Estidama (Abu Dhabi).
- Refrigerant type matters. HFC use is being phased down (see §1).

### plumbing, mep, combined-mep
- In Dubai, design must comply with the DBC, and DEWA approval is needed for water and electricity connections. Al Sa'fat sets water-efficiency expectations for fixtures. Specific flow-rate limits are unverified here.

### insulation (thermal)
- Thermal-envelope and duct or pipe insulation requirements sit in Al Sa'fat and the DBC (Dubai) and in Estidama (Abu Dhabi). Specific U-values or R-values are unverified here. Ask the consultant for the project's compliance sheet.

## 4. Glossary (brief)
- **EER / SEER / CSPF**: cooling efficiency ratios. Higher is better. UAE labels convert these into stars.
- **TR (ton of refrigeration)**: about 3.517 kW of cooling. Used in UAE HVAC quotes.
- **BTU/h**: the unit for split-unit capacity. 12,000 BTU/h is 1 TR.
- **VRF/VRV**: a variable refrigerant flow multi-split system.
- **FCU / AHU / FAHU**: fan coil unit / air handling unit / fresh-air handling unit.
- **Chiller / district cooling**: central chilled-water plant. Many Dubai and Abu Dhabi towers buy chilled water from a district cooling provider.
- **MEP**: mechanical, electrical, plumbing.
- **PPR / uPVC / CPVC / HDPE**: common pipe materials.
- **GRP tank**: a glass-reinforced plastic sectional water tank, common on UAE rooftops.
- **Silver/Gold/Platinum Sa'fa**: Dubai green tiers. **Pearl 1 to 5**: Abu Dhabi Estidama tiers.
- **ECAS / EQM**: the UAE conformity schemes (mandatory and voluntary).

## 5. What a buyer should check in a quote
1. Trade licence activity matches the work, and the licence is valid.
2. For AC supply: brand, model, capacity (TR or BTU/h), **star rating and MoIAT/ECAS label**, refrigerant type, and warranty (compressor warranty stated separately).
3. Whether design or shop drawings and authority submissions (DM, DEWA, Trakhees, DMT, Civil Defence) are included, or are the client's responsibility.
4. Whether the quote states the green-building tier the work must meet: Silver Sa'fa as the Dubai minimum, or 1 Pearl as the Abu Dhabi minimum (2 Pearls for government buildings).
5. A materials schedule (pipe type, insulation thickness and type, duct class), along with testing and commissioning, and handover documents.
6. Water tank cleaning: proof of DM approval (Dubai), the scope (draining, disinfection, lab test, photos), and the visit frequency.
7. Pools: the equipment list, the water-treatment system, and who supplies the lifeguard or safety equipment. For public pools this is the owner's duty under the Dubai Local Orders.
8. Exclusions, payment terms, programme dates, and the defects-liability period.
