Federal level:
- **MoIAT** (Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology) runs **ECAS**, the mandatory conformity scheme for regulated products. Low-voltage electrical products, LV cables, lighting, RoHS, energy-efficiency labelling and Ex equipment all sit under it. **EQM** is MoIAT's quality mark. Sources: https://www.sgs.com/en-ae/news/2024/06/pca-2024-q2-navigating-uae-product-conformity-understanding-ecas-and-eqm-certification ; https://www.tuvsud.com/en-ae/services/product-certification/moiat-ecas-product-certification-services-in-uae (both are notified-body pages, not MoIAT itself).
- **TDRA** (Telecommunications and Digital Government Regulatory Authority, formerly TRA) handles type approval of telecom and radio equipment. Source: https://www.tuv.com/market-access-services/en/certification-filter/united-arab-emirates-telecommunication-apparatus-(tdra)-approval.html (secondary). The TDRA site itself was not reached.

## 2. Contractor and consultant approvals

**Dubai (DEWA)**
- The 2017 Regulations contain a section titled "DEWA enrolment for consultants and electrical contractors". DEWA also publishes separate circulars on enrolment/registration of consultants and contractors and on their authorised engineers. Electrical work that connects to DEWA must be designed and installed by DEWA-enrolled firms. https://dewa.gov.ae/en/builder/useful-tools/dewa-circulars
- Private 11 kV substations need a firm on DEWA's separate approved list for private 11 kV substation contractors. https://www.dewa.gov.ae/-/media/Files/PVT/Mandatory-requirements-for-enlisting-as-DEWA-approved-Private-11KV-Substation-Contractors.ashx
- Claims of contractor "grades" or a "DEWA competency assessment" come from exam-prep sites (unverified).

**Abu Dhabi (TAQA Distribution under the DoE)**
- TAQA Distribution assesses and licenses electrical contractors for installation work in buildings across the emirate, acting under DoE authority. It publishes a list of certified contractors and a drawing-submission checklist. https://addc.ae/en-US/distribution/Pages/Contractors.aspx
- The approval process, Revision 2 dated 1 April 2023 and approved by the DoE, implements Regulation 3.4 of the EWR. It requires consultants who design installations, and Solar PV integrators, to hold a Competency Certificate from the Distribution Company. https://www.addc.ae/en-US/distribution/Documents/Process%20For%20Approval%20of%20Licened%20Contractors.pdf
- That licensing covers LV work only. HV work is referred to the distribution company (stated in the 2015 process; current position unverified).

**Sharjah (SEWA)**
- Projects are submitted to SEWA and approved before site work starts, and supply is not given unless the rules are fully met. New connections, load increases and additional meters need the trade licence of a SEWA-approved electrical contractor. Sources are secondary: https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/sewa-connection-sharjah . The SEWA registration process and fees were not found (unverified).

**Northern Emirates (EtihadWE)**
- Distributed solar must be done by an "Approved Contractor", meaning a system integrator authorised by EtihadWE (definition in the official Connection Conditions). The process for general electrical contractor approval was not found (unverified).

## 3. Solar PV and net metering

**Dubai: Shams Dubai (DEWA)**
- Residential and commercial PV owners may export surplus to the grid under net metering, with the surplus offset at the same price as consumption. https://www.dewa.gov.ae/en/about-us/media-publications/latest-news/2015/11/dewas-shams-dubai-initiative-to-generate-long-lasting-renewable-energy
- **Only firms enrolled with DEWA as Solar PV consultants or contractors may submit applications.** Their staff must pass DEWA's PV expert training and certification. DEWA publishes the enrolled list, which was updated 18 Jul 2025 at the time of search, and disclaims responsibility for listed firms' work. https://www.dewa.gov.ae/-/media/Files/Consultants-and-Contractors/Enrolled-Solar-PV-Contractors-and-Consultants.ashx
- DEWA certified 1,115 specialists and enrolled 111 companies for Shams Dubai in 2024. https://www.dewa.gov.ae/en/about-us/media-publications/latest-news/2025/06/dewa-certified
- Sequence: Solar NOC, then design approval, then installation, then DEWA inspection and connection. This order is reported by secondary sources (partly unverified).
- Reports that DEWA restricted net metering for some segments: https://www.enerdata.net/publications/daily-energy-news/dubai-uae-restricts-net-metering-scheme-solar-pv.html (secondary; current scope unverified, so check with DEWA).

**Abu Dhabi**
- The Small-Scale Solar PV Energy Netting Regulation has been in effect since January 2017. It covers systems up to 5 MW per premises connected to the LV network. Surplus kWh are credited and carried forward rather than paid in cash. Projects need municipal permits and regulator approval before connection. https://pv-magazine.com/2017/06/21/abu-dhabi-releases-safety-standards-for-pv-under-net-metering ; https://pv-tech.org/abu-dhabi-establishes-net-metering-for-small-scale-solar (trade press; primary text not retrieved, so the exact eligibility is unverified)
- PV integrators need a TAQA Distribution Competency Certificate (see section 2).

**Northern Emirates (EtihadWE Distributed Solar Systems)**
- The Connection Conditions are issued under Federal Decree-Law No. 17 of 2022. Phase 1 covers residential, industrial and agricultural customers. Monthly excess export is credited for use within the same year. https://www.etihadwe.ae/Documents/dss/Distributed%20Solar%20systems%20Conditions%20_%20EtihadWE_Final.pdf
- Vendor sources claim a 20 MW aggregate cap and that credits are forfeited at 31 December (unverified).

**Sharjah**
- No official SEWA solar or net-metering text was found (unverified).

## 4. EV chargers

- **Dubai:** Supreme Council of Energy Directive No. 1 of 2017 requires DEWA approval before any public or private body installs, operates or maintains an EV charging station. DEWA coordinates with Dubai Municipality and RTA on standards. https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/new-rules-for-electric-car-chargers-1.1986003 ; https://rise.esmap.org/data/files/library/united-arab-emirates/Energy%20Efficiency/UAE_installation%20of%20electric%20vehicle%20charging%20stations%202017%20in%20Dubai.pdf
- Permit fees (AED 5,000-20,000) and the claim that DC chargers above 22 kW need a dedicated supply application come from a commercial blog (unverified).
- Other emirates: no official EV-charger approval text was found (unverified). Assume a utility load application is needed.

## 5. Generators

- No emirate-level generator-specific code was retrieved. Common practice is that a standby generator feeding a building installation needs a changeover or interlock that prevents back-feed into the utility network, approved through the utility's drawing submission. This is a general principle of the BS 7671-based codes (unverified for the specific clause).
- Diesel storage, exhaust and noise are typically governed by Civil Defence and municipality rules (unverified, not researched here).

## 6. Product conformity buyers ask about

- **ECAS Certificate of Conformity (CoC)** is required before regulated products are imported or sold. CoCs are valid for **1 year** with annual renewal. **EQM** certificates are valid for **3 years**. For LV equipment, IEC CB-scheme test reports can support the file. English and Arabic manuals and rating labels are part of the file. Source: SGS notified-body pages above.
- **TDRA type approval** applies to equipment that connects to a public network or has an intentional radio transmitter: Wi-Fi access points and routers, Bluetooth and Zigbee/Z-Wave hubs, DECT, RFID, GPS trackers. It is held by the registered manufacturer, importer or distributor before sale. Secondary sources say the certificate is valid for 3 years, the dealer registration for 5 years, and the TDRA mark goes on the packaging (unverified on tdra.gov.ae). Source: TÜV pages above.
- Smart-home and AV kit that is wireless (smart switches, Wi-Fi cameras, wireless mics, soundbars with Bluetooth) therefore typically needs **both** ECAS (as an electrical product) **and** TDRA approval (as radio equipment). Whether a specific product needs both is unverified per product.

## 7. IT networking and structured cabling

- TDRA regulates telecom equipment and the licensed operators (e&, du). Building telecom-readiness specifications, such as in-building fibre and telecom room requirements, are set with the operators and building authorities. The specific current document was not retrieved (unverified).
- No UAE-mandated structured-cabling standard was confirmed. Specs commonly cite ISO/IEC 11801 or TIA-568 categories (Cat6/Cat6A, OM3/OM4, OS2). This is industry practice, not a verified UAE mandate.

## 8. Glossary (brief)

- **NOC**: No Objection Certificate from an authority. It must be issued before design approval or work starts.
- **DB / SMDB / MDB**: distribution board / sub-main DB / main DB.
- **RCD / RCBO / MCB / MCCB / ACB**: residual-current device / RCD + overcurrent breaker / miniature, moulded-case and air circuit breakers.
- **ATS / AMF**: automatic transfer switch / automatic mains failure panel (generator changeover).
- **kVA vs kW**: apparent vs real power. Generators are rated in kVA at a stated power factor (usually 0.8). Ask for prime vs standby rating.
- **kWp**: peak DC capacity of a PV array. **Inverter kW (AC)** is often lower than kWp.
- **Net metering / energy netting**: exports offset imports on the bill. Credits are not cash (see section 3 per emirate).
- **ECAS CoC / EQM**: MoIAT product conformity certificate / quality mark.
- **TDRA TA**: TDRA type approval for radio and telecom devices.
- **Cat6A, OM4, OS2**: copper and fibre cable grades. **PoE**: power over Ethernet.
- **As-built drawings**: final drawings submitted for utility inspection and handover.

## 9. What a buyer should check in a quote

1. The contractor's **enrolment or licence with the right utility** for the emirate: DEWA (Dubai), TAQA Distribution (Abu Dhabi), SEWA (Sharjah), EtihadWE (Northern Emirates). For solar in Dubai, check the firm is on DEWA's **Enrolled Solar PV Contractors and Consultants** list. In Abu Dhabi, check for a Competency Certificate.
2. **Who obtains the approvals**: NOC, drawing or design approval, inspection, meter or connection. Check these steps and their fees are in the price.
3. The **code the design follows**, named by edition: DEWA 2017 Regulations, DoE EWR 2020 or SEWA 2019.
4. **ECAS/EQM certificates** for breakers, cables, fittings, luminaires and inverters, and **TDRA approval** for any wireless or networked device.
5. **Brand and model** for every major item, such as panels, inverter, generator set, switchgear and access points. Avoid "or equivalent" with no approval mechanism.
6. **Ratings** stated: generator kVA prime/standby, PV kWp and inverter kW, breaker kA breaking capacity, cable sizes, and the cable category.
7. **Testing and handover**: test certificates, as-built drawings, warranties (panel and inverter warranties are separate), and O&M manuals.
8. **Exclusions**: civil works, utility fees, load-increase charges, scaffolding, and roof or structural certification.
