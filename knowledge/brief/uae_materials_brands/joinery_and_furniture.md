## upholstery, acrylic, job-shop-manufacturing

This pass found no UAE-specific brand source for these categories. Describe the material by spec, not by brand:
- upholstery: foam density and fire-retardant rating, plus the fabric's rub count (Martindale);
- acrylic: sheet thickness, and whether it is cast or extruded.

Public buildings must meet fire-performance rules under the UAE Fire and Life Safety Code. See ../uae_regulations/fire_safety_and_security.md.

## What to ask / what a good quote names
- The board brand, type, thickness and decor code for every carcass, shelf and door, plus the edge-band thickness.
- The hardware brand and model for each item (hinge, runner, lift), not just "soft-close hardware".
- Whether each board and hardware item is local stock or imported, and its lead time.
- Moisture-resistant board for wet areas (sink base, vanity), named on its line.
- The finish (laminate, lacquer, veneer), plus the worktop's supplier, material and thickness.
