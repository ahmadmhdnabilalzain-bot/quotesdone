## What to ask / what a good quote names
- The maker and product name of the system or glass, not just "aluminium windows" or "double glazing".
- The glass build-up (e.g. 6 mm tempered + 12 mm air + 6 mm), coating and safety class.
- The steel grade with its mill certificate, and the stainless grade (316 near the sea or a pool).
- The elevator brand, model, load and speed, and the maintenance term after handover.
- Which items are local stock and which are imported, with the lead time for each.
