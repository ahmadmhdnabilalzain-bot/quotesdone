## it-networking, av-systems

This pass gathered no source on brand distribution in the UAE for these categories. Describe the products by spec instead:
- it-networking: cable category (Cat6/Cat6A), LSZH jacket, switch port count, PoE budget, and the access points' Wi-Fi standard;
- av-systems: display size and brightness.

Name a brand only once the supplier proves it is an authorised source.

---

## What to ask / what a good quote names
- The brand, model and quantity of every camera, recorder, panel and detector.
- For fire equipment: the UAE Civil Defence approval of each product and the installer's Civil Defence licence.
- Days of recording, storage size and camera resolution, named on the quote.
- Cable type and length, and who programs, tests and commissions the system.
- The warranty term, and who services the system locally.
