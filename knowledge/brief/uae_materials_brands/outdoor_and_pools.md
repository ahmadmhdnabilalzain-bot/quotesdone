## What to ask / what a good quote names
- The brand and model of the pump, filter, heater or chiller and dosing system, with flow and power.
- The filter media and the turnover time, plus who supplies the chemicals and their registration.
- The irrigation controller's brand, model and zone count, and the emitter type for each bed.
- Plant species and sizes (pot size or height), and how long dead plants are replaced under warranty.
- Which equipment is local stock and which is imported.
