## movers, general-services-other

This pass gathered no brand source, and these services do not need one. Describe the job by spec: packing materials (boxes, bubble wrap, wardrobe cartons), vehicle size, crew size and insurance cover.

## What to ask / what a good quote names
- For pest control: the products used, their MOCCAE registration, and the company's Dubai Municipality approval.
- For pest control: the re-entry time, the number of visits and the warranty period.
- For cleaning: the machines and chemicals by brand, the crew size and the hours.
- For facade cleaning: the access method, the crew's certification and the third-party liability cover.
- For movers: the inventory list, packing materials, insurance, and who dismantles and reassembles furniture.
