# UAE materials and brands: electrical, power and smart home

Scope (QuotesDone category codes): electrical, generators, solar, smart-home.

Compiled 2026-10-13 by Claude for the sales_engineer seat.

**Agents: cite this as general guidance; availability changes — confirm with the supplier.**

All sources accessed 2026-10-13. "(unverified)" means the claim could not be confirmed from a source in this pass and must not be stated as fact. Listing a brand is not an endorsement and is not a ranking. No prices on purpose. Lead times are typical UAE ranges, not commitments.

Key source: Dubai Municipality electrical vendor list (2026), https://dmpmedia.dm.gov.ae/uploads/2026/07/Electrical_Vendors.pdf — a DM project vendor list, not a general approval of every product of a brand.

| Breakers, DBs, wiring devices | Schneider Electric, ABB, Siemens, Eaton (DM vendor list for switchgear; retail availability unverified) | IEC 60898 / 60947 breakers; BS 1363 sockets (unverified) | Stock 0-1 week (unverified) |

What to ask / what a good quote names:
- Brand and exact model or part number for every cable, breaker and panel; cable size, cores, insulation, armour, voltage grade.
- Country of manufacture and the utility approval it relies on (DEWA, ADDC/AADC, SEWA, EtihadWE) for the emirate of the site.
- Whether panels are type-tested and who the panel builder is.
- Warranty period and the UAE agent who honours it.
- Stock or order item, with a delivery date.

- Engine and alternator brand and model; kVA prime and standby rating; fuel use at 75% load.

- Warranty (hours or months) and the local service dealer.
- For rental: rate basis, who refuels, cabling, remote monitoring.

What to ask / what a good quote names:
- Module and inverter brand and exact model, each on the current DEWA eligible list (Dubai) or the other emirate's equivalent (unverified).
- System kWp, inverter kW, mounting structure material, DC/AC cable sizes.
- Module product and performance warranty years; inverter warranty years.
- The DEWA-accredited contractor or consultant who will submit.
