# UAE materials and brands: HVAC, plumbing and MEP

Scope (QuotesDone category codes): hvac, plumbing, mep, combined-mep, insulation, water-tank-cleaning.

Compiled 2026-10-13 by Claude for the sales_engineer seat.

**Agents: cite this as general guidance; availability changes — confirm with the supplier.**

All sources accessed 2026-10-13. "(unverified)" means the claim could not be confirmed from a source in this pass and must not be stated as fact. Listing a brand is not an endorsement and is not a ranking. No prices on purpose. Lead times are typical UAE ranges, not commitments.

---

## hvac

| Product type | Brands with a UAE source | Standards / specs mentioned | Typical UAE lead time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Split, ducted, VRF, chillers, AHUs | Daikin, Middle East HQ and AHU factory in JAFZA (https://www.daikinmea.com/en_us/press-releases/daikin-middle-east-and-africa-relocates-dubai-regional-hq.html); Zamil Air Conditioners, Saudi maker with UAE distribution via Elapco reported in an older source (https://gulfconstructiononline.com/Article/7815/ZAC_ties_up_with_Elapco_in_the_UAE) | Capacity in TR or kW; UAE energy-efficiency label (star rating) for room ACs (unverified); refrigerant (R32 / R410A) | Splits 0-1 week from stock; VRF and chillers 6-12 weeks (unverified) |
| Pre-insulated ductwork | Kingspan PalDuct, made in Dubai (https://globalinsulation.com/news/1131-kingspan-starts-producing-palduct-hydro-system-in-uae) | Panel thickness or sheet gauge; SMACNA (unverified); fire rating where Civil Defence requires it (unverified) | 1-4 weeks (unverified) |

What to ask / what a good quote names:
- Brand, model, capacity (TR or kW), EER/SEER or star label, refrigerant.
- Indoor and outdoor model pair; country of manufacture.
- Copper pipe size and insulation, drain, controls / thermostat model.
- Compressor warranty and full-unit warranty, and the local service agent.
- The heat-load basis used for sizing.
