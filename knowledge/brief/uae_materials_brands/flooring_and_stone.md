All sources accessed 2026-10-13. "(unverified)" means the claim could not be confirmed from a source in this pass and must not be stated as fact. Listing a brand is not an endorsement and is not a ranking. No prices on purpose. Lead times are typical UAE ranges, not commitments.

| Carpet and vinyl tiles | See interiors_and_finishes.md (carpets) | | |

What to ask / what a good quote names:
- Tile brand, collection, size, thickness, finish and slip rating; country of origin.
- Quantity with the wastage percentage stated.
- Adhesive and grout brand and class.
- Same batch/shade for the full quantity, confirmed in stock.
- Workmanship warranty.

|---|---|---|---|
| Natural marble and granite, engineered quartz, sintered stone | Glaze Granite & Marble, importer with showrooms in Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi (https://gulfnews.com/business/corporate-news/glaze-granite--marble-unveils-its-third-showroom-on-sheikh-zayed-road-1.1694690373363) | Stone name and quarry origin; thickness (18 / 20 / 30 mm); finish; sealing | Slabs in stock 1-2 weeks fabricated; special import 8-12 weeks (unverified) |

What to ask / what a good quote names:
- Stone name and origin (e.g. Carrara, Italy), selection grade, thickness and finish.
- Whether the client views and reserves the slabs before cutting.
- Edge profiles, cut-outs and sealing included.
- Fabrication and installation warranty.

| Epoxy and PU floor systems | Sika, epoxy resins made in Dubai for flooring (https://gcc.sika.com/en/media/media-releases/2020/sika-expands-operations-in-dubai.html); National Paints, floor coating division (https://www.coatingsworld.com/contents/view_online-exclusives/2015-10-14/company-profile-national-paints); Terraco, flooring products (source above) | System type (coating, self-levelling, mortar); thickness in mm or microns; anti-slip; chemical resistance | Stock 0-2 weeks (unverified) |

What to ask / what a good quote names:
- Brand and exact system name for primer, body coat and top coat.
- Total thickness, number of coats, finish and colour.
- Surface preparation (grinding or shot blasting) and moisture test.
- Cure time before use, and warranty years.
