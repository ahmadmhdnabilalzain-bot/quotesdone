What to ask / what a good quote names:
- Brand and exact product for primer, membrane and protection layer.
- Thickness or coats, laps, and the area (roof, wet area, basement, tank).
- Whether the applicator is manufacturer-approved; Polybit states up to 30 years of warranty on its ComboRoof system when applied by Roofcare (source above).
- Warranty years and who issues it (manufacturer or contractor).
- Flood test included or not.
