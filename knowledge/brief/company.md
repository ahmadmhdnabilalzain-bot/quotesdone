## 1. What QuotesDone is

- A UAE B2B **anonymous quotation marketplace** based in Dubai. A customer describes a job (text, voice,
  photo or drawing); the platform turns it into a priceable brief and matches it to vetted suppliers, who
  send **anonymised** quotes. The customer compares them and pays a **3% reveal fee** on the one they choose
  to unlock that supplier's identity and contact details. (FAQ "What is QuotesDone?"; CT§2)
- Operator: **QuotesDone Technologies**, a Sole Establishment licensed by the Dubai Department of Economy and
  Tourism (DET), Trader License **No. 1633275**, Dubai, UAE. (CT preamble, CT§20)
- QuotesDone is a **marketplace and facilitator only**: not a contractor, supplier, agent or guarantor, and not
  a party to the contract for the works. (CT§2, CT§8, SA§5)
- Coverage: all seven emirates. Saudi Arabia is planned, **not live**. (FAQ "What countries…")
- Trades: construction and built-environment services — fit-out and interiors, MEP (electrical, fire, HVAC,
  plumbing), aluminium and glass, joinery and furniture, flooring and tiling, gypsum, painting,
  waterproofing, landscaping and pools, signage, CCTV/security, smart home, solar, facilities, cleaning, pest
  control, movers, AC maintenance and more; new trades are added as suppliers register them.
  (FAQ "What industries…") Food & beverage is not a category. (src/content/categories.json `_source`)
- No advertising and no paid ranking: nobody can pay to rank higher. (FAQ "Can I advertise?", "How are suppliers ranked?")

## 2. How it works for a customer, step by step

1. **Sign up.** Requests are made only after sign-up: OTP-verified mobile and email, name, date of birth
   (under 18 is rejected), and the terms checkbox. (FI 51) WhatsApp, if a different number, is confirmed by
   OTP inside the sign-up. (FI 391, FI 328) The platform is for users aged 18+. (PP§11)
2. **Describe the job** by text, voice, photo, drawing, BOQ or short video. The engine asks a few
   tap-to-answer questions to fill gaps; the request goes out only when it is complete enough to price.
   (FAQ "What happens after I submit…", "What happens if information is missing?")
3. **Anonymity.** Suppliers never see the customer's name, phone, email, company or exact address — only the
   job and general area/emirate. A drawing is shared only after the customer's details on it are covered;
   a drawing that cannot be covered is not shared. (FAQ "Is my identity hidden?")
4. **Matching.** Only suppliers who can genuinely do the job, from anywhere in the UAE; usually a handful,
   capped. "A wrong match is worse than no match." Customers cannot pick or invite their own suppliers.
   (FAQ "How many suppliers…", "Can I invite my own supplier?")
5. **Quotes arrive** — realistically **3–5 working days**, notified as each arrives. Never promise a fixed time.
   (FAQ "How long does the process take?")
6. **Compare.** Side by side as Supplier A, B, C, normalised to the same brief: price, line items, inclusions
   and exclusions, lead time, validity, warranty, payment terms, rating, verification status and a
   price-confidence band. The strongest three are levelled into a **comparison report**, and **every other
   quote is listed too — the customer can choose any of them.** (FAQ "How are suppliers ranked?"; FI 51)
7. **Reveal.** The customer pays the 3% reveal fee (card or Apple Pay, via Stripe) and that one supplier's
   identity and contact details unlock; the supplier is notified to respond. (CT§4; FAQ "What happens after selecting…")
8. **Deal directly.** Price, scope and timing are then agreed between customer and supplier; QuotesDone is
   not a party and holds no money for the job (no escrow). (CT§8, CT§15)

Other customer rules (FAQ): edit an inquiry within **24 hours**; after that, create a new one. Cancel any
time before reveal at no cost. No quote revisions, no reopen. Declining every quote costs nothing. Budget
is optional; the engine never invents a price or a delivery date and is brand-neutral (no approved-brand
or approved-manufacturer list; products must meet the UAE approvals for the job).

## 3. How it works for a supplier, step by step

1. **Register** on the supplier portal: claim or create the company listing. (FAQ "How do I register?")
2. **The mandatory six** must be complete before any match: business name (licensed legal name), mobile
   (OTP-verified), email (verified), valid UAE trade licence, company profile (trades and emirates), signed
   Supplier Agreement. (SA§13; HWV)
3. **Verification** within **3 working days** of complete documents: licence number, issuing authority and
   expiry date are checked. No supplier is approved automatically. (SA§13; HWV)
4. **Receive matched RFQs** anonymised; ignoring an invitation carries no penalty. (FAQ "Can I decline invitations?")
5. **Quote** itemised: scope, line items with brand/model and specs, quantities, unit prices, materials,
   lead time, validity, warranty, payment terms, exclusions. A quote below the quality bar is held as a
   draft with coaching. Submitted quotes are final (no revision); a quote may be withdrawn before reveal.
   (FAQ "What information is mandatory?", "Can I revise quotations?", "Can I withdraw…")
6. **Validity.** A quotation is valid **30 days** from submission unless refreshed or withdrawn before
   reveal; the supplier is bound by a quotation revealed within validity. (SA§12)
7. **Win.** The supplier is notified the moment a customer pays to reveal it, and must **begin responding
   within 5 working days**. (SA§11, SA§33)
8. Every supplier covers the whole UAE and may cover several trades. (FAQ "Can I operate in multiple Emirates?")
9. Supplier contacts must never share or solicit phone, email, links, WhatsApp or social handles during
   anonymised stages. (SA§10)

## 5. Refunds, the silent-supplier ladder, and who decides

- A reveal is performed immediately on payment; the fee is then earned and **not automatically
  refundable; there is no self-service refund.** (CT§5)
- **The one refundable case: the revealed supplier does not respond.** The clock runs from the customer's
  payment without the customer doing anything: day 3 (working) QuotesDone chases the supplier, copying the
  customer; day 5 the case goes to **QuotesDone's CEO** for a final call; if the supplier still does not
  respond, **everything the customer paid is refunded** — QuotesDone deducts nothing. Banks take **7–14
  working days** to show it. Working days are Mon–Fri excluding UAE public holidays. (CT§5)
- **Every refund is decided by QuotesDone's CEO**, recorded against a refund case reference quoted to the
  customer. None is automatic. (CT§5; FI 1, FI 89)
- No refund where the supplier responded (by any reasonable means, incl. WhatsApp or phone), the customer
  already dealt with the supplier off-platform, the same supplier was revealed before, the customer dislikes
  the price or terms, or simply changed their mind. Non-waivable consumer rights are preserved. (CT§5, CT§11)
- The customer may copy **supplier@quotesdone.com** when writing to the supplier to put the attempt on record. (CT§5)
- **The non-responding supplier then pays:** (a) the exact amount refunded (the 3% fee), (b) the processor
  fee QuotesDone could not recover, (c) an **AED 500** administrative fee, and (d) **12% p.a.** simple
  interest (or the lawful maximum if lower) from the due date — payable within 7 days of notice; the supplier
  is not matched until settled. (SA§33; CF `nonresponse_admin_fee_aed`, `late_interest_pct_pa`)
  Note: older rulings (FI 90, FI 179) said AED 250 and 9%; the signed v15 figures above replace them.
- **Disputes between a customer and a supplier** are theirs to resolve; QuotesDone is not a party (CT§8(f)).
  Complaints about the platform: management@quotesdone.com or the Platform; ticket number tracked at
  quotesdone.com/complaints/status; acknowledged within **2 working days**, substantive response within
  **10 working days**; escalation to Dubai DET Consumer Rights (Dubai Consumer app) or the Ministry of
  Economy consumer hotline **800 1222**. (CT§18)
- Chargebacks after a reveal (other than a genuinely unauthorised transaction) breach the terms; a business
  customer also owes an AED 250 handling fee per dispute, a consumer only actual costs. (CT§15)

## 6. Liability — QuotesDone is an intermediary

- QuotesDone introduces and helps compare; any contract for goods, works or services is **solely between the
  customer and the supplier**, consistent with **Articles 252 and 265 of the Commercial Transactions Law
  (Federal Decree-Law No. 50 of 2022)**: an intermediary bears no liability for the performance of the
  contract it brings about or for the value and quality of what is supplied. (CT§8; SA§17)
- **QuotesDone is not liable for** (CT§8(a)–(f)): any supplier statement, licence, certificate or document,
  **including any that is inaccurate, misleading or untrue** (i.e. a supplier lying in a quote — FI 1155, FI
  1156); the contents of any quote (prices, specs, quantities, inclusions, exclusions, validity); the
  supplier's performance, quality, safety, delays, defects or warranties; payments, deposits or refunds
  between customer and supplier; injury, loss or damage caused by the supplier; any customer–supplier dispute.
- The customer's remedy lies against the supplier; QuotesDone will, on request and where the law allows, give
  the supplier details it holds and may act on the supplier's account. (CT§8)
- Cap if a court still holds QuotesDone liable: the greater of the reveal fees paid in the prior 12 months
  and **AED 1,000**. Not excluded: fraud or gross fault, harmful acts, death or injury, non-waivable consumer
  rights. (CT§9)
- Automated outputs (BOQs, estimates, comparisons, support answers) are for convenience, may contain errors,
  and are **not professional, engineering, legal or financial advice**. (CT§14, CT§23(a))
- QuotesDone does not guarantee any number, price or quality of quotations. (CT§14)
- **Customer due diligence:** check the supplier's licence covers your specific works, plus permits,
  insurance and references, before signing. Paying the reveal fee is **not an endorsement**. (DIS§4; CT§8)

## 7. Verification — what "verified" means, exactly

- Checked: the mandatory six, and on the licence its number, issuing authority and expiry date. An expired
  licence holds the supplier out of matching until a renewal is uploaded. (HWV; SA§13)
- **Not checked:** workmanship, financial standing/solvency, insurance (only an uploaded policy's expiry date
  is tracked), job-specific permits, or whether the licence covers the specific works. (HWV; DIS§3)

## 8. Anonymity and non-circumvention

- Anonymity is the core of the service. Customers must not seek to identify or contact a supplier off-platform
  before a paid reveal, nor use revealed identities to avoid the fee. A business customer who does owes the fee
  avoided plus the higher of AED 5,000 or 3× the fee; a consumer owes only the fee avoided. (CT§6)
- A supplier sharing or soliciting contact details during anonymised stages: AED 10,000 per incident; taking
  an introduced customer off-platform: the higher of AED 20,000 or 5× the fee, for 24 months. (CF
  `anonymity_breach_aed`, `circumvention_min_aed`, `circumvention_fee_multiple`, `circumvention_tail_months`; SA§10, SA§24)
- These sums are agreed compensation under the Civil Transactions Law (Federal Decree-Law 25/2025, Art. 340);
  a court keeps its power to reduce them. (CT§22; CF basis)

## 9. Privacy and retention

- Controller: QuotesDone Technologies; personal data processed under the UAE PDPL (Federal Decree-Law 45/2021).
  Data is never sold. Rights (access, correction, deletion, objection) via management@quotesdone.com;
  complaints may go to the UAE Data Office. (CT§10; PP§7–§11)
- A customer's contact details go to **one** supplier only, after a paid reveal for that supplier. (PP)
- Retention follows the 5-year tax-records rule (Cabinet Decision 74/2023, Art. 3) (PP§7; FI 1126):
  account data — while active + **5 years** after last activity; conversations (email, WhatsApp, portal and
  website chat) — while active + 5 years, and in any case 5 years from the end of the calendar year written;
  requests, drawings, BOQs, quotes, comparison reports, and transaction records — at least 5 years from the
  end of the calendar year created; supplier KYC — the relationship + 5 years.
- **Unfinished sign-up** (email, mobile verification or terms acceptance not completed within 30 minutes):
  **deleted in full after 30 minutes, nothing sent to it** — it is never a customer. (PP§7; FI 1181)
- Some decisions (matching, ranking, quote checks, assessing refund requests) are automated; a person affected
  may object and ask for human reconsideration. (PP)
- Marketing only with consent; transactional messages (OTPs, quote notices, payment confirmations) are not
  marketing. (CT§10)

## 13. How to answer (rules around the reasoning)

- Answer the actual question directly, in the customer's language, with the figure and the reason.
  Do not list five options when one answer is asked for. (FI 1196)
- Technical questions (e.g. "MDF vs plywood?") — answer like an expert from your own knowledge and the
  UAE regulations digest, then say the supplier proposes and prices the final specification. (FI 1196; FAQ "Can the Smart Engine recommend specifications?")
- "Compare these 3 quotes" — compare like-for-like on what the price buys (scope, inclusions, exclusions,
  quantities, specs/brands), completeness against the brief, lead time, validity, warranty and payment terms,
  flag gaps, hidden costs and low-spec substitutions, then give a clear recommendation and what to clarify.
  Never reveal or guess a supplier's identity before a paid reveal. (FI 1198; FAQ "Quality policy", "What is included in the comparison?")
- Never promise a turnaround, a price, an approval or a refund. Refunds and anything that moves money or
  changes policy are decided by QuotesDone's CEO. (CT§5; FAQ "What can't the Smart Engine decide?")
- Regulations: give general guidance with the authority's name; the authority's current text governs, and the
  supplier/consultant is responsible for code compliance and approvals. (FAQ "What regulations apply?")
