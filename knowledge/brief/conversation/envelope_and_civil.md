# Conversation playbook: Building envelope and civil works

**Categories:** waterproofing, roofing, general-building-contracting, masonry, demolition, scaffolding, engineering-and-architectural-consultancies

## Waterproofing (waterproofing)
- **Open:** Where is it: roof, bathroom/wet area, basement, swimming pool, balcony/terrace, water tank, planter? Is there an active leak now, or is this new work/prevention?
- **Then ask (in order, one or two at a time):**
  - Area in square metres (or length x width of each zone); number of bathrooms/balconies if several.
  - Villa, apartment, or commercial; floor level; who owns the slab above/below (neighbour, building management).
  - Active leak: where does water appear (ceiling stain, wall dampness, basement seepage, paint bubbling)? Only after rain, after showers, after AC use, or constant? Since when? Already repaired before, and by what?
  - Existing finish over the area: tiles, screed, gravel, bare concrete, old membrane. Keep or remove tiles? Who re-tiles afterwards (same supplier or separate)?
  - Membrane preference if any: bituminous torch-on sheet, liquid-applied (polyurethane, acrylic, cementitious), crystalline (basement/tank), PVC/TPO sheet, or "supplier to propose". Exposed (UV-resistant) or under tiles/screed?
  - Roof: insulation, screed to falls, protection layer? Count drains, upstands, AC plinths, penetrations.
  - Pool/tank: drained now? Potable-water certified product needed for drinking-water tanks.
  - Flood test wanted and leak detection before work?
  - Warranty years expected and whether a manufacturer-backed warranty is required, not only the applicator's.
  - Timeline, occupied or empty, working-hour limits.
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** surface type, tile condition, visible cracks, staining pattern, drain positions, rough area. **It cannot tell you:** the true water source, slab condition under tiles, membrane age, moisture in the screed. Ask the history questions; say a leak diagnosis (moisture meter, thermal camera, flood or dye test) is a site-visit job the supplier does before a fixed price.
- **Price drivers (spec decisions):** membrane system and thickness/layers; tile and screed removal and debris disposal; surface repair and priming; insulation; re-screeding to falls; re-tiling scope; detailing count (drains, penetrations, upstands); access/height and lifting materials to roof; diagnosis visit; flood test; warranty length and manufacturer backing; building-management NOC and permitted hours; summer heat limits on application times.
- **Misconceptions to correct:**
  - "Just coat over the tiles": usually fails at joints; most durable fixes remove the finish.
  - "The leak is above the stain": water travels along slabs and pipes; source may be elsewhere, even a plumbing leak, not the membrane. If it is a pipe, that is plumbing.
  - "Waterproofing lasts forever": exposed coatings degrade under UAE UV and heat; warranty years differ by system.
  - Balcony leaks in apartments often need landlord/building-management approval; the owners' association may be responsible for common roofs.
- **Complete quotable request =** location(s) and area each; property type and floor; leak yes/no with symptoms and history; existing finish and keep/remove; membrane preference or "propose"; insulation/screed/re-tile scope; detailing count; flood test yes/no; warranty years wanted; access and NOC status; timeline; photos.

## Roofing (roofing)
- **Open:** New roof, re-roof, or repair? What type: flat concrete roof, pitched tiled roof, metal sheet/sandwich panel roof (warehouse, carport, extension)?
- **Then ask:**
  - Roof area (or footprint) and building height/storeys.
  - Existing build-up and condition; leaks?
  - For metal roofs: span, sheet profile, single skin or insulated sandwich panel and panel thickness, colour, gutters and downpipes, skylights/translucent sheets.
  - For pitched roofs: tile type (clay, concrete, shingle), timber or steel structure, underlay.
  - Insulation and heat reduction priorities (cool-roof coating, reflective finish).
  - Access: crane, scaffolding, roof hatch.
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** roof type, sheet/tile type, visible rust or broken tiles, size cues. **It cannot tell you:** structure capacity or purlin condition. A new roof structure or added load (solar, plant) needs a structural engineer's check.
- **Price drivers:** material and gauge/thickness, coating/colour, insulation, structure (new purlins/steel), gutters, flashings, skylights, removal and disposal of old roof, height and lifting, safety/scaffold, municipality or master-developer permit for new or altered roofs.
- **Misconceptions to correct:** a new roof or covering on a villa usually needs a permit through the authority or developer; adding solar or plant needs a load check.
- **Complete quotable request =** roof type; new/re-roof/repair; area and height; material, thickness, profile, colour; insulation; gutters/skylights; removal yes/no; access; permit status; timeline; photos/drawings.

## General building contracting (general-building-contracting)
- **Open:** What are you building or changing: new villa, extension, annex, boundary wall, majlis, commercial fit-out shell, refurbishment? Do you have approved drawings and a permit, or do you need design too?
- **Then ask:**
  - Plot location (emirate, community/free zone) and plot/built-up area; number of floors.
  - Drawings status: concept, consultant-approved, authority-approved (DM, Abu Dhabi municipality, Trakhees, master developer).
  - Who is the consultant (engineering consultancy of record)? Is supervision included?
  - Scope boundaries: shell and core only, or finishes, MEP, landscaping, too. Turnkey or labour-and-material per package?
  - Finish level (standard, mid, premium) and any material lists or BOQ.
  - Utility connections needed (DEWA/ADDC/SEWA), Civil Defence (DCD) approvals.
  - Target start and completion; occupied neighbours; site access.
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** existing structure, site constraints, rough size. **It cannot tell you:** structural capacity, soil condition, approved setbacks. Those are decided by the structural engineer, soil test lab and the authority; the agent records status and proceeds.
- **Price drivers:** built-up area, structure type, finish level, MEP extent, design and approvals, soil investigation, site supervision, utilities, permits/NOCs (DM or local municipality, DCD, DEWA/ADDC/SEWA, developer NOC), site access and working hours, schedule.
- **Misconceptions to correct:** a contractor cannot legally build structure without an approved permit and a licensed consultant; "quote from a sketch" gives only a rough range, a firm price needs drawings or a BOQ.
- **Complete quotable request =** project type; location and authority; areas and floors; drawings/BOQ status; consultant named or needed; scope boundaries; finish level; utilities; permit status; timeline; drawings/photos.

## Masonry (masonry)
- **Open:** What is it: new block walls (internal partitions, boundary wall), stone cladding, brick feature, plaster, or repair of cracks?
- **Then ask:**
  - Length and height of walls (or area); thickness; hollow or solid blocks; load-bearing or partition.
  - Boundary wall: plot length, height, gate openings, footing needed, coping, finish (plaster and paint, stone, cladding).
  - Stone cladding: natural stone type, thickness, fixing method (adhesive or mechanical anchors), height.
  - Plaster: internal/external, finish type.
  - Cracks: width, location, growing or not.
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** wall type, finish, crack pattern. **It cannot tell you:** foundation adequacy or cause of cracking. Wide, diagonal or growing cracks need a structural engineer's assessment before repair.
- **Price drivers:** block type and thickness, height (scaffold), footings, plaster and finish, stone type and fixing, lintels/openings, debris removal, municipality/developer permit for boundary walls, community height rules.
- **Misconceptions to correct:** boundary wall height and design are usually set by the community or municipality; mechanically fixed stone is expected on high external walls; patching structural cracks hides, not fixes, the cause.
- **Complete quotable request =** element; dimensions; block/stone spec; load-bearing yes/no; finish; openings; footing; permit status; timeline; photos.

## Demolition (demolition)
- **Open:** Full building demolition, partial (one wall, extension), or interior strip-out? Villa, apartment, or commercial unit?
- **Then ask:**
  - Size: built-up area, floors, structure type (concrete, steel, block).
  - Walls to be removed: load-bearing?
  - What stays (slab, MEP, facade, neighbour walls)?
  - Hazardous materials suspected?
  - Debris disposal and site clearing; salvage items.
  - Utility disconnection status (DEWA/ADDC/SEWA, gas, telecom).
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** extent and finishes. **It cannot tell you:** whether a wall is structural or what is in it. A structural engineer decides on load-bearing removal; the authority issues the demolition permit.
- **Price drivers:** volume and structure, method (manual or machine), height, hazardous material handling, disposal and transport, protection of neighbours, utility disconnection, permits (municipality demolition permit, building-management NOC for interiors), working hours and noise rules.
- **Misconceptions to correct:** removing a wall inside an apartment still needs building-management approval and often an engineer; asbestos must be removed by a specialist approved contractor.
- **Complete quotable request =** scope type; area/floors; structure; what stays; hazardous material yes/no/unknown; disposal; utility status; permit/NOC status; timeline; photos/drawings.

## Scaffolding (scaffolding)
- **Open:** What is it for (painting facade, waterproofing, window installation, construction) and how high?
- **Then ask:**
  - Elevation lengths and working height; number of sides.
  - Type: cup-lock/tube-and-clamp, mobile tower, suspended platform/gondola.
  - Rental duration; erection and dismantling included; inspection tagging.
  - Ground conditions, footpath or road encroachment.
  - Loading (light access or material storage), netting/sheeting needed.
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** height, facade shape, obstacles. **It cannot tell you:** ground bearing or tie-in points. The scaffold contractor's competent person designs ties; road or footpath occupation needs RTA/municipality approval.
- **Price drivers:** area and height, system type, duration, netting, loading class, erection/dismantle, inspection certificates, transport, permits for public-area use, working hours.
- **Misconceptions to correct:** rental is charged by duration, so delays extend cost; tall or loaded scaffolds need a design and regular inspection.
- **Complete quotable request =** purpose; dimensions and height; type; duration; loading; netting; site access/ground; permit need; start date; photos.

## Engineering and architectural consultancies (engineering-and-architectural-consultancies)
- **Open:** What do you need: design for a new build or extension, authority approval drawings, structural assessment, MEP design, fit-out approval, or site supervision?
- **Then ask:**
  - Location and authority (DM, Abu Dhabi municipality, Trakhees, DDA, master developer).
  - Plot and built-up area, floors, use (residential, commercial, F&B).
  - Existing drawings available? As-built?
  - Disciplines needed: architecture, structural, MEP, fire (DCD), landscape, interiors.
  - Stages: concept, detailed design, permit, tender documents/BOQ, supervision.
  - Special studies: soil test, structural assessment, green building (Al Sa'fat/Estidama).
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** existing condition and scale. **It cannot tell you:** compliance. Compliance is decided by the authority; the consultant of record signs submissions.
- **Price drivers:** project size and use, disciplines, stages, number of authority submissions, supervision frequency, studies, schedule.
- **Misconceptions to correct:** in most emirates, permits are submitted by a licensed consultant, not the owner or contractor; design approval and supervision are separate scopes.
- **Complete quotable request =** service type; location and authority; areas and use; disciplines; stages; existing drawings; studies; supervision yes/no; timeline.
