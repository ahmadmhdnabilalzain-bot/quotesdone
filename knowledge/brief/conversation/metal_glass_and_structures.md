# Conversation playbook: Metal, glass and structures

**Categories:** aluminium, glass, steel, structural-steel-fabrication, garage-doors, elevators, signage, tents-and-shade-structures

## Aluminium (aluminium)
- **Open:** What are you after: windows, doors (sliding, hinged, folding), curtain wall/storefront, cladding panels, partitions, or replacement of old frames? How many openings?
- **Must-haves (asked in this order, one or two a turn, then the summary):** size: how many windows or doors and their sizes; system: the glazing (double, thermal break, acoustic); doors: the opening type (sliding, casement, tilt-turn); finish: frame colour; existing: removal of the old frames; location; timing
- **Then ask (in order, one or two at a time):**
  - Width x height of each opening (a list or schedule); villa, apartment or commercial; floor level.
  - Opening type per item: fixed, casement, sliding, lift-and-slide, tilt-and-turn, folding.
  - Thermal-break profile wanted (recommended for heat and AC load) or standard; profile series/system or "supplier to propose".
  - Glass: single or double glazed, tinted/reflective, low-e, laminated or tempered, acoustic needs.
  - Frame finish: powder-coat colour (RAL), anodised, wood-effect.
  - Insect mesh, locks, handles, child safety restrictors.
  - Replace existing: remove and dispose old frames, make good plaster/paint?
  - Coastal location (salt exposure) for finish and hardware grade.
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** opening style, current frame, approximate proportions. **It cannot tell you:** exact sizes or wall condition. Ask for measured sizes or say the supplier takes final site measurements before fabrication.
- **Price drivers (spec decisions):** profile system and thermal break, glass spec, sizes and weight, opening type, hardware grade, finish, mesh, removal of old units and making good, height/access (cradle, scaffold), facade approvals (DCD for cladding, developer/building-management NOC for facade change), installation working hours.
- **Misconceptions to correct:** changing window colour or style on a villa or tower facade usually needs community or building-management approval; cladding panels must meet fire-safety rules and DCD acceptance; cheaper single glazing raises heat gain.
- **Complete quotable request =** item list with sizes and opening types; property type and floor; thermal break yes/no; glass spec; finish colour; hardware/mesh; removal yes/no; access; approvals status; timeline; photos/drawings.

## Glass (glass)
- **Open:** What is the glass for: shower enclosure, balustrade/railing, partition, mirror, tabletop, window replacement, facade, skylight?
- **Must-haves (asked in this order, one or two a turn, then the summary):** size: the run length and height; system: glass type and thickness, framed or frameless; doors: the door type (hinged, sliding); performance: privacy, sound or heat control; permits: landlord or building drawings; location; timing
- **Then ask:**
  - Sizes of each panel; number of panels.
  - Glass type and thickness: tempered, laminated, tempered-laminated, low-iron (extra clear), frosted, tinted, smart glass.
  - Fittings: frameless with patch fittings/channel, framed, hinges and handles finish (chrome, black, brushed).
  - Shower: fixed panel, hinged or sliding door; tray or flush floor.
  - Balustrade: height, indoor or outdoor, top rail, base channel or standoffs, floor level.
  - Replacement: broken unit only, or the whole sash?
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** layout, fitting style, finish. **It cannot tell you:** exact dimensions or fixing substrate. Supplier measures on site before cutting; tempered glass cannot be cut after toughening.
- **Price drivers:** type and thickness, size, edge work and holes, fittings and finish, coatings, height/access, removal of old glass, safety certification (DM glass certification where required), installation.
- **Misconceptions to correct:** balustrades and overhead glass need safety glass (laminated/tempered as the code and consultant require); tempered glass cannot be altered once made; slight tint is normal in standard clear glass. Frameless partitions, glass doors and shower enclosures are toughened (tempered) safety glass, commonly 10-12 mm for frameless office partitions and 8-10 mm for showers; 6 mm ordinary (annealed) glass is not safety glass and is not used for them, so correct it before asking sizes. A frameless partition also needs a visible manifestation band. (Ledger 1200 scenario bank, 27 Oct 2026.)
- **Complete quotable request =** application; sizes and quantity; glass type/thickness; fittings and finish; location (indoor/outdoor, floor); removal yes/no; timeline; photos.

## Steel and structural steel fabrication (steel, structural-steel-fabrication)
- **Open:** What do you need: gates, railings, staircase, mezzanine, canopy/carport frame, warehouse structure, or supply of steel sections?
- **Must-haves (asked in this order, one or two a turn, then the summary):** size: the lengths and opening sizes; doors: sliding or swing, manual or automatic; finish: material and coating; existing: removal of the old one; permits: community rules; location; timing
- **Then ask:**
  - Dimensions (span, height, length), quantity; drawings available?
  - Structural or decorative? For mezzanines, stairs, canopies and buildings: is there a structural design and engineer?
  - Material: mild steel, galvanised, stainless (grade for coastal or indoor).
  - Finish: hot-dip galvanising, primer and paint, powder coat, colour.
  - Design details: pattern for gates/railings, automation for gates, decking (chequer plate, grating, concrete deck).
  - Site conditions: installation height, foundations/anchors existing or new.
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** style, approximate size, existing anchors. **It cannot tell you:** load capacity or connection design. A structural engineer designs and stamps anything carrying people or roof load; the authority approves it.
- **Price drivers:** steel weight and section type, grade, fabrication complexity, welding/connections, finish system, foundations, crane/height, installation, design and calculations, permits (municipality/developer for canopies, mezzanines), working hours.
- **Misconceptions to correct:** painted mild steel rusts quickly near the coast; galvanising or stainless is advised outdoors; mezzanines and canopies are structures needing approval, not furniture.
- **Complete quotable request =** item; dimensions/quantity; drawings or design need; material and grade; finish; details; foundation/install conditions; permit status; timeline; photos/drawings.

## Garage doors (garage-doors)
- **Open:** Garage door for a villa, or a roller shutter for a shop/warehouse? New or replacing/repairing an existing one?
- **Must-haves (asked in this order, one or two a turn, then the summary):** scope: a garage door or a roller shutter; size: the opening width and height; doors: manual or motorised; existing; location; timing
- **Then ask:**
  - Opening width x height; headroom above the opening and side room.
  - Type: sectional overhead, roller shutter, side-sliding, swing; insulated panels or single skin.
  - Material and finish: steel, aluminium, wood-look, colour; windows in panels.
  - Motorised or manual; remotes count; app control; battery backup for power cuts.
  - Safety: photocells, auto-reverse; wind-rated for exposed locations.
  - Repair: symptoms (noise, stuck, broken spring, motor dead), brand and age if known.
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** door type and condition, opening shape. **It cannot tell you:** headroom or wall fixing quality. Ask for measurements; supplier confirms on site.
- **Price drivers:** size, type, insulation, material/finish, motor and accessories, safety devices, removal of old door, electrical point availability, installation.
- **Misconceptions to correct:** sectional doors need headroom; roller shutters suit tight spaces; a motor needs a power point near the opening.
- **Complete quotable request =** type; sizes incl. headroom; material/finish; insulation; motor/remotes/backup; safety devices; removal yes/no; timeline; photos.

## Elevators (elevators)
- **Open:** New lift, modernisation of an existing one, or maintenance/repair? Villa home lift, residential building, commercial, or goods lift?
- **Must-haves (asked in this order, one or two a turn, then the summary):** scope: new, modernisation or maintenance; usage: villa, residential, commercial or goods; size: floors, stops and load; existing: the shaft; location; timing
- **Then ask:**
  - Floors/stops and travel height; number of lifts.
  - Load and passenger capacity; car size; wheelchair use.
  - Shaft: existing shaft dimensions, pit depth, overhead height; machine-room or machine-room-less; or need a self-supporting shaft (villa).
  - Drive type: traction, hydraulic, screw/platform lift (villa).
  - Finishes: cabin panels, doors (automatic sliding, swing), lighting, display.
  - Maintenance: contract scope (preventive, comprehensive), current issues, call-outs.
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** existing cabin, shaft space, finishes. **It cannot tell you:** shaft compliance or structural capacity. Shaft and structure are confirmed by the consultant/structural engineer; DM (in Dubai) or the relevant authority examines and certifies lifts; Civil Defence enforces maintenance.
- **Price drivers:** stops and travel, capacity, drive type, shaft works, finishes, door type, safety features, installation, inspection and certification, maintenance contract scope and duration.
- **Misconceptions to correct:** a villa lift still needs space for pit and overhead; lifts must be inspected and certified before use; a maintenance contract is required, not optional.
- **Complete quotable request =** new/modernise/maintenance; building type; stops and travel; capacity; shaft dimensions or need; drive preference; finishes; timeline; photos/drawings.

## Signage (signage)
- **Open:** What sign: shop front, building name, 3D letters, light box, pylon, wayfinding indoor, vehicle branding, or digital screen?
- **Must-haves (asked in this order, one or two a turn, then the summary):** scope: the sign type; size; finish: lit or not, and material; permits: municipality or landlord approval; location; timing
- **Then ask:**
  - Size (width x height), letter height, quantity; location on facade or ground.
  - Illuminated or not: front-lit, back-lit (halo), LED; operating hours.
  - Materials: acrylic, aluminium, stainless steel, ACP; colours and finishes; artwork file available (vector/logo)?
  - Mounting surface and height; access (cherry picker, scaffold).
  - Mall or developer guidelines; landlord/building-management approval.
  - Permit: outdoor signs need the authority permit (in Dubai, DM/RTA or the free-zone/master developer authority under Decree No. 6 of 2020).
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** facade, space, style. **It cannot tell you:** permitted size and position. Those are set by the permit authority and landlord guidelines; ask whether they have them.
- **Price drivers:** size, materials, illumination, structure/mounting, height/access, electrical connection, design work, permit application handling, removal of old sign, working hours (often night work in malls).
- **Misconceptions to correct:** outdoor signs cannot go up without a permit; mall shopfront signs must follow mall design rules; ask who handles the permit application.
- **Complete quotable request =** sign type; size/quantity; illumination; materials/finish; artwork status; location and mounting; access; landlord approval and permit status; timeline; photos.

## Tents and shade structures (tents-and-shade-structures)
- **Open:** Permanent shade (car parking, garden, pool, playground) or temporary tent (event, Ramadan, wedding)?
- **Must-haves (asked in this order, one or two a turn, then the summary):** scope: permanent shade or a temporary tent, and what it covers; size; finish: the material; fixing: how it is fixed (footings, base plates, ballast) and wind; permits; location; timing
- **Then ask:**
  - Area or dimensions; number of car spaces; height.
  - Type: tensile fabric (HDPE or PVC), pergola, cantilever, pyramid, sail, aluminium louvre; frame steel or aluminium.
  - Fabric colour, UV and fire ratings.
  - Foundations: soil, paving, roof slab mounting.
  - Temporary: event date, duration, AC, flooring, lighting, sides.
  - Wind exposure and coastal location.
- **Photo/drawing tells you:** site layout and surroundings. **It cannot tell you:** foundation or wind design. The supplier's engineer designs for wind loads; permanent shades usually need municipality or developer approval; temporary tents need a permit (DDA, municipality, DCD for events).
- **Price drivers:** area, structure type, fabric spec, frame finish, foundations, height, design calculations, permits, rental duration (temporary), extras (AC, lighting, flooring), installation and removal.
- **Misconceptions to correct:** shades are wind-loaded structures, not decoration; villa car-park shades often need community approval; event tents need permits even for short use.
- **Complete quotable request =** permanent/temporary; dimensions; type and fabric; frame; foundations/mounting; extras; dates/duration; permit status; photos.
