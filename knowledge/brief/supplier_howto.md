# Supplier how-to: quoting well on QuotesDone

compiled 2026-10-13 by Claude for the customer_support and sales_engineer seats

Speak to suppliers in English only. Quote the portal labels exactly as written below. Fees, the
anonymity rules and the penalties are in the company brief; this file does not change them.

## 1. Where things are in the supplier portal

- Main tabs: "Services", "Inquiries received", "Quotations submitted", "Messages", "Documents", "Insights".
- The "Account" menu holds "Profile", "Billing" and "Settings".
- "Services": tick every service the company does. A service that is not ticked is not confirmed, so
  inquiries that name it will not reach the supplier.
- "Settings": the WhatsApp number. Choosing "I do not use WhatsApp" sends those messages by email instead.
- The bell shows held quotes ("N quotes held for exact details") and document alerts. Each item opens the right tab.

## 2. How to quote well

1. Open "Inquiries received" and open the job by its Job No. Read the scope, the drawings (each page
   is shown with identity removed) and the bill of quantities (download it as a CSV).
   - If the BOQ was held back because it could not be proven free of the customer's details, price
     from the drawings and the scope.
2. Send the quote in one of two ways:
   - "Send your quotation to the sales engineer": attach the file with "Attach quotation" in the
     conversation. Accepted: PDF with text, Excel, Word, CSV or email, up to 10 MB.
   - "Or type it into the quote form".
3. The sales engineer reviews the quote straight away, by fixed rules, and lists "Adjustment",
   "Clarification" and "Your choice" points. Each open point stays open until the supplier answers it.
   Reply in the box, for example "validity 30 days" or "BOQ line 3 excluded — by others". Attach a
   new version with "Attach a revised file", or pick "Generate it for me" / "I will upload a new version".
4. When nothing is open, press "Submit to Job <number>". The customer sees the quote without the
   supplier's name until they pay to reveal it.

What the review checks, in order. Get these right first time:
- Every BOQ line is priced.
- Quantity and unit for each line match the BOQ.
- Every line that has a quantity also has a price.
- Line amounts add up to the total.
- Prices are in AED.
- The VAT basis is stated: includes VAT, excludes VAT, or not VAT-registered. A TRN is never asked for.
- Validity is concrete: a number of days or a date.
- Exact specs on every line: the material spec for the trade and for every component the customer
  named, a real delivery date or lead time, installation and warranty where they apply, and the price
  excluding VAT.

Vague answers count as missing. The system rejects "N/A", "TBD", "TBC", "standard", "equivalent",
"or equivalent", "approved equivalent", "as per …", "as required", "as approved", "to be confirmed",
"later" and "same". Write the brand, model, size, grade or number of days.

The review never judges whether a price is high or low, and never compares the supplier with other
suppliers.

Other rules:
- One quote can carry up to 20 options.
- Status labels in "Quotations submitted": "Held — not sent yet", "Sent to the customer", "Customer
  revealed you", "Withdrawn", "Expired".
- The quotation is issued as a standard PDF in English and Arabic. If the Arabic PDF is not ready
  yet, the English PDF can be opened now.
- A submitted quote cannot be revised. It can be withdrawn before a reveal. It is valid for the period your quotation states (QuotesDone sets none); if it
  states none, the customer reads "Validity: not stated by the supplier".
- "Why … was not chosen" on a quote shows the loss feedback.
- When a request is concluded, the portal says "Concluded — the customer has chosen."

## 3. Why a quote is held, and how to release it

A held quote is saved and is not sent to the customer. The panel reads: "On hold — the QuotesDone
sales engineer needs these exact details before this quote can reach the customer:" and then lists
each missing detail by "Line N", or under "Quote terms" for details that apply to the whole quote
(delivery, installation, warranty period and coverage, price excluding VAT).

There are two ways out:
- **"Fill in the missing details"**: the form asks only for the missing fields, with units and examples
  (e.g. delivery "21 days from approval", warranty "2 years"). Press "Save and regenerate my
  quotation". If anything is still missing, it says "Saved. These details are still missing:". Then
  read the regenerated quotation ("Your regenerated quotation — check every line", or open the PDF).
  Tick "I confirm this regenerated quotation is correct." and press "Approve and send to the customer".
  - If it says "Approved. The hold is released; the quote still needs its commercial terms before it is
    sent.", the supplier still has to add those terms.
- **"Upload a revised quote"**: this opens the quote form again. The new submission replaces the hold
  and is checked again.

Field errors and their fixes:
- "State the exact value": write the exact value.
- "Enter a number": write a number.
- "State a date or a number of days, weeks or months": give a date or a period.
- "Choose yes or no": pick one.

Other hold-related messages:
- "Your quote was saved as a draft. Add the missing details so we can send it to the customer."
- "Your quote needs the changes listed in the review before we can send it to the customer."

## 4. Contact details and identity in a quote

Until the paid reveal, the customer sees no identifying details. The system removes these from typed
and uploaded quotes, and each one becomes "[removed]":
- the supplier's own company name, including LLC / Trading / Contracting / Est / FZ-LLC forms;
- email addresses;
- websites and bare domains (.ae, .com and others);
- UAE phone numbers (+971, 00971, 05x mobiles and landlines);
- trade licence numbers and TRNs.

Letterhead names are also kept from the customer. Removed text is logged for review.

Tell suppliers:
- Do not type phone numbers, emails, links, WhatsApp, social handles or the company name into the
  scope, notes, conditions or exclusions. They are removed anyway, which wastes space in the quote.
- Sharing or asking for contact details before the reveal breaks the Supplier Agreement. The agreed
  compensation is in the company brief (AED 10,000 per incident).
- An account can be suspended for it ("Your account is suspended for sharing contact details
  off-platform."), and a chat can be closed ("This chat is closed due to a policy violation.").
- After a reveal: "A customer revealed your quote — their contact details are in Messages".

## 5. Trade licence and document expiry

- The trade licence is required before approval: "Upload your trade licence". Use the current licence
  from the licensing authority (Economic Department or free-zone authority).
- Upload it in "Documents". It is checked again automatically as soon as it is saved. The system reads
  the company name, the licence number and the expiry date.
- When a document is close to expiry, the warning says "Your trade licence expires in N days" (the
  same warning exists for the insurance certificate). When it expires, the account is paused
  automatically and no more requests arrive. Upload the renewed copy before that.
- After expiry: "Your account is paused — trade licence expired". Upload a valid copy and the account
  is restored automatically, usually within a day, at no fee. Insurance works the same way.
- Document hints in the portal: "{doc} has expired" means "Upload a renewed copy". "{doc} expires in N
  days" means "Renew it now so nothing pauses when it lapses."
- "Certifications" shows each document as "Valid", "Expiring", "Expired" or "Not uploaded", marks the
  ones "blocking your approval", and links to "Upload in Documents".
- The licence is held, and never approved, when the system cannot read it:
  - The company name, expiry date or licence number cannot be read: upload a clearer copy (a PDF, or
    a sharp photo of the whole page). It is read again as soon as it arrives.
  - The name on the licence does not match the registered company name: upload the company's own
    licence, or contact us if the registered name should change.
- Verification takes up to 3 working days once all documents are complete (company brief).

## 6. Upload rules

Every file is checked by its actual content type, then its size, then a malware scan. Files of any
other type are refused.

| Upload | Where | Types accepted | Size / count |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quotation file | Sales engineer conversation | PDF, Excel, Word, CSV, email (also images, CAD, zip by type check) | 10 MB per file |
| Trade licence | "Documents" | PDF, JPEG, PNG, WEBP | 25 MB (default limit) |
| Company profile, catalogues | "Documents" | PDF, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx) | 25 MB (default limit); catalogues add up, never replace |
| "Supporting company documents" | "Documents" | "PDF, image or office file, up to 25 MB each." | 25 MB each, 30 documents max |
| Product catalogue upload | "Choose a catalog file" | CSV, TSV, TXT, Excel, PDF, PNG, JPG, WEBP, DWG, DXF | Not stated in code |

- Supporting documents need a "Document name" and a "Type": "Certificate", "Accreditation",
  "Reference letter", "Project reference", "Financial statement", "Organisation chart", "HSE policy",
  "Quality policy", "Brochure" or "Other". Only the supplier sees them until they share them.
- The company profile is shown to customers only after a reveal.
- The code states no rule on logos or contact details inside photos, catalogues or supporting
  documents. Quotes and drawings are made anonymous; other files are not covered by that rule.
  Advise suppliers to keep logos and contact details out of anything a customer sees before a reveal.
- No limit is stated for the number of photos in a quote.

## 7. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Why | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Quote shows "Held — not sent yet" | Missing or vague per-line specs or terms | Open the hold panel, then use "Fill in the missing details" or "Upload a revised quote" |
| "Saved. These details are still missing:" | Some required fields are still empty or vague | Fill the fields listed, with exact values |
| Approved, but the quote was still not sent | Commercial terms are missing | Add delivery, warranty, validity, VAT basis and the price excluding VAT |
| Review keeps flagging lines | BOQ line unpriced, wrong unit or quantity, totals don't add up, not in AED | Fix each listed line, or reply e.g. "excluded — by others" |
| "We could not read that quotation automatically" | File could not be read | Type it into the quote form instead |
| "Could not read the file pages" | Blank or unreadable scan | Attach a clear PDF or photo |
| Text in the quote shows "[removed]" | Contact or identity details were scrubbed | Remove them; they are never shown before a reveal |
| "That file is larger than 25 MB." | Supporting document too big | Compress or split the file |
| Quotation file refused | Over 10 MB or a type that isn't allowed | Send a PDF/Excel/Word/CSV under 10 MB |
| "You have reached the limit of 30 documents." | Supporting-document cap | Remove one, then add the new one |
| "That file could not be accepted." | Wrong type or failed the scan | Upload a PDF, an image or an office document |
| "Your account is paused — trade licence expired" | Licence past its expiry | Upload the renewed licence in "Documents"; it is restored automatically, usually within a day |
| Not verified: company name, number or expiry not read | Licence copy is unclear | Upload a clear PDF or a sharp photo of the whole page |
| Not verified: name mismatch | Licence belongs to another name | Upload the company's own licence, or contact us |
| No inquiries for a service | The service is not ticked in "Services" | Tick it and confirm |
| "This request has been concluded" | The customer has already chosen | Nothing to do; wait for the next request |
| "A quote can carry at most 20 options." | Too many options | Combine options or drop some |
