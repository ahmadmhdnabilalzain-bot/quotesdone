# QuotesDone — Company Canon

The authoritative, grounded answer to any question about QuotesDone. Every agent should be able to answer from this. Grounded in the live product + config as of 2026-07-24.

## Identity

**What is QuotesDone?**

QuotesDone is a UAE B2B anonymous quotation marketplace. A buyer posts a request for quote (RFQ) describing a job or product, verified UAE suppliers submit quotes, and those quotes reach the buyer anonymised so the supplier's identity is hidden. The buyer then pays a small reveal fee to unlock and contact a chosen supplier.

**What is QuotesDone's vision?**

QuotesDone's vision is to become the smartest marketplace engine in the world. Everything the company builds and every department's work is oriented toward that goal.

## Anonymity

**Why is QuotesDone anonymous?**

Anonymity protects buyers and keeps competition fair. Because supplier identities are hidden until reveal, suppliers can't spam-call buyers, and incumbent suppliers can't retaliate against buyers who shop around for better prices. Buyers only share contact once they choose to engage a supplier.

**Can a supplier's identity leak through a quote?**

No. Supplier identity must never leak through a quote. Quotes are delivered anonymised, and the trust signals shown on a quote card are designed to be non-identifying so the buyer cannot work out who the supplier is before paying to reveal.

## Buyer Journey

**How does QuotesDone work for a buyer?**

A buyer signs in via OTP and completes a profile (name, phone, SMS-verified mobile, verified email, accepting terms). They post an RFQ through a conversational intake. Verified suppliers submit quotes which arrive anonymised, and the buyer pays a reveal fee to unlock a chosen supplier and contact them directly.

**How fast will I get quotes?**

Quotes come from verified UAE suppliers who respond to your RFQ. QuotesDone is pre-launch with no real transactions yet, so we don't advertise a fixed turnaround. Quote cards show response statistics so you can see how quickly suppliers tend to reply.

**What do I see on a quote before I pay?**

Before reveal, a quote card can show non-identifying trust signals: a licence-verified badge, years trading, the emirate, response statistics, and ratings from a six-dimension rating system. The supplier's identity and contact stay hidden until you pay the reveal fee.

## Fees

**How much does it cost to use QuotesDone as a buyer?**

Posting RFQs and receiving anonymised quotes is free; you pay only when you choose to unlock a supplier. You then pay the 3% reveal fee on the quote value (no cap), shown in full before payment: 3% of the selected quotation, nothing added for paying by card. QuotesDone is not VAT registered, so no VAT is charged on the reveal fee.

**What is the reveal fee?**

The reveal fee is what a buyer pays to unlock a chosen supplier's identity and contact. It is 3% of the quote value, with no discount and no cap. Nothing is added for paying by card: QuotesDone bears Stripe's fee itself (Cabinet Resolution 66/2023 Art. 5(3)). No VAT is charged on the reveal fee.

**Do you take commission on the work I win?**

No. QuotesDone takes no commission on won work. The only supplier-side charge is the non-response charge, and only if the supplier did not respond and the buyer was refunded: the exact amount refunded to the buyer (the 3% reveal fee), the processor fee QuotesDone could not recover, an AED 500 administrative fee, and simple interest at 12% a year from the due date (Supplier Agreement v15 clause 33; a supplier bound by an earlier version owes what it signed, clause 33: v13 AED 500 and 9% a year; v10 to v12 AED 250 and 9% a year, with the card processing fee in the refunded amount).

## Supplier Journey

**How does QuotesDone work for a supplier?**

A supplier registers and completes the mandatory six: the company's licensed name, a UAE mobile number verified with a code, a verified work email, the trade licence (uploaded), the company profile (uploaded) and acceptance of the Supplier Agreement. Once approved they receive relevant RFQs and submit quotes (uploaded and OCR-read, or as line items). Quotes are quality-scored and delivered anonymised; the supplier is contacted only after the buyer pays to reveal.

**How do I register as a supplier?**

Open the supplier portal, choose "Create account" with your work email, and complete the mandatory six: the company's licensed name, a UAE mobile number verified with a code, a verified work email, the trade licence (uploaded), the company profile (uploaded) and acceptance of the Supplier Agreement. The licence number, issuing authority and expiry date are read from the licence itself. Approval follows within 3 working days of complete documents. It is automatic once the six are complete, the licence is read and in date, and you have confirmed the services your company provides in the Services tab of your portal; until you confirm them you are held, and the portal says what is left. A licence issued by an authority outside the UAE, or by an authority we cannot confirm, is held too, and you are asked for a current UAE trade licence or to name the UAE authority or free zone that issued it.

## Verification

**How are suppliers verified?**

Every supplier completes the mandatory six before it can be matched: the company's licensed name, a UAE mobile number verified with a code, a verified work email, the trade licence (uploaded), the company profile (uploaded) and acceptance of the Supplier Agreement. The licence number, issuing authority and expiry date are read from the licence itself and checked (Supplier Agreement clause 13). This is what lets a quote carry a licence-verified badge.

**Do I need insurance or ISO certificates to join as a supplier?**

No. Insurance, ISO, manufacturer authorisation, EGR, and catalogues are all optional; they only widen reach and improve ranking. The two required documents are your trade licence and your company profile, both uploaded.

**How long does supplier approval take?**

Within 3 working days of complete documents (Supplier Agreement clause 13). Approval is automatic when the mandatory six are complete, the licence is read and in date, and the supplier has confirmed the services it provides (Services tab); a supplier is held until it confirms them. A licence issued by an authority outside the UAE, or by an authority that cannot be confirmed, is held, and the supplier is asked for a current UAE trade licence or to name the UAE authority or free zone that issued it. An expired licence must be renewed first, and a licence that cannot be read in full is held for review; the supplier is told what is pending.

## Matching

**How are suppliers matched to my request?**

Matching uses keyword shortlisting plus a capability-matching engine (behind a feature flag). It excludes any supplier whose matching is blocked, banned, or permanently closed, routing each RFQ to suppliers who can actually do the job.

## Quote Quality

**How does QuotesDone ensure quotes are good quality?**

Every supplier quote is scored for quality with 70 as the pass mark, and suppliers get coaching to improve. This raises the standard of quotes reaching buyers.

## Trust Signals

**What trust signals do buyers see on a quote?**

Pre-reveal, a quote card can show a licence-verified badge, years trading, the emirate, response statistics, and ratings from a six-dimension rating system. All are non-identifying; the identity unlocks only when the buyer pays the reveal fee.

## Refunds

**How do I get a refund if the supplier doesn't answer?**

If you paid to reveal a supplier, they have 5 working days from your payment to respond. QuotesDone chases them at 3 working days with you copied, and QuotesDone's CEO makes the final call at day 5. If they are still silent, everything you paid (the 3% reveal fee) is refunded; banks take 7 to 14 working days to show it.

**What is the refund window?**

The refund ladder runs 5 working days from your payment, with a chase at 3 working days and the CEO's final call at day 5. Don't confuse this with the 7 to 14 working days banks take to show a refund.

**How long does a refund take to be paid?**

After a refund is decided, banks take 7 to 14 working days to show it. This is separate from the 5-working-day ladder that comes before it.

**Who approves refunds?**

Every refund is decided by QuotesDone's CEO on the record; none is automatic. Once decided, banks take 7 to 14 working days to show it.

**What is deducted from a refund?**

Nothing. When a supplier does not respond, everything the buyer paid (the 3% reveal fee) is refunded. Separately, QuotesDone recovers from the unresponsive supplier the exact amount refunded, the processor fee QuotesDone could not recover, an AED 500 administrative fee, and simple interest at 12% a year from the due date (Supplier Agreement v15 clause 33; a supplier bound by an earlier version owes what it signed, clause 33: v13 AED 500 and 9% a year; v10 to v12 AED 250 and 9% a year, with the card processing fee in the refunded amount).

## Supplier Charges

**What happens to a supplier who doesn't respond after being revealed?**

If the supplier did not respond and the buyer was refunded, QuotesDone recovers from that supplier the exact amount refunded to the buyer (the 3% reveal fee), the processor fee QuotesDone could not recover, an AED 500 administrative fee, and simple interest at 12% a year from the due date (Supplier Agreement v15 clause 33; a supplier bound by an earlier version owes what it signed, clause 33: v13 AED 500 and 9% a year; v10 to v12 AED 250 and 9% a year, with the card processing fee in the refunded amount) (Supplier Agreement clause 33). This is the only supplier-side charge.

## Supplier Fees

**Is QuotesDone free for suppliers?**

Yes, currently. Registration is free for the first 1,000 suppliers, and AED 1,000 per year after that. Since everyone is still in the free tier, the fee is dormant and no supplier is charged to register.

**How much does supplier registration cost?**

Registration is free for the first 1,000 suppliers; after that it is AED 1,000 per year. Right now everyone is on the free tier, so the fee is dormant.

**Are there any other fees for suppliers?**

No. There is no commission on won work and registration is currently free. The only supplier-side charge is the non-response charge (the exact amount refunded to the buyer, the processor fee QuotesDone could not recover, an AED 500 administrative fee, and simple interest at 12% a year from the due date), and only if the supplier did not respond and the buyer was refunded.

## Licence Expiry

**What happens if my trade licence or insurance expires?**

An expired trade licence or insurance automatically pauses your matching, so you stop receiving RFQs until you renew. On renewal, matching auto-restores with no fee.

## Payments

**How does money move on QuotesDone?**

All money moves through Stripe, both ways. QuotesDone never holds a supplier's bank details. This applies to reveal fees paid by buyers and to any refunds or recoveries.

## Tech

**What technology is QuotesDone built on?**

QuotesDone is built on Supabase (Postgres plus Deno edge functions) with a Next.js operations console. The business database runs in the ap-southeast-1 region. Payments run through Stripe and supplier documents are read using OCR.
