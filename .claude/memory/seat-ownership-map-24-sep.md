The founder's seat ownership, given 24 Sep 2026. It is exact and must not be reinterpreted.

**Timing**
- No customer revenue before **January 2027**. Customers are launched only once the supplier base is strong enough to quote every inquiry.
- Oct to Dec 2026 is supplier registration ONLY. There are no customer-facing campaigns yet.

**What each seat owns**
- **sales_engineer** owns BOTH suppliers and customers. That covers the customer portal and the supplier portal, inquiries, drawings and documents, specs, matching, quotes, the comparison report and **reveals**. (Enterprise accounts were REMOVED on 30 Sep 2026: "we have customer accounts and supplier accounts, that's it.")
- **customer_support** is the COMMUNICATION seat. It:
  - handles the homepage chat for unregistered visitors;
  - owns the email and WhatsApp channels (it is the only seat with WhatsApp);
  - delivers every message in coordination with the other seats.

  It does NOT own reveals.
- **marketing** covers:
  - social media: posting, comments, insights;
  - SEO and GEO through the website blog;
  - trend-spotting related to the business;
  - much more, as a senior marketing manager would. Not just posts.
- **it_engineer** monitors everything on the platform, comprehensively.
- **ceo** acts like a visionary chief executive ("what Elon Musk does for Tesla"): strategy, direction, raising the bar, innovation, driving growth and holding every seat accountable. Not just monitoring.
- **Company knowledge** (the rules, targets and facts) is shared by ALL seats.

**Why:** the founder, 24 Sep 2026, rejected the first CEO plan: "your plan is shit... audit their tasks properly... if you're hiring a senior human for this role, what you will give him as tasks... I don't want you to simplify stuff."

**How to apply:** design every seat's duties from a full senior-human job description, then audit the code against it.

- monitoring Stripe payments at every reveal;
- reading the expenses mailbox where the founder forwards receipts;
- issuing the documents for refunds, charges and invoices;
- the books, reconciliation, VAT and tax.

It must be in the picture for anything to do with money.
