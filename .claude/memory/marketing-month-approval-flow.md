The monthly marketing calendar is approved in ONE step. The founder gets the calendar email, replies "approve", and it is scheduled. There is no separate go-live switch. Any other reply is routed to the marketing seat, which makes the requested adjustment and re-sends the calendar; alternatively he sorts it out in a Claude session.
**The monthly loop from November 2026:** the marketing seat proposes the next month's video ideas with a plan for each. The founder creates the content and shares it back. The seat builds the calendar (dates, captions, hashtags, links) and sends it for approval. October 2026 is already produced by the founder, so it only needs scheduling and captions.
**Supplier campaign:** a daily email, preferably from customer_support, with a PDF listing which suppliers will be contacted the next day. It is fully coded with no LLM.
**Why:** founder, 24 Sep 2026: "It should be one shot. I say approve... it's done."
Related: [[routine-work-is-code-not-llm]], [[do-not-ask-about-campaign-go-or-subscriptions]].
**Posting days (standing rule, 24 Sep):** never post on weekends (UAE weekend is Saturday and Sunday) or on UAE official public holidays, including the Eid and Hijri-calendar holidays. The scheduler skips them in code. The first calendar starts 25 Sep 2026 (a Friday, which is a working day), not October.
