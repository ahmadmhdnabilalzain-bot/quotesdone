When work can be done once, here in Claude Code on the founder's subscription, do it here and hand the result to the agent or seat, rather than paying OpenRouter for it. Examples: classifying leads, drafting templates, bulk sorting, and seeding knowledge or tables. Paid model calls are for live, runtime decisions only.

The supplier invitation campaign needs NO trade classification of the leads. The invitation is generic: "Dear <company name>, this is QuotesDone... join the first 1,000 for free lifetime membership". A lead's name (e.g. "X Joinery") already says what it does. The AED 2,540 lead re-sort is therefore unnecessary. Leave it off, and never propose a paid classification study of the leads.

The no-word-matching rule (890) is about the seats understanding documents, specs and services at runtime (quote review, a supplier's services, customer messages). It does not ban common sense in a simple one-off job like addressing an invitation.

**Why:** founder, 24 Sep 2026: "there's no need to do a study for an excel sheet that we have for leads" and "there are many tasks that you should be doing and handing it over to the agent instead of me paying LLMs. I have a subscription here. Why shall I pay twice?"
