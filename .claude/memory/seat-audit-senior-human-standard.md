Founder, 28 Sep 2026: *"An agent doesn't just do tasks… They need to help the company grow. That's why people have employees."* He added that when I say a seat is "good to go", he assumes it can handle anything, and then problems arrive the next day. My bar for "expert" was too low.

**Stage 1, qualifications.** Define what a senior human in the role knows and can do: credentials-equivalent knowledge, judgement and tools. Measure our agent against that list. Be able to answer "how senior is our marketing agent?" with evidence, e.g. "junior on X, mid on Y, senior on Z".

**Stage 2, contribution.** Imagine we hired that senior human at QuotesDone today. List what they would DO to grow the company: initiatives, analysis, proactive work, improvements, not only routine tasks. Check whether our agent does each one. Build what's missing.

**Why:** a task-runner agent isn't an employee, and the company can't run autonomously on task-runners.

**How to apply:**
- Run this audit for every seat.
- Report seniority honestly, per area.
- Never say "ready", "expert" or "good to go" without meeting this bar with evidence.
