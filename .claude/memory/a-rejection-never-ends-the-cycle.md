Founder, 29 Sep 2026, said about blogs and stated as the general rule: approvals and rejections work like the real world.

- The CEO gives reasons for every rejection and a proper logic for every approval.
- The originating seat works through those comments, drafts a new version and goes back to the CEO. This repeats until the item is approved.
- The CEO always approves what is good for the company.
- The founder is never in this loop. His one remaining approval is the monthly social calendar.

**Why:** a bare "rejected" is a dead end that a real company would never accept, and it wastes the work.

**How to apply:**
- The database refuses a decision that carries no reasons.
- Every round is kept.
- The originator receives the reasons as a handoff (see [[nothing-is-one-way]]).
- Loop guard: after 4 revise rounds the decision must be to replace or to withdraw, with reasons. It is never escalated to the founder.
- Built for blogs in #1114; the ceo seat audits and fixes every other CEO decision path in a separate PR.
