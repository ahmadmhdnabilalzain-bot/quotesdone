Founder, 28 Sep 2026, after he received an Arabic supplier-invitation seed that I had ordered for a readiness test: *"for suppliers its always english. never in arabic. for customers, its depending on the language they selected when they created the inquiry"*. He had already said this before; I broke it anyway.

**Why:** supplier communication is English by his ruling. Arabic belongs only to customers who chose it.

**How to apply:**
- Never build, test, seed or send an Arabic supplier message: invitations, nudges, inquiries, WhatsApp, SMS or PDFs.
- A readiness gate must not require an Arabic supplier seed.
- Customer messages follow the language chosen on the inquiry.
