Fetched in-browser 18 Sep 2026 (several of these 403 to curl/Playwright and need the Chrome tool). Kept because the knowledge chapter resumes Sunday and these are the primary sources.

**AC energy efficiency:** Cabinet Resolution No. (157) of 2025, "Technical Regulations for Energy Consumption Efficiency Requirements for Low and Large Capacity Air Conditioners". Issued 04 Nov 2025, effective 15 Nov 2025, Gazette 811 of 14 Nov 2025, ACTIVE. Repeals Cabinet Resolution No. (3) of 2020; 365 days to regularise. **The schedule with the actual EER figures sits behind a separate link and was NOT opened — the numbers are NOT known.** https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/3862

**Al Sa'fat (Dubai Green Building System, 2nd ed., Jan 2023):** contains NO COP/IPLV/EER/kW-per-TR figure anywhere. Clause 502.01 delegates efficiency to Dubai Building Code H.4.8.1 (buildings) and K.10.1.5.1 (villas); Golden Sa'fa needs a 4-star household AC rating, Platinum 5-star, and commercial/central at least 10% above the Building Code figures. Clause 701.05: HVAC and refrigeration refrigerants must be zero-ODP or GWP < 100 unless the unit holds < 0.23 kg; no CFC/HCFC/halon in fire suppression; from 1 Jan 2030 no HCFC or any ODP material. 503.04 chilled-water metering, 503.05 CCMS for >= 1 MW cooling or >= 5,000 sqm GFA.

**DEWA Regulations for Electrical Installations, 2017 edition** (supersedes 1997). MDB/SMDB and final distribution boards: factory built assemblies complying with BS EN 61439 / IEC 61439 (cl. 4.5.1, 4.5.2); busbar trunking to BS EN 61439 (cl. 7.3.2). **"Type-tested" is NOT required in those words for LV boards** — it appears only in the busbar trunking definition; type-test certificates are demanded for protection relays and instrument transformers. **There is NO DEWA approved-manufacturer list for LV switchboards** — cl. 1.6 accepts equipment meeting Appendix-1 standards / IEC / ISO. DEWA does publish approved-CONTRACTOR lists for 11 kV substation work and cable laying. The standards-and-regulations page 404s; the PDF is linked from the DEWA homepage.

**UAE Fire and Life Safety Code of Practice:** the current published edition is **September 2018**, ref CDGH-OP-25, 1,348 pages (page last updated 11 Jun 2026, no newer edition listed). **NFPA 72 is REFERENCED, not adopted** — cl. 2.2.4 and 2.2.7 tell the consultant to refer to NFPA 70/72/75/76/110/111 and manufacturer guidance; there is no "this code adopts NFPA 72" clause. Do not claim adoption.

**DMS-035-2020 Requirements for Steel Structures:** the published URL returns the Dubai Municipality homepage, and DM site search is broken. **NOT OBTAINED** — its scope and whether it invokes EN 1090 / ISO 3834 / AWS D1.1 is unknown. Do not assume.
