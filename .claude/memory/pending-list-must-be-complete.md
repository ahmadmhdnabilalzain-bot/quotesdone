When the founder asks what is pending, the answer must include every open item. On 24 Sep he found the CEO plan page (rebuilt, never republished) missing from two earlier "pending" answers. His words: *"when i ask you what is pending mention everything"*.

**Why:** an item left out of the list looks done, and he then discovers it himself. That is the same failure as a false "nothing pending".

**How to apply:** before answering, reconcile these sources:
1. public.founder_instruction where status='open'
2. open PRs
3. deliverables built but not published/sent (scratchpad artifacts, local files)
4. items on hold until a date
5. external waits (Twilio, LinkedIn, …)
6. things to verify with the first real data

Group the items, but never drop one. Related: [[every-founder-message-is-an-instruction]].
