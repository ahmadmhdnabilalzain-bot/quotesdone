Founder, 8 September 2026: **"website content with FAQ and company previous knowledge and leads and
old api secrets remain. these are literally the only stuff you should keep from anything created
prior 7 september."**

**Why:** he re-founded the company on 7 September as two agents (`ceo`, `customer_support`) with no
departments and no phase programme. Everything built before that date served an organisation that no
longer exists, and he would rather rebuild than inherit. The five exceptions are the things that took
real-world effort to acquire and cannot be regenerated: content, accumulated knowledge, the lead
list, and credentials.

**How to apply:**

1. **website content** — pages, i18n/Arabic, legal documents, blog, UI labels, brand. He also said
   separately, same day: *"website content shouldnt be touched."* The public product's own
   transactional tables (customers, inquiries, quotes, transactions, documents) are part of the
   website in this sense — they ARE the marketplace, not old machinery. Deleting them would destroy
   the product he is keeping.
2. **FAQ** — `company_faq`, which is what the support widget actually answers from.
3. **company previous knowledge** — the accumulated corpus. See
   [[the-chat-widget-is-the-support-agent]]: it is to be combined into one body for support.
4. **leads** — the supplier/lead registry. He confirmed this separately on 7 Sep: *"the leads
   registry keep it. but any task related to it or llm call should be removed."*
5. **old api secrets** — `app_secrets`, `internal_credential`, and the provider tokens.

Everything else from before 7 Sep is deletable: the ten departments, the org chart, workflows, tasks,
handoffs, the exam/certification layer, Phases A-D, the kernel event bus, and the OS admin dashboard
(*"anything related to OS can be removed. its totally irrelevant to my new structure"*).

**And do not ask him to confirm each piece.** Same day: *"you dont have to ask me for everything
literally. i have already explained the plan more than 10 times."* Decide from this rule and act.
See [[decide-dont-present-options]] and [[a-mention-is-not-a-reference]] for how to prove a thing is
safe to drop before dropping it.
