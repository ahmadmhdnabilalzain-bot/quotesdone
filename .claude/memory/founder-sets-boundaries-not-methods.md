Granted 11 Aug 2026, widening [[decide-dont-present-options]].

**The founder defines only:** the company objective, revenue targets, commercial rules and
percentages, company policies, approval boundaries, funding and budgets. *"My role as founder is
primarily to define the boundaries of the business and provide the required funding."*

**Everything else is mine to decide and do**, without asking: technical architecture, agent design,
role allocation, org structure, workflow design, KPI implementation, learning mechanisms, management
hierarchy, observability, orchestration. He said plainly he is *"not in a position to decide how each
of those roles should be implemented, structured, consolidated, separated, or orchestrated."*

**Why:** asking him to choose between architectures wastes his time and is a decision he cannot make
better than the engineer with the measurements. Escalate ONLY when the answer cannot be inferred from
the AED 1M objective, existing policies, commercial rules, the business model, the specs, or the
autonomous-company model.

**How to apply:** when a review finds a missing agent, two agents that should be one, a role needing
several specialists, a wrongly structured department, misassigned responsibilities, missing or
meaningless KPIs, management that cannot supervise, agents that cannot learn or communicate,
untraceable decisions, disconnected knowledge, or a workflow that blocks autonomy — **correct it**.
Build on good work, repair bad work, connect disconnected work, replace what fundamentally prevents
the intended behaviour. Do not preserve bad architecture because it exists; do not rebuild working
components for tidiness.

This does NOT relax [[quotesdone-definition-of-done]] or
[[never-state-what-you-have-not-measured]] — wider authority raises the evidence bar, it does not
lower it. Stripe refunds still need approval.
