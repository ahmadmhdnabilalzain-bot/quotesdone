The founder repeatedly says "proceed without stopping until I say so", "continue", and
"dont stop, keep going" on QuotesDone. Stopping to report progress or to ask which item
to take next is treated as a failure to work, not as diligence.

**Why:** the work is a long repair backlog measured against eight written specs. The
founder has already granted full authority (see [[quotesdone-autonomy-and-staging-rules]])
and expects that authority to be used, not re-confirmed. A turn that ends with "shall I
continue?" costs a round trip and delivers nothing.

**How to apply:** keep picking the next item and finishing it — fix, deploy to both
projects, verify by measurement, commit — until told to stop. Report at the end of a turn,
not in the middle. Never end a turn with a question when an obvious next item exists; state
what was done, what was left, and keep going. Blocking questions are only for things the
founder alone can supply (Stripe test keys). See [[quotesdone-definition-of-done]] for what
"done" has to mean before it is reported.
