Standing instruction from the founder, 11 Aug 2026. The acceptance standard for every change:

> THE COMPANY CAN OPERATE, LEARN, MANAGE ITSELF, IDENTIFY PROBLEMS, MAKE PROFESSIONAL DECISIONS,
> IMPROVE ITS PERFORMANCE, AND ACTIVELY WORK TOWARD AED 1,000,000 IN REVENUE BY AUGUST 2027 WITH
> MINIMAL FOUNDER INVOLVEMENT.

Explicitly NOT the standard: "the agent exists", "the workflow runs", "the table was created".

**The one business objective is AED 1,000,000 revenue by August 2027**, and it must flow downward:
management sets what departments must achieve → departments set what employees must achieve →
employees decide how to execute. He does not define the method; the system must derive it.

Every AI employee must: belong to a department · have a defined role · develop deep role expertise ·
know its department's objectives · receive tasks and priorities from management · report results and
performance back · have measurable KPIs · be monitored against them · learn from outcomes and
failures · improve its methods over time · coordinate across employees and departments · own the
operational decisions in its scope. **Management operates the same way.**

Failure must be traced up the chain, not filed at the point it appears. His example: a category
specialist that under-qualifies an inquiry → thin spec → suppliers cannot quote → poor quotes →
conversion drops → **revenue is lost**. Management must detect the revenue impact, find which
employee/workflow/knowledge/prompt/rule caused it, assign the correction, and check the KPI moved.

Knowledge gaps must never be answered with a weak guess. Expected behaviour: retrieve company
knowledge, consult another specialist, research via an approved tool, request the missing
information, raise a knowledge-gap task, escalate to a manager, then learn from the outcome.

Departments must self-assess. Social media does not finish at "posted" — it owns reach, impressions,
engagement, clicks, conversions, audience response, ad cost, and performance by format/topic/timing/
platform/campaign, and must not propose spending money without evidence it serves the objective.
