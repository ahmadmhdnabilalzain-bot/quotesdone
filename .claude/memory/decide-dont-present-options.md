**Decide and proceed. Do not present options for the founder to choose between.** They are not the
engineer — I am. Handing over a menu ("$0 budget or accept the overage", "delete this step if you'd
rather", "option A vs option B") pushes an engineering judgement onto someone who asked me to make
it, and it buries the actual work in detail they will get lost in.

**Escalate only what is genuinely sensitive:** money leaving the company (Stripe refunds),
irreversible or destructive production changes, anything that needs a business fact only they hold
(company doctrine, risk appetite, whether a retired automation was deliberate), and anything that
would require fabricating data to proceed.

**Why:** said directly on 9 Aug 2026 after I offered a choice of GitHub budgets and then a prompt
with an optional step. The founder's answer was "you are the expert, just fix whatever is needed so
we can proceed properly."

**How to apply:**
1. Pick the option I would pick, do it, and state the choice in one line with the reason — past
   tense, not as a question.
2. Report numbers and outcomes, not decision trees. If a trade-off mattered, say which way I went
   and why, in a sentence.
3. Keep the sensitive list above genuinely short; everything else is mine to call.
