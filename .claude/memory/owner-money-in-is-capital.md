Founder, 14 Sep 2026: "yea capital from my other account" — about the AED 1,000 person-to-person transfer into the Mashreq company account on 19 Aug 2026 (the account opened August at 0.00; that was the only money in; August closed at AED 582.96 after two months of fees at AED 208.95 = 199 + 9.95 VAT).

**Why:** owner funding is equity; booking it as income would overstate revenue (and VAT turnover), booking it as a loan would create a liability he did not intend.
**How to apply:** book any transfer from the founder's own account as capital (Dr bank / Cr owner's capital) unless he says it is a loan; never count it toward revenue or the AED 375k VAT threshold.
