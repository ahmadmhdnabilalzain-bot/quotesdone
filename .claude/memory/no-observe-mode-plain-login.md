Founder, 10 Aug 2026: *"no need for that observe mode. I don't think that I have to text to see
whatever. Normal login with the mobile number, verification and that's it."*

He is rejecting a specific shipped behaviour: a mode where he must send a message before a surface
becomes visible to him. **Remove the gate, do not redesign around it.**

**Why:** he is the owner of the company. Auth exists to keep other people out, not to make the
founder prove intent every time he looks at his own business. Ceremony on his own path is why the
dashboard goes unopened.

**How to apply:**
- Founder auth: mobile number → verification code → in. Nothing further.
- This is about the FOUNDER's path. It is not licence to weaken customer, supplier or service-role
  authentication, which stay as they are.
- Any surface currently behind "observe mode" should be reachable straight from a normal session.
