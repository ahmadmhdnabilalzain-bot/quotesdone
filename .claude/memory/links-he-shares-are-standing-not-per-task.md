When the founder shares a link — 26 Aug 2026, `https://github.com/public-apis/public-apis`, a curated
directory of free public APIs — it is a **standing pointer**, not a suggestion for the current task.
His words: *"when i share a github link it doesn't mean that its just only for this task. its in
general, maybe the next session would have something that we can use from github."*

**Why:** he is building a toolbox over many sessions. Answering "that doesn't apply to what I'm doing
right now" throws away the reason he sent it, and he has to say it twice.

**How to apply:** take the link, note what it is and what it could be useful for, and keep going.
Record it where a later session will find it rather than replying only in the moment. Do not
evaluate it solely against the current ticket.

**And the obvious constraint he should not have to state:** never reach for a third-party API where
the company already has an account and a live connection — Supabase, Stripe, Twilio, Dropbox,
OpenRouter, GitHub. His words: *"of course you wont use a random api for something we have an
account for and you are already connected to it. be logical."* A public directory is for gaps
nothing connected already covers.

[[the-hold-is-on-routes-the-key-is-on-twelve-functions]] (the zero-cost hold is about **paid AI
providers and live business effects** — it is not a reason to avoid reading a public list or using a
free tool, and conflating the two is its own error).
