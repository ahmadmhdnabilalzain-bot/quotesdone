"always make sure things are in loop. nothing literally should ever be a one way communication" (14 Sep 2026).

**Why:** a notice nobody acknowledges is indistinguishable from one that was lost; he relies on the seats closing work without him.
**How to apply:** every finding/handoff/report is a tracked agent_actions item with an owner and a reply window; the responder acts and replies; the ORIGINATOR re-verifies and is the only one who closes; a missing reply is itself a finding that escalates seat → CEO → founder email. Automatic fixes count only after re-verification. Check any new design for fire-and-forget sends before building. Same day: "also we should not rely on the LLM as much as possible in the operation" — detection, fixes, verification, escalation, money, approvals and notices are deterministic and templated; a model only where no rule can do the job (reading arbitrary drawings/documents, free-text chat), always with a deterministic fallback.
