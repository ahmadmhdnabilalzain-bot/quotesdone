**Why:** the founder can't diagnose problems himself. A premature "ready" lands real suppliers and customers on a broken path.
**How to apply:**
- Keep a written go/no-go checklist with evidence per gate. Re-measure every gate on the day of the "ready" call; never reuse yesterday's result.
- Anything NOT MEASURED or flaky blocks the word "ready". Report "not ready because X", not "mostly ready".
- After launch, run the first-day watch myself: spend vs estimate, the outbox, bounces/complaints, errors. Report problems with their fix, not as a question to him.
