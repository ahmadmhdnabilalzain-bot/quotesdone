Binding inputs for the investor pack and the five-year model, from the founder on 24 Sep 2026:

**Revenue**
- Supplier subscriptions are a MAIN revenue line, not a side line: AED 1,000 a year for every supplier from #1,001 onwards.
- Supplier targets: the first 50 within 2 weeks (his own network, separate from the leads); 200 by Nov 2026; 300 by Dec 2026; about 500 by Jul/Aug 2027; at least 3,000 by year 5 (a minimum, and he aims higher).
- Reveal fees: include the enterprise case, where a company runs its procurement through QuotesDone.

**Costs** (total under about AED 250k a year)
- The founder is the ONLY human cost: AED 10,000 a month management fee.
- Ads: at most AED 5,000 a month.
- LLM cost falls over time as models get cheaper.
- Subscriptions as actually paid.
- One-off: LLC AED 7,000 and trademark AED 5,000.
- NOTHING for legal, insurance, accounting, content or branding, and no staff. The seats do that work: the accountant does the books and VAT, and evidence for disputes comes from the platform's signed-terms, IP and refund records.

**The ask:** AED 200,000 for 50%. Of that, AED 100k repays the founder (development plus subscriptions paid) and AED 100k funds about 6 months of running costs.

**Design:** investor-grade. Use the brand kit (public/brand logo, the site's theme colours and fonts), a complete detailed financial study, and a compelling story.

**Why:** his words: the first pack "would ruin things badly" and "if I saw this pitch deck I'm definitely not investing". **How to apply:** these inputs override the earlier agent assumptions. The AED 1M target stays withdrawn. Related: [[the-only-hard-rules-and-operating-doctrine]].
**24 Sep correction:** the CEO seat gets only the TARGETS (milestones per metric and per seat, plus cost ceilings). Never share the investor pack, financial study, ask, valuation or cap table with any seat; the investor deal may not happen and has nothing to do with running the company.
