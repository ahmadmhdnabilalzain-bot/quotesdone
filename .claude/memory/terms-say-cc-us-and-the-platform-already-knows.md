Terms of Service **v6, 29 June 2026, §5** (`src/content/legal.json`, key `terms`) requires,
for a non-responsive-supplier refund, that the customer:

- (a) raise the complaint within **7 days** of the Reveal, and
- (b) **contact the Supplier in writing and copy `supplier@quotesdone.com`** on that message,
  "so that your genuine attempt to reach the Supplier is on record".

A refund is then due only if the supplier fails to respond to **both** the customer and
QuotesDone within **3 working days**. "All refunds are assessed and issued by QuotesDone at
its reasonable discretion; no refund is automatic."

**Founder, 19 Aug 2026, in two parts:**

1. *"it's not the customer who has to send to the supplier and put us on the loop"* — the
   platform already notifies both parties on reveal, so a prior CC is not the mechanism.
2. *"when he is raising a complaint through the customer portal, or through the chat widget
   he has, or through our WhatsApp agent, then we ask for proof to make sure the supplier
   is not responding."*

**So the proof obligation is real, but it is PULLED BY US AT COMPLAINT TIME, not pushed by
the customer at outreach time.** Clause (b) as published makes a refund conditional on
something the customer had to do earlier or forfeit; the real policy asks for evidence when
they complain. The **7-day** and **3-working-day** windows were not contradicted and stand.

**Three complaint intake channels:** customer portal · chat widget · WhatsApp agent.

**Why:** the frozen Phase B model already matches the real policy — W17-S01 opens a case
"from customer request, provider event, internal alert or fraud signal" and W17-S03 is
"Collect transaction and communication evidence … and complaint evidence". It is the
PUBLISHED TERMS that are out of step with the product, not the workflow.

**How to apply:** W17 encodes the real policy; build to it. The legal text is outward-facing —
do not edit §5 without his explicit go-ahead on exact wording. See
[[reveal-notifies-both-parties]] and [[every-refund-needs-the-founder]].
