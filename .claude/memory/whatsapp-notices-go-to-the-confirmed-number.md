Founder, 14 Sep 2026 (founder_instruction, subject "supplier agreement clause 21"): "whatsapp number supplier confirmed." Clause 21 of v11 said WhatsApp notices go "to the registered mobile"; from v12 they go to the WhatsApp number the supplier confirmed with a verification code (or the registered mobile when they said it is their WhatsApp). SMS stays on the registered mobile.

**Why:** the confirmed number is the one the supplier proved they use; a legal notice must reach a channel the supplier agreed to.
**How to apply:** v12 needs re-acceptance; until a supplier accepts v12, notices follow the version they accepted (v11 → registered mobile). Never send a notice to a channel the supplier's accepted version does not name. Builds on the confirmed-number system of PR #657 (fn_whatsapp_destination).
