On 12 Aug 2026 four independent defects turned out to be one shape: a green number, or a red
alarm, about something other than what it named. None of them had failed, so no watcher saw them.

- 17 "lessons" at confidence 0.9 saying "Promote to standard practice" were 4 readings of our own
  HTTP health-check pass rate, credited to 8 unrelated agents including legal and brand_mgr.
- `workflow_step_sweep` raised `error` 144×/day calling a deliberate reflection throttle a stalled
  engine; `it_health` believed it and routed an incident to the CTO.
- `login_otp` proved a mailbox and wrote it `where claimed_by = <email>` at a moment when no row
  carried that address — `UPDATE 0`, forever, on the invite path.
- `agent_brain`'s "stable" cache prefix was recomputed from the query-matched tail's length, so it
  moved on every call.

Ask it of any metric before trusting it, and especially before building what a brief proposes.
