The founder's rules are exact, and nobody may reinterpret or reword them. Every customer and supplier message, template, page and knowledge row must state them as ruled. No seat or agent may invent a variant.

The rule that was broken on 24 Sep: ANY number of suppliers may quote on a request (there could be 100 quotations). "Top 3" means ONLY that the comparison report recommends the top three by value. Never write "up to three suppliers will quote".

Links: a customer email links to the customer sign-in at /me. /portal is the supplier entry.

**Why:** founder, 24 Sep 2026: "When such instructions are being given by me, they are like a holy book. You cannot just change that." This came after a customer activation email said "up to three qualified UAE suppliers will quote" and linked to the supplier page.

**How to apply:** every rule goes into the single source of truth, along with its forbidden wrong forms, and is enforced by a drift check. Related: [[company-knowledge-stays-in-sync-everywhere]], [[the-only-hard-rules-and-operating-doctrine]].
