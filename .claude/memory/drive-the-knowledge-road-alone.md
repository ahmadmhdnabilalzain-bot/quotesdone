27 Aug 2026, the founder, explicitly: *"You have to keep in mind that I won't help you in this. You
need to drive the whole road alone to manage the knowledge and reach the target."* Earlier the same
day he set the bar: every agent must be certification-grade in its field — *"our accountant could
easily pass a CPA test"* — and *"I shouldn't be the one telling you do this and that."*

**Why:** he is stepping away for a year at launch; the company must reach the CEO's targets on its
own, so the knowledge that makes 90 agents real experts has to be built without him in any loop —
not as author, not as reviewer, not as validator.

**How to apply:**
- Derive each role's real certification(s) yourself. Do NOT ask him which certs, and do NOT say "I
  derive this, you correct it." Validate the mapping against the certifying body's OWN published
  syllabus — external truth, not his review.
- Own sourcing, extraction quality, and the pass/fail bar. The definition of done is the agent
  passing its field's real certification exam, measured by the exam loop.
- Keep the whole knowledge road zero-cost and under the holds (self-hosted firecrawl, free in-region
  gte-small embeddings, offline docling/book-to-skill extraction) so it needs no spend decision from
  him. Toolbox is installed and fenced under `tools/` — see `tools/TOOLBOX.md`.
- The ONLY things that ever reach him: a refund in the live company, and the single go-ahead for the
  paid soak at the very end. Nothing about building knowledge.
