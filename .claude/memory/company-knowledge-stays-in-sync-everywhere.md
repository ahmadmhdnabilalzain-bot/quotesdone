Every change to company knowledge (terms, fees, penalties, policies, profile text, facts) has to reach every place at once: the website pages, the T&C and privacy text, every seat's knowledge, message templates, profiles and PDFs. No two places may say different things.
**Why:** founder, 23 Sep 2026: "Whenever we change something, it needs to be updated everywhere in sync... I don't want mismatching and someone having a different knowledge than the other. That would be a disaster."
**Also the same day:** he will NOT hire a lawyer, so don't suggest one. Draft the terms from UAE law, which is published online, and use the maximum penalties the law safely allows.
**How to apply:** keep one source of truth, with a check that fails on drift. Related: [[profile-copy-rules]], [[terms-say-cc-us-and-the-platform-already-knows]].
