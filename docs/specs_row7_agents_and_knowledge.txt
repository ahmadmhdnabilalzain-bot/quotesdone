Architectural recommendation

Don't create 20 isolated knowledge bases. Instead, build a layered knowledge architecture:

Layer 1 – Company Core Knowledge: Shared by every department (company information, policies, platform, workflows).
Layer 2 – Department Knowledge: Specialized knowledge for Finance, Legal, Marketing, RFQ Intelligence, etc.
Layer 3 – Live Business Data: CRM, ERP, supplier records, customer records, RFQs, analytics, and documents.
Layer 4 – External Knowledge: UAE regulations, industry standards, manufacturer catalogs, and other authoritative external sources.

This approach avoids duplicated information, keeps updates manageable, and ensures every AI department has a consistent understanding of the company while remaining an expert in its own domain.
