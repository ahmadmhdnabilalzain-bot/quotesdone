# Licensed photo shortlist: faceless, real UAE and GCC work

**Status: a shortlist only. Nothing here has been downloaded or cleared for use.**
Compiled 14 Sep 2026 by the `ceo` agent.

**Why this exists.** The founder ruled on 14 Sep 2026 that the site uses **no AI-generated people**: no
generated Emiratis and no generated suited teams. Corporate visuals must be either:

1. **Product-first.** These are real depictions of what the platform does. They are built as
   components in `src/components/visual/product.tsx`.
2. **Faceless, real UAE work imagery from properly licensed stock.** Never generated. This file is
   the candidate list for this kind.

Real people come later, and only with their consent.

## How this was checked, and what was not

- **Licences.** Both licence pages were fetched on 14 Sep 2026.
- **Unsplash pages.** Each page was confirmed through Unsplash's photo data, which gave the
  **original resolution (measured)**. None of the Unsplash candidates is an Unsplash+ (paid) photo,
  and none is flagged as AI-generated.
- **Pexels pages.** Each page loaded, and the photographer and alt text were read from it. The pages
  did not expose pixel dimensions, so **Pexels resolutions are not verified**.
- **Download size and file size are not verified for any candidate.** The "size to use" column
  names the standard download option each site offers. It does not state a measured byte count.
  Measure the size when the file is actually taken.
- **Faces and logos.** These were judged **from each photo's description and tags only**. Nobody
  has opened the images yet. **Each photo must be visually checked before use** for faces, national
  dress, brand logos, signage and maker marks on equipment.
- **UAE location.** Candidates 1–3 and 7 are tagged Dubai. The others are generic trade subjects
  from elsewhere, which suits trade and material close-ups where location does not show.

## The licences (fetched 14 Sep 2026)

**Unsplash License** (https://unsplash.com/license): free to use, "including for commercial
purposes". No attribution is required, though it is appreciated. You may not:

- sell a photo without significantly modifying it;
- compile photos to build a similar or competing service.

**Pexels License** (https://www.pexels.com/license/): free to use. Attribution is not required. You
may not:

- show identifiable people in a bad light or in an offensive way;
- sell unaltered copies, such as prints or physical products;
- imply that the people or brands in a photo endorse the product;
- redistribute photos on other stock or wallpaper sites;
- use a photo as part of a trademark or business name.

## Before any of these goes on the site

1. Open the photo and check it for faces, national dress, logos, signage and maker marks. Drop any
   photo that fails. Do not blur a face to rescue a photo.
2. Download the named size and **record the actual pixel size and byte size** here.
3. Convert to AVIF or WebP through `next/image` and give it alt text in both EN and AR.
4. Record the photographer and the page URL next to the asset. Neither licence requires this, but it
   keeps the source traceable for an audit.
