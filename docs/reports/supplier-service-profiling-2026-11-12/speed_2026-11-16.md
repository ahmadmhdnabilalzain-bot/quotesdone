# A supplier's services on screen within a minute: before and after (16 Nov 2026)

Seat: sales_engineer. Branch `sales_engineer-a-suppliers-services-are-on-screen-within-a-minute`. All runs are paid runs of
`scripts/e2e/services_hard_profiles/run.mjs` on the 16 ZZ DEMO hard profiles, luna-pro, step A and step B at medium (effort_policy,
unchanged). Times are the reader's own clock: the list is ready when step B ends. The registration adds about 4.5 s before step A.

## Why it was slow
The paid certification of 16 Nov (J9) put the list on screen after 117-122 s. Step A was one call of 74-86 s on a 1,800-character
profile (24k tokens in, ~10k out, mostly reasoning), and the slowest of 4 mapping calls of 8 phrases took 28-35 s. The effort and model
are set in `_shared/effort_policy.ts` SURFACES `supplier_doc_trades.profile_reading` / `.profile_mapping` (medium: rulings 1630, 1642,
sales_engineer 12 Nov) and `model_policy` row `supplier_trade_choice` (openai/gpt-6-luna-pro > claude-sonnet-5.5, max_tokens 8000:
`scripts/sql/a_supplier_profile_s_trades_are_chosen_by_luna_pro.sql`, founder 19 Oct, rulings 1218/1241). A lower effort is not open:
`effortFor` raises a 'low' on a class that decides (ruling 1630). A faster model is a model_policy change, so a founder decision.

## What changed (code only; effort and model unchanged)
Measured: a reading call's time grows with the claims it lists, about 2 s each on a 15-25 s floor. A document set of up to 6,000
characters is now read at once: one call for the whole (summary, licence fit, also-ask, supply-only; no evidence) and parts of 300
characters that list only their own evidence and vague lines, with the parts before and after as context. Every part is mapped with the
whole call's summary, 4 phrases a call. A call still unanswered after 35 s (read) or 16 s (map) is asked once more, and the first
answer wins. When the parts judge every claim not covered by the licence, the fit is 'no'. Longer sets and typed text are read as before.
Tried and dropped: parts given the whole documents listed beyond their part, and a whole call told "evidence: []" still listed every
service (33-41 items, 67-82 s). The system prompt has to change; an instruction in the user text is not enough.
