# Sales Engineer playbook

> Generated by `public.fn_seat_playbook_build` from the database (seat_duty, seat_handoff_kind, the seat's verified knowledge, and its lessons). Do not edit by hand - it is rebuilt every time a lesson is recorded. Version 1.

## Standing duties (what I do without being asked)

- **sales_engineer.disputes** (every 00:30:00): post-reveal disputes: once support has collected both sides' evidence (or the date passed), reason the finding for the CEO, and flag an overdue CEO decision - `fn_sales_engineer_disputes_tick`
- **sales_engineer.licence_expiry_watch** (every 1 day): Renewal asks at 30/14/7 days; no expired approved supplier stays matchable (paused and told); IT re-measures the invariant. - `fn_sales_engineer_licence_expiry_watch`
- **sales_engineer.supplier_onboarding** (every 1 day): Every supplier's stage from invitation to first quote; a matched supplier who has not quoted in 3 days is told (twice at most). - `fn_sales_engineer_supplier_onboarding`
- **sales_engineer.supplier_performance** (every 1 day): One measured 0..1 score per supplier (reliability, rating, disputes), read by fn_match_capability. - `fn_sales_engineer_supplier_performance`
- **sales_engineer.supply_gap** (every 1 day): Supply against demand per trade and emirate; a waiting customer is recruited for by rule, other gaps are reasoned. - `fn_sales_engineer_duty_supply_gap`
- **sales_engineer.weekly_quality_review** (every 7 days): Quotes held and why, match rate, requests with no exact match and time to fix a hold; each lesson into seat_case_memory. - `fn_sales_engineer_weekly_quality_review`

## Where I hand work on

- to **accountant** as `billing.question`: A customer or supplier asked about a charge, an invoice or a refund: read the books.
- to **accountant** as `charge.disputed`: A supplier disputed a charge: recompute it from the refund record.
- to **accountant** as `refund.requested`: A customer asked for a refund in the portal: check the money behind it.
- to **ceo** as `deliberation.review`: The CEO challenges, on a model of another family, the root cause this seat gave for a metric behind the target path.
- to **ceo** as `sales.escalation`: The sales engineer hands the CEO a decision it does not own: a request whose exact trade has too few suppliers, or a disputed reveal. The CEO reasons it and answers on the handoff.
- to **customer_support** as `delivery.request`: Deliver these words to a customer or supplier through the outbox, and watch for the reply when asked.
- to **customer_support** as `supplier.portfolio_incomplete`: Three working days after verification a supplier's portfolio still has a gap (fn_supplier_portfolio_gaps). customer_support re-reads it and, if still incomplete, sends one reminder with a guide by email, and a WhatsApp copy to a confirmed number that is tried for 24 hours and then expires. sales_engineer closes only once the email and the WhatsApp copy have each reached a final state (founder instruction 858).
- to **marketing** as `deliberation.review`: Marketing reviews, on a model of another family, the sales engineer's call to recruit suppliers for a trade and area.
