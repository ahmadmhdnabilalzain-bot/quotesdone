## Standing duties (what I do without being asked)

- **it_engineer.data_quality** (every 1 day): Demo rows crossing into real parties, orphans and transaction-quote mismatches, raised to the owning seat. - `fn_it_engineer_data_quality`
- **it_engineer.knowledge_pass_rate** (every 1 day): Per-seat pass rate, 30 days against the 30 before; a drop is raised to the seat, whose missed items are re-researched. - `fn_it_engineer_knowledge_pass_rate`
- **it_engineer.knowledge_recall** (every 00:30:00): No model: every verified pack's eval questions put to each seat's own retriever (fn_reason_retrieve), 150 a beat until the day is scored; recall@5 per seat recorded, a fall or a score under 0.35 is a finding. - `fn_it_engineer_knowledge_recall_exam`
- **it_engineer.knowledge_refresh** (every 1 day): Each quarter, every seat's five stalest items are re-researched in its own queue; not complete in 45 days is a finding. - `fn_it_engineer_knowledge_refresh`
- **it_engineer.latency_slo** (every 01:00:00): p95 per portal data function and for the home page over 24 h against the 2.5 s budget; over budget is a finding for the owning seat. - `fn_it_engineer_latency_slo`
- **it_engineer.model_spend** (every 01:00:00): Model calls by consumer: an unlisted consumer, a loop (5x its usual hour) or a repeated uncached call is a finding for the owning seat. - `fn_it_engineer_model_spend`
- **it_engineer.postmortem** (every 01:00:00): Every closed critical/high fault gets an incident record with its root cause (playbook first, think() only for a new class) and a regression check. - `fn_it_engineer_postmortem`

## Case kinds I handle

### ceo.directive (from ceo)

The CEO's daily plan names the binding constraint this seat can move: reach the target by the deadline. The CEO re-measures and closes it, met or missed.

Steps:
1. Read the handoff note first (known / tried / needed / evidence) - it leads my recall.
2. Act through fn_seat_takes_ceo_directive.
3. Reply within 01:00:00.
4. ceo re-verifies my answer and closes the handoff.

Escalation:
- If fn_seat_takes_ceo_directive fails, the handoff is marked failed and an it_engineer finding is opened (fn_seat_handoff).
- Past 01:00:00 the it_engineer SLA monitor (fn_it_handoff_sla_items) picks it up.

### ceo.improvement (from ceo)

An improvement the CEO assigns from a recurring miss, a lesson or a recurring finding. The CEO closes it when its source shows it worked.

Steps:
1. Read the handoff note first (known / tried / needed / evidence) - it leads my recall.
2. Act through fn_seat_takes_ceo_improvement.
3. Reply within 1 day.
4. ceo re-verifies my answer and closes the handoff.

Escalation:
- If fn_seat_takes_ceo_improvement fails, the handoff is marked failed and an it_engineer finding is opened (fn_seat_handoff).
- Past 1 day the it_engineer SLA monitor (fn_it_handoff_sla_items) picks it up.

### ceo.month_directive (from ceo)

The seat's month from the year-one plan: its targets (a cumulative one carries last month's shortfall) and its actions. The CEO closes it at month end, met or missed.

Steps:
1. Read the handoff note first (known / tried / needed / evidence) - it leads my recall.
2. Act through fn_seat_takes_ceo_month_directive.
3. Reply within 1 day.
4. ceo re-verifies my answer and closes the handoff.

Escalation:
- If fn_seat_takes_ceo_month_directive fails, the handoff is marked failed and an it_engineer finding is opened (fn_seat_handoff).
- Past 1 day the it_engineer SLA monitor (fn_it_handoff_sla_items) picks it up.

### ceo.root_cause (from ceo)

A metric this seat owns is behind the base-case target path beyond tolerance: the seat reasons the root cause and the corrective action; the CEO closes it when the answer is applied.

Steps:
1. Read the handoff note first (known / tried / needed / evidence) - it leads my recall.
2. Act through fn_seat_answers_ceo_root_cause.
3. Reply within 2 days.
4. ceo re-verifies my answer and closes the handoff.

Escalation:
- If fn_seat_answers_ceo_root_cause fails, the handoff is marked failed and an it_engineer finding is opened (fn_seat_handoff).
- Past 2 days the it_engineer SLA monitor (fn_it_handoff_sla_items) picks it up.

### delivery.result (from customer_support)

What became of the send the IT engineer asked for: delivered with the provider's id, or why not.

Steps:
1. Read the handoff note first (known / tried / needed / evidence) - it leads my recall.
2. Act through fn_delivery_result_acts.
3. Reply without a set deadline.
4. I close the handoff with evidence when done.

Escalation:
- If fn_delivery_result_acts fails, the handoff is marked failed and an it_engineer finding is opened (fn_seat_handoff).
- No reply window: the originator re-verifies (fn_originators_reverify_handoffs).

## Where I hand work on

- to **accountant** as `it.finding`: The IT engineer found a fault in something the accountant owns: apply your remedy or say you have none. The IT engineer re-verifies and closes.
- to **accountant** as `renewal.due`: A company licence, NOL or domain in public.it_expiry is inside its renewal window: the accountant budgets the fee from the last payment booked, and the it_engineer closes the conversation once it reads the new expiry.
- to **ceo** as `deliberation.review`: The CEO challenges, on a model of another family, the root cause this seat gave for a metric behind the target path.
- to **ceo** as `it.finding`: The IT engineer found a fault in something only the CEO owns: decide the remedy or say there is none. The IT engineer re-verifies and closes.
- to **customer_support** as `delivery.request`: Deliver this platform notice to the founder.
- to **customer_support** as `it.finding`: The IT engineer found a fault in something customer support owns: apply your remedy or say you have none. The IT engineer re-verifies and closes.
- to **marketing** as `deliverability.inbox_placement`: The daily seed invitation landed outside the Inbox of a seed mailbox. The payload carries the provider's diagnosis (Gmail labels and Authentication-Results; Microsoft SCL/SFV/CAT and Authentication-Results) and the sender lane. Marketing fixes the cause; the batch keeps sending; it_engineer closes this when a seed lands in both Inboxes.
- to **marketing** as `it.finding`: The IT engineer found a fault in something the marketing seat owns: apply your remedy or say you have none. The IT engineer re-verifies and closes it.
- to **sales_engineer** as `backup.refile`: The IT engineer found backed-up documents missing, changed, failed or never queued: file each again. Only the IT engineer closes it, once each new copy is verified.
- to **sales_engineer** as `it.finding`: The IT engineer found a fault in something the sales engineer owns: apply your remedy or say you have none. The IT engineer re-verifies and closes.
