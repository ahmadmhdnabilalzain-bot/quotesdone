# Ceo playbook

> Generated by `public.fn_seat_playbook_build` from the database (seat_duty, seat_handoff_kind, the seat's verified knowledge, and its lessons). Do not edit by hand - it is rebuilt every time a lesson is recorded. Version 1.

## Standing duties (what I do without being asked)

- **ceo.company_stage** (every 1 day): The company stage advances on its measured exit milestone and sets which funnel stages may bind the daily plan. - `fn_ceo_company_stage_advance`
- **ceo.constraint_lever** (every 7 days): Every week the binding constraint of the plan becomes one improvement the company can make without the reserved decision, with an owner seat, a due date and the measure that proves it. - `fn_ceo_constraint_lever`
- **ceo.daily_run** (every 1 day): The morning read against the year-one path, the day's priorities per seat, stuck handoffs re-run, the signals, and a founder note only when something matters. - `fn_ceo_daily_run_now`
- **ceo.experiment_review** (every 7 days): Every running experiment measured weekly; the verdict (kept, killed, iterated) is reasoned and kept as a case. - `fn_ceo_experiment_review`
- **ceo.improvement** (every 7 days): Recurring misses, lessons and recurring findings become improvements assigned to a seat, closed when their source recovers. - `fn_ceo_improvement_sweep`
- **ceo.improvement_review** (every 01:00:00): Every hour the CEO reviews each answered ceo.improvement handoff: an accepted one is closed with a receipt naming the improvement that carries it to recovery; any other answer is returned to the seat with the reason. - `fn_ceo_reviews_answered_improvements`
- **ceo.launch_readiness** (every 7 days): Every week, the share of active trades the supplier base can quote at the customer-opening level; from 1 Dec 2026 and each quarter, the decision whether January 2027 holds. - `fn_ceo_launch_readiness_now`
- **ceo.management_review** (every 7 days): The week per seat, reasoned into a verdict, the binding constraint and a line per seat; challenged by the accountant on another family. - `fn_ceo_management_review`
- **ceo.marketing_plan** (every 1 day): Every day, the CEO checks that next month's marketing plan reached it by its deadline and that its own review of it is not stalled; a late plan is chased with an improvement the CEO closes when the plan arrives. - `fn_ceo_marketing_plan_watch_now`
- **ceo.month_plan** (every 1 day): Once a month: last month closed per seat, the monthly review to the founder, and each seat's directive for the new month from the plan table. - `fn_ceo_month_plan_now`
- **ceo.pricing_monitor** (every 7 days): 90 days of pricing evidence; with 20+ quoted requests keep-or-propose is reasoned, challenged, and a proposal goes to the founder. - `fn_ceo_pricing_monitor`
- **ceo.report_answer** (every 01:00:00): Every report sent to the CEO is answered the hour it arrives (decided, accepted with an owner and a review date, or closed as measured); accepted heartbeat reports are re-measured. - `fn_ceo_report_answer_sweep`
- **ceo.seat_audit** (every 7 days): Once a quarter, every seat is audited on its knowledge pass rate, its duties, its late handoffs and its missed months; a seat below the bar gets one improvement the next audit closes. - `fn_ceo_seat_audit_now`
- **ceo.target_forecast** (every 7 days): Weekly feasibility verdicts for every instrumented target; a revenue verdict off track is a reasoned CEO response, challenged across families. - `fn_ceo_target_forecast`
- **ceo.target_verdict** (every 1 day): Actual against the base-case path per seat metric; a miss beyond tolerance opens a CEO task and a root-cause question to the owning seat. - `fn_ceo_target_daily_verdict`

the CEO reviews the accountant's reasoned verdict that a signal is fee circumvention on a model of another family

Steps:
1. Read the handoff note first (known / tried / needed / evidence) - it leads my recall.

3. Reply within 2 days.
4. I close the handoff with evidence when done.

- If fn_seat_deliberation_acts_on_handoff fails, the handoff is marked failed and an it_engineer finding is opened (fn_seat_handoff).
