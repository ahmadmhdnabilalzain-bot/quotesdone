
# Founder Autonomy & Approval Policy

## Operating intent

QuotesDone operates as a fully automated company. The founder supplies capital, defines constitutional/commercial policies, issues high-level commands and observes outcomes through the Command Center.

The founder is not a routine operational approver.

## Only routine founder approval

The only routine manual approval is:

```text
approve or reject a specific refund case
```

The system must enforce:

```text
founder approval requests where action_class != refund_decision = 0
```

A refund case must be prepared by the responsible roles with amount, reason, evidence, customer/supplier impact, fraud/risk checks and recommendation. The founder approves or rejects. After approval, Payments executes and Financial Control independently reconciles.

## Autonomous internal operation

All other ordinary company actions are decided and executed by the accountable roles, managers and independent reviewers defined in Phase A and Phase B, within policy, available funds, authority, gates and evidence.

Examples that must not be sent to the founder for approval:

- supplier approval/refusal;
- inquiry quote-readiness;
- supplier-safe brief approval;
- supplier matching and dispatch;
- quotation comparison/publication;
- marketing content and channel decisions;
- spend allocation inside available budgets;
- production release after independent QA/security gates;
- legal/compliance decisions within approved policy;
- knowledge validation;
- ordinary company payments within budget;
- account bans/fraud enforcement;
- staff/agent task assignment and remediation.

## Founder command authority

The founder can at any time:

- issue an order;
- set/revise priority;
- ask for status or evidence;
- cancel or supersede work;
- initiate a policy, commercial, organizational or strategic change;
- provide or limit funding.

A founder command is not a bypass. It enters the canonical workflow, keeps safety/privacy/legal/financial gates, creates tasks and remains auditable.

## Founder-initiated-only matters

The following cannot be originated or executed autonomously as completed commitments:

- equity, ownership, fundraising, investor admission, sale, merger or winding-up;
- reveal fee, subscription price/start, exemption or commission-policy change;
- company revenue-target replacement;
- country entry/exit commitment;
- permanent founder-policy change;
- material business-model change;
- Phase A/Phase B structural change.

Agents may research and recommend these. They may not create an approval queue. They wait for an explicit founder command.

## Funding gap

When available funds are insufficient, management reports:

- amount required;
- purpose;
- evidence and expected effect;
- alternatives;
- consequences of not funding.

The founder decides whether to provide funds. Management remains responsible for the operational method and outcome.

## Prohibited escalation pattern

An agent may not escalate a normal operational decision to the founder merely because it lacks confidence. It must first use internal evidence, tools, specialist consultation, manager judgment, reviewer verdict, clarification, or fail-closed blocking.
