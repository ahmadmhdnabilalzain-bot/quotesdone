# QuotesDone — Website Audit (2026-09-14) — Part 1 of 2: what was found

> **Status of this document.** This is the completed AUDIT half: ten readers covered every page, the design system, both portals, Arabic, mobile/performance/SEO/accessibility, where the intelligence shows, trust and legal, and a benchmark of five world-class front doors. All of it is here, unabridged. The second half — three complete design directions scored by a contractor, a designer and the engineer who has to build it, the refutation pass on these findings, and the maximum upgrade plan in order — was paused before it finished, on the founder's instruction. It resumes from cache.
>
