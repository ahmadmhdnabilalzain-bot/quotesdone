Cross-OS Communication Contract

This is the part that transforms 10 isolated systems into one company.

Every Operating System should communicate through a standard contract:

Event Created
        ↓
Event Bus
        ↓
Relevant Operating Systems Subscribe
        ↓
Each OS Assesses Impact
        ↓
Each OS Creates Internal Tasks
        ↓
Each OS Executes Within Its Authority
        ↓
Results Returned
        ↓
Chief of Staff Consolidates Outcomes
        ↓
CEO Updates Company Priorities (if required)
        ↓
Knowledge OS Captures Lessons
        ↓
Intelligence OS Updates KPIs and Forecasts

No OS should call another OS directly for business logic. Communication should be event-driven with clear ownership, making the system easier to scale and audit.

The final instruction I'd give Claude

"Do not implement these as ten folders or ten groups of agents. Implement them as ten independent Operating Systems that all inherit the same architecture (Purpose, Mission, Objectives, Responsibilities, Employees, Knowledge, Inputs, Outputs, Workflows, Decision Engine, KPI Engine, Memory, Learning, Governance, Collaboration, Health, and Continuous Improvement). Each Operating System must own its business domain, expose standardized interfaces to the rest of the company, and communicate through an event-driven architecture with full auditability, measurable outcomes, and continuous learning. The result should resemble the operating model of a large enterprise rather than a collection of AI agents."

This approach gives Claude a clear architectural target and makes the platform much easier to verify, extend, and maintain as QuotesDone grows.
