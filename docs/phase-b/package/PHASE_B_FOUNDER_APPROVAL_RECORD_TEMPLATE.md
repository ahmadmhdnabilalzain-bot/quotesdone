## Freeze rule

After approval, Phase B may be changed only through a formal workflow-change request containing:

- observed problem;
- evidence;
- affected workflow and steps;
- affected owner/reviewer;
- state and transition impact;
- handoff impact;
- business-rule impact;
- founder-boundary impact;
- historical compatibility;
- migration and rollback;
- founder approval requirement.

Later technical wiring may not silently change Phase B ownership or business behavior.
