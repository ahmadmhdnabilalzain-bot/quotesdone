# Improved Supplier Portal target

## Launch-grade modules

1. **AI onboarding conversation** backed by a deterministic onboarding state machine.
2. **Supplier command center**: profile health, document risks, relevant opportunities, quote drafts, performance and recommended actions.
3. **Supplier digital twin**: evidence-backed capability and compliance profile.
4. **Product intelligence**: versioned catalogues, products, variants, attributes, documents and brands.
5. **Opportunity intelligence**: deterministic fit score and plain-language reasons.
6. **Quote workspace**: upload/extract or form, structured completeness, line items and coaching.
7. **Performance and risk**: response, wins, quality, expiry and remediation.
8. **Project collaboration** after reveal.
9. **Grounded supplier assistant** using only supplier-owned data, category corpus and privacy-safe aggregates.

## Important improvements to the founder vision

- Do not show a recommended quote price unless it is derived from the supplier's own approved price list, costs or historical quotes. AI must not invent commercial terms.
- Negotiation suggestions require supplier-defined minimum margin, discount authority and explicit send approval.
- Market-price/demand insights need freshness and minimum sample sizes; never expose another supplier's confidential pricing.
- Inventory is optional and should use integrations where possible rather than forcing manual updates.
- Gamification should be transparent quality/eligibility tiers, not pay-to-win visibility.
- 3D factory/network views and native mobile are deferred until the core portal has adoption.
