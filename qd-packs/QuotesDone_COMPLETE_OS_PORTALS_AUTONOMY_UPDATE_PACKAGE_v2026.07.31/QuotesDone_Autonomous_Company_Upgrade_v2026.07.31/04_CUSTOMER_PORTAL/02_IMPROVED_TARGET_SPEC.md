# Improved Customer Portal target

## Launch-grade modules

1. **Procurement command workspace** with current inquiries, quote decisions, projects, alerts and recommendations.
2. **AI procurement assistant** connected to the 86-category corpus and deterministic question engine.
3. **Inquiry workspace** with status, scope, documents, matched-pool health and quote responses.
4. **Comparison room** where every quote is accessible and the best three are recommended transparently.
5. **Project workspace** connecting multiple inquiries, transactions, documents and timeline.
6. **Document vault** with permission-safe search and provenance.
7. **Post-reveal supplier relationship and messaging**.
8. **Full Arabic customer experience and bilingual reports**.

## Comparison algorithm

1. Extract and normalize every quote and line item.
2. Apply hard gates: expiry, mandatory scope, quantity, required documents and eligibility.
3. Calculate technical compliance against category corpus rules.
4. Calculate evaluated total cost including explicit included/excluded cost components.
5. Score delivery, warranty, payment, completeness and supplier reliability.
6. Apply customer priorities only after compliance.
7. Rank all quotes deterministically and retain the score breakdown.
8. Select top three.
9. Use AI only to explain the already-calculated result in plain English/Arabic.
10. If AI is unavailable, show a deterministic report rather than failing.

## Important improvements to the founder vision

- Pre-reveal supplier discovery must remain anonymous. Show pool size, capability fit and verification—not company identity.
- Savings and market-price claims require real, fresh data and must show methodology.
- Customer memory is opt-in and editable.
- Enterprise approvals are optional and isolated from the simple individual customer flow.
- 3D/BIM is a specialized view, not the primary navigation.
