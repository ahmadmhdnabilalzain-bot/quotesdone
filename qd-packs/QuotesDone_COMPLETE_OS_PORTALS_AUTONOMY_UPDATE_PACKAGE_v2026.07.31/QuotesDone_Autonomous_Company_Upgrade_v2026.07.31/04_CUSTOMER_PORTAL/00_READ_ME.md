# Customer Portal upgrade

The deployed backend already supports the core anonymous marketplace: customer identity/profile, AI intake, drawing/BOQ analysis, inquiry creation, matching, quote display, top-three comparison, PDFs, reveal/payment, refunds, ratings, inquiry edits/cancellation, project records and post-reveal messaging.

The maximum Customer OS vision requires a stronger self-service workspace, deterministic comparison, document/project intelligence and optional enterprise organization/approval features.
