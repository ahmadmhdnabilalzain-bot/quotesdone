# Executive OS / Command Center upgrade

The backend already contains much of the founder's conceptual OS: ten operating systems, objectives, employees, events, health, executive meetings, reflection, simulations, graph, time-machine snapshots and operating manual.

The missing work is to turn those primitives into a coherent founder experience and close operational gaps. The frontend cannot be certified from the export, so Claude must audit the repository first.

## Improved product principle

The founder should open QuotesDone and understand the company in minutes. The OS should not force routine approvals. It should surface:

- what changed;
- what is off target;
- what the company already handled;
- what it could not handle;
- which rare strategic decision genuinely needs the founder;
- the evidence behind every recommendation.
