# Reconciled target architecture

## 1. Experience layer

- **Founder Executive OS** — Observe Mode by default, rare strategic instructions, optional Control Mode.
- **Customer Procurement OS** — inquiry, projects, anonymous quote comparison, reveal and post-reveal collaboration.
- **Supplier Growth OS** — onboarding, products, opportunities, quote quality, performance and documents.

## 2. Ten Operating Systems

Leadership, Commercial, Marketplace, Operations, Technology, Finance, Legal & Governance, Knowledge, Intelligence and Evolution remain the business ownership model.

## 3. Orchestration plane

- Canonical event bus and event schema registry.
- Durable workflow runtime with run and step state.
- Decision engine with policy/authority/confidence gates.
- Action executor registry for actual side effects.
- Retry, idempotency, locking, compensation and dead-letter handling.
- Unified decision trace and evidence lineage.

## 4. Domain services

Marketplace, customer, supplier, projects, quote extraction/comparison, payments, finance, legal, communications, marketing, document intelligence and knowledge.

## 5. Knowledge plane

- 86-route supplier-category corpus.
- Company and department operations corpus.
- Live Supabase business data.
- Source-backed external regulations/standards.
- Deterministic questions/rules separate from narrative retrieval.

## 6. AI plane

- No model call when SQL, rules, templates or corpus retrieval can complete the task.
- Small model for natural-language parsing and rephrasing.
- Strong model only for ambiguous reasoning, vision/drawings and exceptional analysis.
- Outcome feedback, cost tracking, cache and model fallback.

## 7. Governance and safety

- Default Observe Mode for the founder.
- Temporary MFA-protected Control Mode for manual changes.
- Routine low-risk actions delegated by policy.
- High-risk actions founder-gated.
- Complete auditability, kill switches, redaction, data retention and rollback.

## 8. Presentation strategy

Command-first does not mean navigation-free. Provide:

- Natural-language command bar.
- Keyboard command palette.
- Accessible fallback navigation.
- Live workspaces, drill-down and evidence drawers.
- 2D graph and timelines first.
- 3D/spatial layers only when they add measurable value.
