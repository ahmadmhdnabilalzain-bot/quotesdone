# Implementation roadmap

## P0 — correctness and autonomy foundation

1. Audit the real frontend repository.
2. Replace the legacy 16-category `profile_ai` classification with the canonical 86-route taxonomy.
3. Connect the supplier-category corpus to inquiry qualification and quote comparison.
4. Add durable workflow runs/steps, correlation IDs, event outbox and unified decision traces.
5. Expand action executor coverage for safe real actions.
6. Implement deterministic top-three comparison with an AI explanation layer and fallback report.
7. Add automated privacy, idempotency and outage tests.

## P1 — launch-grade portals and Observe Mode

1. Executive Home v2 and live activity stream.
2. Default founder Observe Mode; separate MFA Control Mode.
3. Customer command workspace, inquiry lifecycle and comparison room.
4. Supplier command center, digital twin, opportunity ranking and document center.
5. Full Arabic acceptance suite for customer-facing routes.

## P2 — intelligence and collaboration

1. Supplier product intelligence and product cards.
2. Customer projects, document vault and supplier relationships.
3. Supplier/customer grounded assistants.
4. Interactive executive reports, employee workspace and executive meeting room.
5. Cross-department case/workflow views.

## P3 — enterprise and optimization

1. Customer organizations, roles and approval policies.
2. Negotiation assistants with explicit authority boundaries.
3. Market/spend intelligence with privacy and data-quality controls.
4. Generalized scenario simulation and competitive intelligence.
5. Event-level replay and richer graph.

## P4/P5 — optional advanced presentation

- Voice companion.
- Native mobile apps after PWA usage proof.
- 3D factory/project/HQ views only where data exists and users benefit.
- Autonomous organization restructuring remains founder-gated.
