## Charter
Maintain layered company knowledge, institutional memory, learning, source lineage, reviews and role certification across every department.

## Authority and operating boundary
The department may act only within the live authority matrix. Legal judgment, material money movement, identity/authentication, secrets, destructive data operations, production deployment, public claims and changes to the autonomy framework remain hard-gated.

## Knowledge records
### Company Core Knowledge — operating knowledge
Knowledge Engineering & Corporate Learning must understand company core knowledge as an operational capability, not as a generic definition. The knowledge must identify the business purpose, owners, inputs, outputs, evidence, decision thresholds, failure conditions and downstream effects. It must preserve the department charter — Maintain layered company knowledge, institutional memory, learning, source lineage, reviews and role certification across every department. — and contribute to the north-star outcome: Employees and agents answer from current, cited knowledge and the company preserves lessons across time. Knowledge records should distinguish stable policy from live operational facts and should point to live tables rather than copying values that change.

### Company Core Knowledge — how the department uses it
When work involves company core knowledge, the department should first establish the entity and workflow state, then retrieve current operational data, then apply the approved SOP and decision rules. The output must be structured for the next department rather than left as an informal narrative. The agent should identify what is known, what is inferred, what is missing and what evidence supports the conclusion. Repeated work should update reusable templates or rules only through the knowledge-change process.

### Company Core Knowledge — controls and boundaries
For company core knowledge, the department must apply least privilege, data minimisation, audit logging, version control and the relevant authority tier. It must not treat a generated answer as proof. Where the topic affects legal rights, money, identity, privacy, security, production deployment or public claims, execution is blocked until the required approval and evidence are present. Conflicts between live data and static knowledge are resolved in favour of the authorised live source, with the discrepancy logged for review.

### Company Core Knowledge — evidence and live-data requirements
The department should prove work on company core knowledge through the authoritative record for the entity, relevant documents, event history, approvals and outcome metrics. Candidate live sources include: dept_library, employee_knowledge, employee_knowledge_standard, agent_knowledge, agent_memory, institutional_memory, org_memory, kb_chunks. Candidate execution evidence includes: dept_learn, kb_ingest, kb_embed, kb_search, knowledge_exam, learn_gaps, reflect, export_all_knowledge. These names come from the supplied production export or the target design; Claude must map them to the current repository and schema, confirm permissions and record any mismatch instead of silently inventing a binding.

### Knowledge Engineering & Corporate Learning exception 10 — Repeated workflow failure
