# HR & People Operations — Operating Knowledge Pack

**Department key:** `hr_people`  
**Production mapping:** No one-to-one production department key confirmed  
**Implementation assessment:** `partial`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

## Charter
Define roles, recruit and onboard people or agents, maintain policies, coordinate compensation and leave, and develop capability and accountability.

## North-star outcome
Every worker or agent has a clear role, verified competence, current knowledge and measurable performance.

## Authority and operating boundary
The department may act only within the live authority matrix. Legal judgment, material money movement, identity/authentication, secrets, destructive data operations, production deployment, public claims and changes to the autonomy framework remain hard-gated.

## Knowledge records
### Workforce Planning — operating knowledge
HR & People Operations must understand workforce planning as an operational capability, not as a generic definition. The knowledge must identify the business purpose, owners, inputs, outputs, evidence, decision thresholds, failure conditions and downstream effects. It must preserve the department charter — Define roles, recruit and onboard people or agents, maintain policies, coordinate compensation and leave, and develop capability and accountability. — and contribute to the north-star outcome: Every worker or agent has a clear role, verified competence, current knowledge and measurable performance. Knowledge records should distinguish stable policy from live operational facts and should point to live tables rather than copying values that change.

### Workforce Planning — how the department uses it
When work involves workforce planning, the department should first establish the entity and workflow state, then retrieve current operational data, then apply the approved SOP and decision rules. The output must be structured for the next department rather than left as an informal narrative. The agent should identify what is known, what is inferred, what is missing and what evidence supports the conclusion. Repeated work should update reusable templates or rules only through the knowledge-change process.

### Workforce Planning — controls and boundaries
For workforce planning, the department must apply least privilege, data minimisation, audit logging, version control and the relevant authority tier. It must not treat a generated answer as proof. Where the topic affects legal rights, money, identity, privacy, security, production deployment or public claims, execution is blocked until the required approval and evidence are present. Conflicts between live data and static knowledge are resolved in favour of the authorised live source, with the discrepancy logged for review.

### Workforce Planning — evidence and live-data requirements
The department should prove work on workforce planning through the authoritative record for the entity, relevant documents, event history, approvals and outcome metrics. ## Decision rules
### HR & People Operations rule 01 — Missing mandatory evidence
If a required document, field or provider event is absent, do not infer completion. Set the case to needs_information, request the exact missing evidence and suspend irreversible execution. The HR & People Operations agent must record which rule fired, the relevant entity state and the downstream action.

### HR & People Operations rule 02 — Confidence below floor
If routing or interpretation confidence is below the configured floor, ask one targeted clarification or route to the responsible specialist. The HR & People Operations agent must record which rule fired, the relevant entity state and the downstream action.

### HR & People Operations rule 03 — Live data conflicts with static knowledge
Treat the authoritative live record as operational truth, prevent execution if the conflict affects safety or rights, and create a knowledge-correction task. The HR & People Operations agent must record which rule fired, the relevant entity state and the downstream action.

### HR & People Operations rule 04 — Duplicate task or event
Use the idempotency key and current entity state to suppress duplicate execution while preserving the duplicate event in the audit trail. The HR & People Operations agent must record which rule fired, the relevant entity state and the downstream action.

### HR & People Operations rule 05 — Authority exceeded
Block execution and request approval from the role specified in the authority matrix, including amount, impact, evidence and rollback plan. The HR & People Operations agent must record which rule fired, the relevant entity state and the downstream action.

### HR & People Operations rule 06 — Customer or supplier impact
Before executing a material change, identify affected users, required notice, contractual or privacy implications and a remediation path. The HR & People Operations agent must record which rule fired, the relevant entity state and the downstream action.

### HR & People Operations rule 07 — Provider or integration unavailable
Queue recoverable work, switch only to approved fallback services, apply backoff, communicate service impact and reconcile after recovery. The HR & People Operations agent must record which rule fired, the relevant entity state and the downstream action.

### HR & People Operations rule 08 — Irreversible action
Require two-step confirmation, evidence snapshot, explicit scope and legal or data-owner review where applicable. The HR & People Operations agent must record which rule fired, the relevant entity state and the downstream action.

### HR & People Operations rule 09 — Policy ambiguity
Do not create an ad-hoc policy through a model answer. Escalate the policy question and continue only with reversible advisory work. The HR & People Operations agent must record which rule fired, the relevant entity state and the downstream action.

### HR & People Operations rule 10 — Material anomaly
Compare against prior baselines, rule out data-quality errors, create an incident if genuine and stop automated amplification. The HR & People Operations agent must record which rule fired, the relevant entity state and the downstream action.

## Exception playbooks
### HR & People Operations exception 01 — External AI provider unavailable
Route to deterministic logic or the approved small/local model; queue tasks requiring stronger reasoning; never weaken privacy, legal or financial controls to maintain throughput. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### HR & People Operations exception 02 — Database or vector retrieval failure
Stop decisions that depend on missing evidence, retry with backoff, use a verified read replica or last-known snapshot only where policy allows, and reconcile once restored. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### HR & People Operations exception 03 — Email delivery failure
Record provider response, retry through the outbox, respect suppression and idempotency, use an approved alternative channel only when consent and urgency permit. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### HR & People Operations exception 04 — WhatsApp or SMS outage
Queue messages, avoid duplicate sends, fall back to email or in-app notification only according to channel policy, and notify operations when SLA is at risk. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### HR & People Operations exception 05 — Corrupted or unsupported document
Quarantine the file, preserve the original, ask for a supported replacement or conversion and do not treat partial extraction as complete evidence. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### HR & People Operations exception 06 — Two agents disagree
Compare source quality and authority, run the independent review chain and escalate only the unresolved decision with both recommendations and evidence. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### HR & People Operations exception 07 — Locked or conflicting record
Do not overwrite. Retry safely, surface the competing update, merge only through the entity's concurrency policy and maintain version history. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### HR & People Operations exception 08 — User changes material requirements mid-workflow
Version the scope, freeze prior quotations from direct comparison, notify affected parties and restart qualification for changed fields. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### HR & People Operations exception 09 — Security or privacy suspicion
Contain first, preserve evidence, restrict access, notify the security/privacy owner and follow the incident and breach-assessment runbook. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### HR & People Operations exception 10 — Repeated workflow failure
Open an incident and root-cause task, disable unsafe automation if needed, use the manual safe path and update tests before re-enabling. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.
