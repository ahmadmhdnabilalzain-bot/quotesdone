# Executive Office / Chief of Staff — Operating Knowledge Pack

**Department key:** `executive_office`  
**Production mapping:** exec  
**Implementation assessment:** `implemented`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

## Charter
Translate founder objectives into coordinated company execution, maintain strategic alignment, surface only genuine exceptions, and keep the founder informed without turning the command centre into a manual approval queue.

## North-star outcome
Founder objectives completed with clear outcomes, controlled risk and minimal unnecessary founder intervention.

## Core knowledge domains
1. company vision and mission
2. organizational structure
3. business strategy
4. company goals and OKRs
5. department responsibilities
6. executive KPIs and dashboards
7. financial performance
8. risk register
9. decision-making framework
10. priority hierarchy
11. project portfolio
12. competitor and market intelligence
13. board and investor reporting
14. company calendar and operating cadence
15. delegation and escalation
16. business continuity
17. capital allocation
18. scenario planning
19. founder communications
20. organizational health

## Owned workflows
1. founder objective to execution
2. weekly executive review
3. monthly strategy review
4. company-wide risk review
5. board and investor reporting
6. cross-department conflict resolution
7. priority reset
8. capital-allocation recommendation

## Authority and operating boundary
The department may act only within the live authority matrix. Legal judgment, material money movement, identity/authentication, secrets, destructive data operations, production deployment, public claims and changes to the autonomy framework remain hard-gated.

## Knowledge records
### Company Vision And Mission — operating knowledge
Executive Office / Chief of Staff must understand company vision and mission as an operational capability, not as a generic definition. The knowledge must identify the business purpose, owners, inputs, outputs, evidence, decision thresholds, failure conditions and downstream effects. It must preserve the department charter — Translate founder objectives into coordinated company execution, maintain strategic alignment, surface only genuine exceptions, and keep the founder informed without turning the command centre into a manual approval queue. — and contribute to the north-star outcome: Founder objectives completed with clear outcomes, controlled risk and minimal unnecessary founder intervention. Knowledge records should distinguish stable policy from live operational facts and should point to live tables rather than copying values that change.

### Company Vision And Mission — how the department uses it
When work involves company vision and mission, the department should first establish the entity and workflow state, then retrieve current operational data, then apply the approved SOP and decision rules. The output must be structured for the next department rather than left as an informal narrative. The agent should identify what is known, what is inferred, what is missing and what evidence supports the conclusion. Repeated work should update reusable templates or rules only through the knowledge-change process.

### Company Vision And Mission — controls and boundaries
For company vision and mission, the department must apply least privilege, data minimisation, audit logging, version control and the relevant authority tier. It must not treat a generated answer as proof. Where the topic affects legal rights, money, identity, privacy, security, production deployment or public claims, execution is blocked until the required approval and evidence are present. Conflicts between live data and static knowledge are resolved in favour of the authorised live source, with the discrepancy logged for review.

### Company Vision And Mission — evidence and live-data requirements
The department should prove work on company vision and mission through the authoritative record for the entity, relevant documents, event history, approvals and outcome metrics.
