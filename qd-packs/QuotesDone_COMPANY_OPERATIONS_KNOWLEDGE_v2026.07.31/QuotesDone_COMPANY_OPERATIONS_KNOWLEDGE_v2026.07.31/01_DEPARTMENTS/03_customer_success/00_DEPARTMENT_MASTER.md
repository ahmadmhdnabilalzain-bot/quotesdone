# Customer Success — Operating Knowledge Pack

**Department key:** `customer_success`  
**Production mapping:** customer_success  
**Implementation assessment:** `implemented`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

## Charter
Own customer onboarding, inquiry support, service communication, complaints, retention and fair remediation while protecting customer privacy.

## North-star outcome
Customers reach useful, comparable quotations quickly and understand every material decision and fee.

## Core knowledge domains
1. customer onboarding
2. OTP and account verification
3. customer profiles
4. terms acceptance
5. customer lifecycle
6. support documentation
7. customer communication
8. complaint resolution

11. customer satisfaction
12. retention
13. repeat usage
14. accessibility

18. reveal support
19. feedback analysis
20. escalation procedures

## Owned workflows
1. customer registration
2. terms acceptance evidence
3. inquiry support
4. comparison-report delivery
5. reveal support
6. supplier non-response complaint
7. refund case coordination
8. customer feedback and retention
9. PDPL request intake

## Authority and operating boundary
The department may act only within the live authority matrix. Legal judgment, material money movement, identity/authentication, secrets, destructive data operations, production deployment, public claims and changes to the autonomy framework remain hard-gated.

## Knowledge records
### Customer Onboarding — operating knowledge
Customer Success must understand customer onboarding as an operational capability, not as a generic definition. The knowledge must identify the business purpose, owners, inputs, outputs, evidence, decision thresholds, failure conditions and downstream effects. It must preserve the department charter — Own customer onboarding, inquiry support, service communication, complaints, retention and fair remediation while protecting customer privacy. — and contribute to the north-star outcome: Customers reach useful, comparable quotations quickly and understand every material decision and fee. Knowledge records should distinguish stable policy from live operational facts and should point to live tables rather than copying values that change.

### Customer Onboarding — how the department uses it
When work involves customer onboarding, the department should first establish the entity and workflow state, then retrieve current operational data, then apply the approved SOP and decision rules. The output must be structured for the next department rather than left as an informal narrative. The agent should identify what is known, what is inferred, what is missing and what evidence supports the conclusion. Repeated work should update reusable templates or rules only through the knowledge-change process.

### Customer Onboarding — controls and boundaries
For customer onboarding, the department must apply least privilege, data minimisation, audit logging, version control and the relevant authority tier. It must not treat a generated answer as proof. Where the topic affects legal rights, money, identity, privacy, security, production deployment or public claims, execution is blocked until the required approval and evidence are present. Conflicts between live data and static knowledge are resolved in favour of the authorised live source, with the discrepancy logged for review.

### Customer Onboarding — evidence and live-data requirements
The department should prove work on customer onboarding through the authoritative record for the entity, relevant documents, event history, approvals and outcome metrics. Candidate live sources include: customers, customer_score, customer_kpi_snapshots, feedback, contact_messages, pdpl_requests, inquiries, quote_comparisons. Candidate execution evidence includes: customer_api, customer_portal, login_otp, mobile_otp, pdpl_request, refund_flow, refunds_api, messages_api. These names come from the supplied production export or the target design; Claude must map them to the current repository and schema, confirm permissions and record any mismatch instead of silently inventing a binding.

### Customer Success rule 07 — Provider or integration unavailable

### Customer Success exception 10 — Repeated workflow failure
