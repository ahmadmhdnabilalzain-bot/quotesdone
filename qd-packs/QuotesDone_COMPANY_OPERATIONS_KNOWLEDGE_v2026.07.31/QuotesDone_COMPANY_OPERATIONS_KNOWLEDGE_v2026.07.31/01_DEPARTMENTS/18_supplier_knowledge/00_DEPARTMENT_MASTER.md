# Supplier Knowledge — Operating Knowledge Pack

**Department key:** `supplier_knowledge`  
**Production mapping:** knowledge, knowledge_eng  
**Implementation assessment:** `implemented`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

## Charter
Own the supplier taxonomy and professional knowledge corpus, including sources, products, standards, regulations, qualification logic and update cycles.

## North-star outcome
Every inquiry and comparison uses current, cited and validated category knowledge.

## Authority and operating boundary
The department may act only within the live authority matrix. Legal judgment, material money movement, identity/authentication, secrets, destructive data operations, production deployment, public claims and changes to the autonomy framework remain hard-gated.

## Knowledge records
### Supplier Taxonomy — operating knowledge
Supplier Knowledge must understand supplier taxonomy as an operational capability, not as a generic definition. The knowledge must identify the business purpose, owners, inputs, outputs, evidence, decision thresholds, failure conditions and downstream effects. It must preserve the department charter — Own the supplier taxonomy and professional knowledge corpus, including sources, products, standards, regulations, qualification logic and update cycles. — and contribute to the north-star outcome: Every inquiry and comparison uses current, cited and validated category knowledge. Knowledge records should distinguish stable policy from live operational facts and should point to live tables rather than copying values that change.

### Supplier Taxonomy — how the department uses it
When work involves supplier taxonomy, the department should first establish the entity and workflow state, then retrieve current operational data, then apply the approved SOP and decision rules. The output must be structured for the next department rather than left as an informal narrative. The agent should identify what is known, what is inferred, what is missing and what evidence supports the conclusion. Repeated work should update reusable templates or rules only through the knowledge-change process.

### Supplier Taxonomy — controls and boundaries
For supplier taxonomy, the department must apply least privilege, data minimisation, audit logging, version control and the relevant authority tier. It must not treat a generated answer as proof. Where the topic affects legal rights, money, identity, privacy, security, production deployment or public claims, execution is blocked until the required approval and evidence are present. Conflicts between live data and static knowledge are resolved in favour of the authorised live source, with the discrepancy logged for review.

### Supplier Taxonomy — evidence and live-data requirements
The department should prove work on supplier taxonomy through the authoritative record for the entity, relevant documents, event history, approvals and outcome metrics. Candidate live sources include: knowledge_domain, agent_knowledge, kb_chunks, kb_revisions, kb_usage_log, knowledge_module_standard, knowledge_exam_questions, knowledge_exam_results. Candidate execution evidence includes: kb_ingest, kb_import, kb_embed, kb_deepen, kb_fill_gap, kb_autofill_gaps, knowledge_exam, gen_category_knowledge. These names come from the supplied production export or the target design; Claude must map them to the current repository and schema, confirm permissions and record any mismatch instead of silently inventing a binding.

### Supplier Knowledge rule 07 — Provider or integration unavailable

### Supplier Knowledge rule 08 — Irreversible action

### Supplier Knowledge rule 09 — Policy ambiguity

### Supplier Knowledge rule 10 — Material anomaly
Compare against prior baselines, rule out data-quality errors, create an incident if genuine and stop automated amplification. The Supplier Knowledge agent must record which rule fired, the relevant entity state and the downstream action.

### Supplier Knowledge exception 02 — Database or vector retrieval failure

### Supplier Knowledge exception 03 — Email delivery failure

### Supplier Knowledge exception 04 — WhatsApp or SMS outage
Queue messages, avoid duplicate sends, fall back to email or in-app notification only according to channel policy, and notify operations when SLA is at risk. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### Supplier Knowledge exception 05 — Corrupted or unsupported document
Quarantine the file, preserve the original, ask for a supported replacement or conversion and do not treat partial extraction as complete evidence. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### Supplier Knowledge exception 06 — Two agents disagree
Compare source quality and authority, run the independent review chain and escalate only the unresolved decision with both recommendations and evidence. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### Supplier Knowledge exception 07 — Locked or conflicting record

### Supplier Knowledge exception 08 — User changes material requirements mid-workflow
Version the scope, freeze prior quotations from direct comparison, notify affected parties and restart qualification for changed fields. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### Supplier Knowledge exception 09 — Security or privacy suspicion

### Supplier Knowledge exception 10 — Repeated workflow failure
