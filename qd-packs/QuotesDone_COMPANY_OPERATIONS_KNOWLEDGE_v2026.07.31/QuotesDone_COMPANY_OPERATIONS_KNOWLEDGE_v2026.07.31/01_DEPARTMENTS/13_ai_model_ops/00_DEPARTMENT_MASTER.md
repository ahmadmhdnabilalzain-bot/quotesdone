# AI & Model Operations — Operating Knowledge Pack

**Department key:** `ai_model_ops`  
**Production mapping:** knowledge_eng, evolution  
**Implementation assessment:** `implemented`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

## Charter
Design, evaluate, route and monitor models and agents so that deterministic logic is used where possible and expensive models are reserved for justified exceptions.

## North-star outcome
High task accuracy at the lowest safe cost, with traceable model decisions and controlled autonomy.

## Authority and operating boundary
The department may act only within the live authority matrix. Legal judgment, material money movement, identity/authentication, secrets, destructive data operations, production deployment, public claims and changes to the autonomy framework remain hard-gated.

## Knowledge records
### Llms — operating knowledge
AI & Model Operations must understand LLMs as an operational capability, not as a generic definition. The knowledge must identify the business purpose, owners, inputs, outputs, evidence, decision thresholds, failure conditions and downstream effects. It must preserve the department charter — Design, evaluate, route and monitor models and agents so that deterministic logic is used where possible and expensive models are reserved for justified exceptions. — and contribute to the north-star outcome: High task accuracy at the lowest safe cost, with traceable model decisions and controlled autonomy. Knowledge records should distinguish stable policy from live operational facts and should point to live tables rather than copying values that change.

### Llms — how the department uses it
When work involves LLMs, the department should first establish the entity and workflow state, then retrieve current operational data, then apply the approved SOP and decision rules. The output must be structured for the next department rather than left as an informal narrative. The agent should identify what is known, what is inferred, what is missing and what evidence supports the conclusion. Repeated work should update reusable templates or rules only through the knowledge-change process.

### Llms — controls and boundaries
For LLMs, the department must apply least privilege, data minimisation, audit logging, version control and the relevant authority tier. It must not treat a generated answer as proof. Where the topic affects legal rights, money, identity, privacy, security, production deployment or public claims, execution is blocked until the required approval and evidence are present. Conflicts between live data and static knowledge are resolved in favour of the authorised live source, with the discrepancy logged for review.

### Llms — evidence and live-data requirements
The department should prove work on LLMs through the authoritative record for the entity, relevant documents, event history, approvals and outcome metrics. ### AI & Model Operations exception 09 — Security or privacy suspicion

### AI & Model Operations exception 10 — Repeated workflow failure
