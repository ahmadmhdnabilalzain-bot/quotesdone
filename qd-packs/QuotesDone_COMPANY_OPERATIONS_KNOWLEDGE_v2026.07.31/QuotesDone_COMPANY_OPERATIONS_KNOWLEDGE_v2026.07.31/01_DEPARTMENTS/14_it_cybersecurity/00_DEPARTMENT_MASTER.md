# IT, SRE & Cybersecurity — Operating Knowledge Pack

**Department key:** `it_cybersecurity`  
**Production mapping:** it  
**Implementation assessment:** `implemented`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

## Charter
Keep the platform available, access-controlled, recoverable and secure while protecting credentials, customer data and operational continuity.

## North-star outcome
Reliable services with rapid detection and recovery, least-privilege access and no unmanaged critical security exposure.

## Authority and operating boundary
The department may act only within the live authority matrix. Legal judgment, material money movement, identity/authentication, secrets, destructive data operations, production deployment, public claims and changes to the autonomy framework remain hard-gated.

## Knowledge records
### Networking — operating knowledge
IT, SRE & Cybersecurity must understand networking as an operational capability, not as a generic definition. The knowledge must identify the business purpose, owners, inputs, outputs, evidence, decision thresholds, failure conditions and downstream effects. It must preserve the department charter — Keep the platform available, access-controlled, recoverable and secure while protecting credentials, customer data and operational continuity. — and contribute to the north-star outcome: Reliable services with rapid detection and recovery, least-privilege access and no unmanaged critical security exposure. Knowledge records should distinguish stable policy from live operational facts and should point to live tables rather than copying values that change.

### Networking — how the department uses it
When work involves networking, the department should first establish the entity and workflow state, then retrieve current operational data, then apply the approved SOP and decision rules. The output must be structured for the next department rather than left as an informal narrative. The agent should identify what is known, what is inferred, what is missing and what evidence supports the conclusion. Repeated work should update reusable templates or rules only through the knowledge-change process.

### Networking — controls and boundaries
For networking, the department must apply least privilege, data minimisation, audit logging, version control and the relevant authority tier. It must not treat a generated answer as proof. Where the topic affects legal rights, money, identity, privacy, security, production deployment or public claims, execution is blocked until the required approval and evidence are present. Conflicts between live data and static knowledge are resolved in favour of the authorised live source, with the discrepancy logged for review.

### Networking — evidence and live-data requirements
The department should prove work on networking through the authoritative record for the entity, relevant documents, event history, approvals and outcome metrics. Candidate live sources include: admin_mfa_sessions, app_secrets, security_events, security_audit_log, security_selfaudit_snapshots, secops_rule, health_checks, health_state. Candidate execution evidence includes: os_health, ai_health, dr_drill, backup, dropbox_backup, pdpl_erase, qa_suite. These names come from the supplied production export or the target design; Claude must map them to the current repository and schema, confirm permissions and record any mismatch instead of silently inventing a binding.

## Exception playbooks
### IT, SRE & Cybersecurity exception 01 — External AI provider unavailable
Route to deterministic logic or the approved small/local model; queue tasks requiring stronger reasoning; never weaken privacy, legal or financial controls to maintain throughput. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### IT, SRE & Cybersecurity exception 02 — Database or vector retrieval failure
Stop decisions that depend on missing evidence, retry with backoff, use a verified read replica or last-known snapshot only where policy allows, and reconcile once restored. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### IT, SRE & Cybersecurity exception 03 — Email delivery failure
Record provider response, retry through the outbox, respect suppression and idempotency, use an approved alternative channel only when consent and urgency permit. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### IT, SRE & Cybersecurity exception 04 — WhatsApp or SMS outage
Queue messages, avoid duplicate sends, fall back to email or in-app notification only according to channel policy, and notify operations when SLA is at risk. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### IT, SRE & Cybersecurity exception 05 — Corrupted or unsupported document
Quarantine the file, preserve the original, ask for a supported replacement or conversion and do not treat partial extraction as complete evidence. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### IT, SRE & Cybersecurity exception 06 — Two agents disagree
Compare source quality and authority, run the independent review chain and escalate only the unresolved decision with both recommendations and evidence. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### IT, SRE & Cybersecurity exception 07 — Locked or conflicting record
Do not overwrite. Retry safely, surface the competing update, merge only through the entity's concurrency policy and maintain version history. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### IT, SRE & Cybersecurity exception 08 — User changes material requirements mid-workflow
Version the scope, freeze prior quotations from direct comparison, notify affected parties and restart qualification for changed fields. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### IT, SRE & Cybersecurity exception 09 — Security or privacy suspicion
Contain first, preserve evidence, restrict access, notify the security/privacy owner and follow the incident and breach-assessment runbook. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.

### IT, SRE & Cybersecurity exception 10 — Repeated workflow failure
Open an incident and root-cause task, disable unsafe automation if needed, use the manual safe path and update tests before re-enabling. The department remains accountable for state, communication and reconciliation until the exception is formally handed off or closed.
