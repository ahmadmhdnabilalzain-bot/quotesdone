## Authority and operating boundary
The department may act only within the live authority matrix. Legal judgment, material money movement, identity/authentication, secrets, destructive data operations, production deployment, public claims and changes to the autonomy framework remain hard-gated.

## Knowledge records
### Partnership Strategy — operating knowledge
Business Development & Partnerships must understand partnership strategy as an operational capability, not as a generic definition. The knowledge must identify the business purpose, owners, inputs, outputs, evidence, decision thresholds, failure conditions and downstream effects. It must preserve the department charter — Create strategic relationships, enterprise accounts, government connections and market-expansion opportunities that strengthen marketplace liquidity. — and contribute to the north-star outcome: Partnerships deliver measurable suppliers, customers, revenue or strategic access. Knowledge records should distinguish stable policy from live operational facts and should point to live tables rather than copying values that change.

### Partnership Strategy — how the department uses it
When work involves partnership strategy, the department should first establish the entity and workflow state, then retrieve current operational data, then apply the approved SOP and decision rules. The output must be structured for the next department rather than left as an informal narrative. The agent should identify what is known, what is inferred, what is missing and what evidence supports the conclusion. Repeated work should update reusable templates or rules only through the knowledge-change process.

### Partnership Strategy — controls and boundaries
For partnership strategy, the department must apply least privilege, data minimisation, audit logging, version control and the relevant authority tier. It must not treat a generated answer as proof. Where the topic affects legal rights, money, identity, privacy, security, production deployment or public claims, execution is blocked until the required approval and evidence are present. Conflicts between live data and static knowledge are resolved in favour of the authorised live source, with the discrepancy logged for review.
