# Legal & Compliance — Operating Knowledge Pack

**Department key:** `legal_compliance`  
**Production mapping:** legal  
**Implementation assessment:** `implemented`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  

## Charter
Maintain defensible legal documents, monitor regulatory obligations, protect privacy and intellectual property, and escalate legal judgment to qualified counsel.

## North-star outcome
Current, aligned legal documents and no untracked material legal or regulatory risk.

## Authority and operating boundary
The department may act only within the live authority matrix. Legal judgment, material money movement, identity/authentication, secrets, destructive data operations, production deployment, public claims and changes to the autonomy framework remain hard-gated.

## Knowledge records
### Uae Commercial Law — operating knowledge
Legal & Compliance must understand UAE commercial law as an operational capability, not as a generic definition. The knowledge must identify the business purpose, owners, inputs, outputs, evidence, decision thresholds, failure conditions and downstream effects. It must preserve the department charter — Maintain defensible legal documents, monitor regulatory obligations, protect privacy and intellectual property, and escalate legal judgment to qualified counsel. — and contribute to the north-star outcome: Current, aligned legal documents and no untracked material legal or regulatory risk. Knowledge records should distinguish stable policy from live operational facts and should point to live tables rather than copying values that change.

### Uae Commercial Law — how the department uses it
When work involves UAE commercial law, the department should first establish the entity and workflow state, then retrieve current operational data, then apply the approved SOP and decision rules. The output must be structured for the next department rather than left as an informal narrative. The agent should identify what is known, what is inferred, what is missing and what evidence supports the conclusion. Repeated work should update reusable templates or rules only through the knowledge-change process.

### Uae Commercial Law — controls and boundaries
For UAE commercial law, the department must apply least privilege, data minimisation, audit logging, version control and the relevant authority tier. It must not treat a generated answer as proof. Where the topic affects legal rights, money, identity, privacy, security, production deployment or public claims, execution is blocked until the required approval and evidence are present. Conflicts between live data and static knowledge are resolved in favour of the authorised live source, with the discrepancy logged for review.
