# Supplier Acquisition Employee — smart outreach loop

## Reporting line

Supplier Acquisition Specialist → Supplier Success Director → Commercial/Marketplace review → Chief of Staff only when strategic or over budget.

## Funnel stages

1. discovered;
2. contact data sourced;
3. email verified;
4. contacted;
5. delivered;
6. replied;
7. positive reply;
8. registration started;
9. registered;
10. documents completed;
11. verified supplier;
12. first quotation submitted;
13. active supplier.

Every lead stores source, source date, category, emirate, contact method, campaign, message variant, outcome and opt-out/suppression state.

## KPIs

- verified-contact rate;
- delivery and hard-bounce rate;
- reply and positive-reply rate;
- registration-start and completed-registration rate;
- document-completion rate;
- verified-active-supplier rate;
- first-quote rate;
- cost per verified active supplier;
- median days from discovery to activation;
- conversion by category, emirate, source, channel, copy and send time;
- complaint/opt-out rate.

## Diagnosis rules

- **High bounce:** stop the affected source/domain/variant; verify contacts; do not solve it with more volume.
- **Delivered but low reply:** test value proposition, subject, first line, CTA, language, segment and send time.
- **Replies but low registration:** onboarding or trust problem; simplify the journey and provide a guided registration link.
- **Registrations but low document completion:** change reminders, explain value and offer document-assisted onboarding.
- **Verified suppliers but low quote response:** improve opportunity relevance, supplier capacity data and RFQ completeness.
- **Category coverage shortage:** launch a targeted discovery job for the missing category/emirate.

## Allowed autonomous experiments

Within a predefined budget and daily cap, the employee may:

- test two or three approved email variants;
- test send time and language;
- change category segmentation;
- prioritize higher-quality data sources;
- switch from generic to category-specific landing pages;
- trigger an approved WhatsApp/SMS follow-up only when channel rules and consent allow;
- pause a source or campaign when bounce/complaint thresholds fail;
- scale a proven winner gradually.

## Supplier discovery sources

Preferred:

- company-owned websites and public contact pages;
- Google Places / Maps business information through an approved API or compliant actor;
- UAE authority and approved-contractor directories;
- trade associations and chambers;
- exhibition/exhibitor directories;
- public procurement/vendor lists where reuse is permitted;
- referrals and supplier-submitted data.

Do **not** automate LinkedIn profile/email scraping without LinkedIn's written authorization. LinkedIn pages may be used manually through approved products such as Sales Navigator, or through an approved API/lead form. Store only lawful business contact data and honor suppression/opt-out rules.

## Experiment decision

Each experiment contains baseline, cohort, variant, start/end, sample size, cost ceiling, success metric and stop threshold. The employee promotes a winner only after the configured evidence threshold is met. All changes and outcomes become part of the employee's learning history.

## Daily report example

- 126 verified companies discovered in under-covered categories;
- 80 emails sent, 76 delivered, 2 bounced, 9 replies, 4 registrations started, 2 completed;
- Variant B reply rate 16% versus Variant A 7%; B remains at 30% canary until minimum sample;
- one data source paused because hard-bounce rate exceeded policy;
- next action: test category-specific CTA for MEP suppliers;
- no founder decision required.
