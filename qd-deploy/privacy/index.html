Privacy Policy

How QuotesDone collects and uses information.

1. Who we are

QuotesDone ("we") operates the QuotesDone marketplace. For privacy questions contact management@quotesdone.com.

2. What we collect

  • Customer account data: your name, email and mobile number, each verified by one-time code (OTP).
  • Request data: the job details, voice transcripts, photos and dimensions you submit.
  • Payment data: when you pay a Reveal Fee, payment is processed by a third-party payment processor; we receive a transaction reference and status, not your full card details.
  • Supplier data: business and company information, trade licence and registration documents, and contact details that suppliers provide or that are collected from public sources.
  • Usage data: basic technical logs needed to run and secure the service.

3. How we use it

  • To verify your identity (email and mobile OTP) and operate your account.
  • To structure your request and match it to suitable suppliers.
  • To send your request to suppliers without revealing your identity until you reveal and pay.
  • To process Reveal Fees and any refunds, and to operate, secure and improve the service.

4. AI processing

We use automated/AI tools to convert your voice, photos and text into a structured request, to match suppliers, and to summarise supplier profiles. Submitted content is processed for these purposes only.

5. Sharing

We share the content of your request (not your identity) with matched suppliers. When you reveal a supplier and pay the Reveal Fee, you consent to us sharing your contact details with that supplier so you can deal directly. We share payment information with our payment processor as needed to take payment. We do not sell your personal data.

6. Retention

We keep account, request and transaction data for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal, accounting and tax obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised. You may request deletion of your account data by emailing us, subject to records we must keep by law.

7. Your rights

Subject to UAE law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes & governing law

We may update this policy; the current version is shown above. This policy is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates.
