Supplier Agreement & Registration Terms

Applies to suppliers who register, claim a profile, or submit quotes on QuotesDone.

1. Your relationship with QuotesDone

You are an independent business. QuotesDone introduces you to potential customers but is not your employer, agent or partner, and is not a party to any contract you form with a customer. You are solely responsible for the quality, safety, pricing, legality and completion of your work.

2. Registration and fees

QuotesDone does not charge you any per-job or commission fee. Suppliers are charged a registration fee only:

  • The first 1,000 suppliers to register receive free registration.
  • After that, registration costs AED 100, renewable yearly, to keep your profile active and receiving leads.

When a customer reveals and accepts your quote, the customer pays QuotesDone a separate Reveal Fee; this does not reduce the amount you receive for the work. You receive the full amount you quoted, paid to you directly by the customer on terms you agree.

3. Accurate information & verification

  • You confirm that the company details, trade licence, Economic Register / establishment certificate, bank details and other information you provide are accurate and current, and that you are authorised to act for the business.
  • You will maintain a valid UAE trade licence and any approvals required for your trade.
  • QuotesDone may verify your details and may suspend, unverify or remove accounts that are unverified, expired, inaccurate or misrepresented.

4. Quoting and conduct

When you submit a quote, that amount is the price you wish to receive for the work and you agree to honour it. You will respond to revealed customers professionally and promptly, honour quoted prices and agreed timelines, and comply with applicable UAE law and consumer protection rules.

5. Responsiveness and customer refunds

If you do not respond to a customer within 3 working days of being revealed, or you decline the job, the customer may be entitled to a refund of their Reveal Fee from QuotesDone. Repeated non-responsiveness may result in suspension or removal from the platform.

6. No circumvention

For customers introduced through QuotesDone, you agree to engage with them through the introduction and not to encourage customers to avoid the platform. Repeated abuse may result in removal without refund of any registration fee.

7. Liability

QuotesDone provides the platform "as is" and does not guarantee any volume of leads, quotes or jobs. To the maximum extent permitted by law, QuotesDone is not liable for disputes between you and a customer, for any loss of business, or for any indirect or consequential loss.

8. Data

Information you submit is handled per our Privacy Policy. Your company information may be shown to customers as part of a quote, and after a reveal your contact details are shared with that customer.

9. Termination, changes & governing law

Either party may stop using the platform at any time; registration fees already paid are non-refundable. We may update these terms and fees; the current version is shown above. This agreement is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates, with disputes subject to the competent courts of Dubai.
