Terms of Service

For customers using QuotesDone to request quotes and reveal suppliers.

1. What QuotesDone is

QuotesDone is an online marketplace that lets you describe a building, fit-out or facilities job and receive price quotes from independent third-party suppliers in the UAE. QuotesDone is an intermediary and technology platform only. We are not the supplier, contractor or manufacturer; we do not perform, supervise, guarantee or take part in the work, and we are not a party to any contract you enter into with a supplier.

2. How it works

  • You register with your name, email and mobile number, which are verified by one-time code.
  • You describe your job by text, voice or photo; our system structures it into a request.
  • Your request is sent — without your contact details — to matched suppliers, who submit their prices.
  • You compare quotes anonymously. When you choose to connect with a supplier, you pay a Reveal Fee and the supplier's contact details are released to you.

3. The Reveal Fee (what you pay us)

QuotesDone's charge to you is the Reveal Fee, payable at the moment you reveal a supplier. During the launch promotion the Reveal Fee is 2% of the supplier's quoted amount, capped at AED 250 per reveal. The applicable fee is always shown to you before you confirm payment. The Reveal Fee is the only amount QuotesDone charges you; it is separate from, and does not include, the price of the actual work, which you pay directly to the supplier on terms you agree with them. QuotesDone does not collect, hold, escrow or guarantee payment for the work itself.

4. Refund policy

The Reveal Fee is refundable in the following case: if, within 3 (three) working days of revealing a supplier, that supplier has not responded to you or has declined to undertake the job, you may request a refund of that Reveal Fee by emailing management@quotesdone.com before the 3-working-day deadline. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method. Working days exclude Saturdays, Sundays and UAE public holidays. The Reveal Fee is not refundable once the supplier has responded or engaged with you, where you simply change your mind, or after the 3-working-day window has passed.

5. Consent to share your details

By revealing a supplier and paying the Reveal Fee, you expressly consent to QuotesDone sharing your contact details (name, phone and email) with that supplier so the two of you can deal directly. You acknowledge that, after a reveal, QuotesDone has no control over how the supplier contacts or communicates with you.

We do not guarantee that you will receive any quote, that quotes are accurate or final, or that a supplier is licensed, available, suitable, or will perform or complete any work. Supplier ratings and information are provided for convenience only and are not endorsements. You are solely responsible for verifying a supplier's identity, licensing, insurance, suitability and pricing before engaging them and before making any payment to them.
