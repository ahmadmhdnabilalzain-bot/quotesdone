# Financial, Privacy and Legal Controls

Only a specific refund approve/reject requires routine founder approval.

Payments: checkout ≠ confirmation ≠ reconciliation. Reveal waits for the frozen reconciled state. Executor and reconciler are distinct. Unknown monetary states are quarantined.

Refund: exact case/evidence, immutable founder decision, Payments execution, Financial Controller reconciliation. No live refund in Phase D.

Terms: exact displayed version/hash, append-only acceptance, no certificate before acceptance, no trigger rewriting versions.

Privacy: suppliers never read raw customer identity before approved reveal. Supplier-safe brief/files require independent approval. Public privacy requests use an explicit safe contract and never impersonate the founder.
