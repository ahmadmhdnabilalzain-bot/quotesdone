# Implementation Waves

- **D0:** effective baseline, full 257-step reconciliation and frozen handler registry.
- **D1:** handler executor, business-object services, auth/RLS/storage, idempotency, review and mock effect foundations.
- **D2:** launch-critical W04–W17 customer/inquiry/supplier/quote/comparison/payment/refund contracts.
- **D3:** W03, W18–W21 growth, customer/supplier lifecycle and partnerships.
- **D4:** W22–W24 and W29 product, AI-change contracts, release and security/reliability.
- **D5:** W01–W02 and W25–W30 executive, data, knowledge, finance, legal and workforce.
- **D6:** 30/30 deterministic workflow closure, failure/cancel/replay, evidence and freeze.

Do not start a later wave with an unresolved critical defect in the previous wave.
